# Pixy Liao — source shortlist

This source set follows the 12 works in Silver Eye Center for Photography’s 2022 *Experimental Relationship* exhibition. The exhibition provides a public, full-frame 1500px display JPEG for each work, paired with an artist interview that identifies the project as staged performance with her partner Moro rather than documentary. Its selection gives the chapter a useful chronological range (2008–2020) and a concentrated study of Liao’s controlled gesture, domestic staging, pastel color, and reversals of customary heterosexual roles. The Art Institute of Chicago identifies comparable *Experimental Relationship* works as “Courtesy of the artist. © Pixy Liao”; no open-licensed reproduction was located.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. These are copyrighted contemporary photographs by Pixy Liao (© Pixy Liao), publicly displayed by Silver Eye Center for Photography. Each assessment is limited and transformative: this nonprofit book uses a credited, web-sized copy for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The complete published frame is necessary to discuss the bodies, props, color fields, and staged spatial relationships; a crop would remove the formal argument. Each entry links to the exhibition record and uses only its public display rendition, which does not replace the artist’s print, the exhibition, a gallery record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Silver Eye describes *Experimental Relationship* as a long-term project in which Liao uses gesture, color, and space to create stages for exaggerated intimate moments, and its interview records her account of the photographs as performances rather than documents: https://silvereye.org/exhibitions/experimental-relationship. The Art Institute of Chicago’s exhibition page dates the project from shortly after Liao and Moro met, describes its art-historical and popular-cultural references, and credits displayed works “Courtesy of the artist. © Pixy Liao”: https://www.artic.edu/exhibitions/10548. Liao’s own project page confirms the series’ 2007–present span and its concern with alternative possibilities for heterosexual relationships: https://www.pixyliao.com/experimental-relationship.
