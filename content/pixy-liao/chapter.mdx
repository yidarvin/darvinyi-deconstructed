Pixy Liao's *Experimental Relationship* turns a shared life into a sequence of precise propositions. The photographs are staged performances with her partner Moro, not incidental domestic snapshots: a window becomes a proscenium, a mattress a stack of horizontal measures, and a cable a literal tether running out of the action. The pair may be aligned, crossed, or held apart, but the composition makes every relation visible before it is explained. [Source notes](../../content/pixy-liao/sources.md)

## bodies measured by rooms

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="01-beach-sister-ii" label="Beach Sister II, 2014" caption="A shared vertical axis and a single enclosing body mass turn back-to-back profiles into one deliberately assembled figure." />

In *Beach Sister II*, the central axis runs through the seam where the two patterned garments meet, while the ellipse keeps the claim modest: the pair read as one tall, doubled mass, not as a mirror image. Their profiles point in opposite directions. The normalized image's pale palette, beginning #dccfcb and #eaeaf5, lets the red, green, and orange bands of the garments carry the visual weight against beach and water.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="02-clapping-feet" label="Clapping Feet, 2020" caption="Three window bays and a long leg rhythm hold the pair apart until their feet meet at the center." />

The broad blank wall in *Clapping Feet* is active negative space, not unused room. The overlay names the two sitters and traces the route their legs take to the touching feet; the window bays make this improbable contact look measured rather than spontaneous. A bright palette (#d9d4d3, #bfb7ae, #eae7e9) and low measured edge density (0.011) preserve the room's airy stillness around that one junction.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="03-how-to-build-a-relationship-with-layered-meanings" label="How to Build a Relationship with Layered Meanings, 2008" caption="Two bodies, mattress strata, and a trailing cord turn a cramped room into a literal diagram of layers." />

Here the photograph has no need for a false horizon. The mattress edges are real horizontal bands, the ellipse contains the fused body silhouette, and the cord is a physical line escaping toward the lower frame. The restrained pale palette (#edebf0, #d4cdcd, #bfb1a7) and low edge density (0.007) make the stacked fabrics and the cable unusually legible.

## landscape as a stage

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="04-moro-by-the-pond" label="Moro by the Pond, 2010" caption="A pond edge, overhead branch, and enclosing rock make a seated figure a small, deliberate event." />

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="06-hug-by-the-pond" label="Hug by the Pond, 2010" caption="The compact embrace remains isolated between a heavy branch canopy and a lateral pond edge." />

The two pond works use the same setting differently. In *Moro by the Pond*, the rock gives the seated body a definite base; in *Hug by the Pond*, that base supports a vertical knot of two bodies. The branch line and pond edge are the stable large-scale structures, while reflection and foliage keep the space textured. Their normalized tonal spreads are broad (p05/p95 0.157/0.816 and 0.137/0.769), but the palette remains within layered greens, so the small skin-colored figures acquire emphasis without needing an artificial spotlight.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="12-the-hug" label="The Hug, 2010" caption="A tree corridor and a narrow path lead to a brightly patterned embrace held in a dark forest field." />

*The Hug* makes that outdoor compression more severe. The trees are an actual loose frame, and the path is a visible approach rather than a speculative vanishing system. The analysis finds the darkest mean luminance in the selection (0.105); against that dark green field, the floral dress carries the picture's sharpest color interruption.

## furniture, screens, and the terms of looking

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="05-beauty-is-in-the-eye-of-the-beholder" label="Beauty is in the Eye of the Beholder, 2015" caption="A screen grid, its central stile, and a folded garment make the turned figure read as a controlled interruption." />

The shoji screen is an exact frame-in-frame, not generic decorative symmetry. Its base supplies a horizontal threshold, and the centered stile pushes against the asymmetry of the figure's turned head and wrapped garment. The image's wide normalized tonal range (p05 0.067; p95 0.984) explains why the luminous screen can surround a warm body without making it flat.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="07-start-off-your-day-with-a-good-breakfast-together" label="Start Off Your Day with a Good Breakfast Together, 2009" caption="A tabletop, a reclining body, and a spoon-bearing sitter make breakfast service a staged distribution of attention." />

The table's long front edge is the photograph's governing stage line. Bread, cups, fruit, and the body are spaced across it, while the seated figure's spoon stays high against the kitchen grid. The web rendition carries a broad tonal range (p05 0.267; p95 0.992), but the low-detail pale room keeps the tabletop sequence readable.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="08-moro-on-a-couch" label="Moro on a Couch, 2017" caption="A red armchair encloses Moro while clock and partial mirror keep the large blank wall from becoming empty." />

The red armchair is a warm, localized field rather than a perspectival contour. Above and beside Moro, the wall clock and gilded mirror punctuate the wide expanse of wall without enclosing it. The muted gray, rose, and brown palette (#8c8681, #c7c6cb, #755c50, #381e23) lets the chair hold the figure without turning the room into a theatrical backdrop.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="09-devour" label="Devour, 2015" caption="Facing silhouettes and the central shoji division make a backlit screen into a stage for reciprocal, exaggerated gesture." />

In *Devour*, the axial line names the narrow gap between the faces rather than claiming that the bodies are symmetrical. The window rectangle contains the silhouettes, and the ellipse holds their face-to-face action. Its tonal spread is the most extreme in this group (p05 0.043; p95 0.961), which is why the profiles can read as graphic cutouts against the bright screen.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="10-twin-bed" label="Twin Bed, 2015" caption="A central head-to-head axis and the bed's broad rectangle hold opposing bodies in one shared horizontal field." />

The exact point of contact is between the two heads, while the legs and arms move outward in different directions. The centered window join above is only a subtle vertical aid; the bed is the real internal frame. The image's cool gray palette (#b4b3b7, #e3e4e5, #8f8a8a) keeps the body arrangement from becoming a high-contrast theatrical tableau.

<Plate slug="pixy-liao" id="11-welcome-to-the-gates-of-a-new-era" label="Welcome to the Gates of a New Era, 2014" caption="An exact window threshold and crossed limbs make the inverted pair an architectural knot in front of the city." />

The crossed limbs create an X, but the window frame prevents that graphic gesture from floating free of a real room. The sill is a low, measured threshold; the distant water tower holds near the central opening above the pair. High-key grays and whites (#f6f6f1, #cbcacc, #a199a0) put the inverted bodies into crisp relief without treating the city as a generic background.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Let furniture set the rule">
Make a photograph in which a table, bed, sill, or chair gives the body a measurable limit. Keep the frame only if the object changes how distance, contact, or balance reads.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Use one exact point of contact">
Stage two figures with one visible junction: feet, heads, hands, or a shared object. Leave enough surrounding space for that connection to carry the composition.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Build an outdoor room">
Find a branch canopy, pond edge, trunks, or a path that can act as an enclosure. Place the figure so the landscape organizes the pose without becoming a decorative backdrop.
</ExerciseCard>
