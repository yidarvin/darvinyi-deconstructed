# Pixy Liao — research notes

## What the supplied sources establish

The twelve selected photographs are from Pixy Liao's ongoing *Experimental Relationship* project. Liao's project page dates the series 2007–present; the Silver Eye Center for Photography exhibition records the selected works from 2008–2020 and describes the photographs with her partner Moro as staged performances rather than documentary scenes. The Art Institute of Chicago and Silver Eye records support describing the project as an inquiry into alternative possibilities for heterosexual relationships, carried through gesture, color, space, and deliberately exaggerated intimacy. Titles, dates, copyright credit, source links, and the per-image fair-use rationale are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md).

## What the supplied files show

The normalized exhibition JPEGs consistently use domestic architecture and furnishing as measured structure: windows, shoji screens, a tabletop, mattress bands, a bed, and a couch set the limits within which bodies can lean, cross, touch, or withhold contact. Outdoor works invert that compression: pond edge, branch canopy, dark trees, and an open path make a paired body small but emphatic. In the normalized files, pale interiors and beach sky carry high-key color fields, while *Devour* and *The Hug* make silhouette and saturated floral cloth read against dark ground. These are observations about the provided web reproductions, not claims about original-print color.

## Technique and limits of record

The source record supports staged performance, but does not identify a camera body, lens, film stock, exposure, lighting plan, print process, or post-production method for these photographs. The ingested files retain no EXIF. Accordingly, this chapter does not infer technical settings or a general workflow from web JPEGs. Its palette, tonal spread, and edge-density observations come from deterministic analysis of the normalized exhibition renditions and are used only to describe the visible supplied image.
