# Carrie Mae Weems — source shortlist

This set follows the narrative sequence of Weems’s *Kitchen Table Series* (1990), using the artist’s own public portfolio page rather than a third-party repost. The series holds its setting, square format, and suspended lamp remarkably constant while recasting the artist’s protagonist through intimacy, friendship, mothering, solitude, and self-possession. All twelve files were retrieved as 1500 × 1500px public display derivatives from the official page and verified at that size; the local source files are normalized to opening JPEGs. The official page does not grant an open licence; the works remain copyrighted.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. Each assessment below is individual: this book uses a complete-frame, web-sized copy for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching; the complete frame is necessary to examine the table, lamp, bodies, props, and their changes across the sequence; each copy credits and links to the artist’s official presentation; and a 1500px public-display derivative does not replace the original print, the artist’s page, or a licensed reproduction. Carrie Mae Weems is the artist and known rightsholder; the artist’s portfolio supplies the public display files.

## Research context

MoMA identifies *Kitchen Table Series* as twenty gelatin silver prints and fourteen text panels. It describes the recurring domestic interior—anchored by the wooden table and overhead light—and identifies *Untitled (Man Smoking)* and *Untitled (Woman and Daughter with Makeup)* as key scenes: https://www.moma.org/artists/7177-carrie-mae-weems. The Cleveland Museum of Art’s records for *Man Smoking*, *Eating Lobster*, *Man Reading Newspaper*, and *Woman Feeding Bird* corroborate the 1990 date, series title, and the artist credit: https://www.clevelandart.org/art/2008.116.2 and https://www.clevelandart.org/art/2008.116.17. The source selection preserves a teachable progression from couple scenes to friendship, mothering, and solitude without mixing undocumented third-party reproductions into the asset set.
