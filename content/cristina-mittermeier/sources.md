# Cristina Mittermeier — source shortlist

This is a twelve-image, artist-authored selection from Mittermeier’s public journal. The pages name each work, location, and year, and expose complete public display JPEGs without sign-in or other access control. Each downloaded file is the unaltered full displayed photograph, opens as a JPEG, and is 2,000px or more on its long edge. The images move between people, coastal and underwater life, and ice: a useful cross-section of Mittermeier’s conservation photography rather than a generic wildlife set.

**Rights basis:** all selections are **fair-use**, not open content. The artist’s website identifies the work as Cristina Mittermeier Photography and does not offer an open licence; credit/rightsholder is **Cristina Mittermeier / Cristina Mittermeier Photography**. Each assessment below is independent: this book uses a credited, linked, web-sized copy for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching; each photograph is a creative work; the complete frame is needed to teach its spatial arrangement rather than an extracted detail; and the limited display copy does not replace the artist’s print, the source page, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

- The [National Geographic profile](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/impact/article/cristina-mittermeier) identifies Mittermeier as a marine-biologist-turned conservation photographer specializing in ocean and Indigenous-culture stories, and notes her National Geographic assignments and SeaLegacy co-founding role.
- [SeaLegacy’s founder profile](https://sealegacy.org/our-founders/) describes her photography as linking biodiversity, climate mitigation, and coastal-community/Indigenous stewardship. These sources support reading the varied locations above as a conservation practice built through relationship and context, not detached spectacle.
