# Candida Höfer — research note

## What the source record establishes

The ten supplied photographs date from 2000 to 2003. They move between the
Schindler House, museum galleries, archives, libraries, and reading rooms, and
the records identify the individual title, location, and date for each work.
The Hessisches Landesmuseum Darmstadt describes Höfer's better-known large-format
photographs as empty public interiors—museums, opera houses, theaters, churches,
zoos, and libraries—and characterizes them as portraits of spaces. That context
fits this selected group, but the chapter's more specific claims concern only the
visible structures in the supplied full-frame display renditions.

## Printing and technical limits

The supplied ALBERTINA and Museo Reina Sofía records do not establish a
work-specific camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO,
film or digital format, lighting setup, print process, print size, or edition
for these ten photographs. The ingested JPEGs contain no capture EXIF. The
chapter therefore does not infer technique from their carefully ordered
appearance: it describes the displayed image and identifies only the dates,
titles, locations, and sources documented in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Rights note

Each selection is a publicly accessible, web-sized display rendition used under
the image-specific fair-use rationale in [sources.md](sources.md). The credited,
record-linked copies are necessary to teach the photographs' spatial structures
and do not substitute for a print, a licensed reproduction, or the source
collection record.
