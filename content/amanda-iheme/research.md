# Amanda Iheme — research note

## Scope and sources

The source dossier documents twelve photographs from Amanda Iheme’s *The Way of
Life* project: *Death — Casa de Fernandez* (dated 2015/2019 on the artist’s
page), *Stagnation — Old Secretariat* (2018), *Preservation — Waterhouse*
(2018), and *Restoration — Jaekel House* (2017). The artist’s public portfolio
identifies the buildings and project cycles. MoMA’s *New Photography 2023*
checklist independently lists works from the Old Secretariat, Waterhouse, and
Jaekel House cycles. See [sources.md](sources.md) for the work-by-work links,
credit, and fair-use assessment.

## Process and technique

The photographed material combines frontal and oblique architectural views,
street-scale scenes, and a single interior threshold. Repeated arches, roof
lines, tiled walls, balconies, roads, market umbrellas, and pedestrians are
visible formal material; the chapter treats them as such rather than asserting
an undocumented camera position or production workflow.

Neither the artist portfolio nor the MoMA material consulted for this chapter
documents a camera body, lens, focal length, film or sensor, aperture, shutter
speed, ISO, lighting setup, negative, post-production method, print process,
paper, dimensions, or edition for these individual works. The ingested web
JPEGs retain no capture EXIF. No camera-setting or printing-process claim is
made here.

## Reading the analyzed files

The analyzer records distinct but related tonal structures: the broad facade
and market scene in *Casa de Fernandez—Death—01* spans p05 0.035 to p95 0.965
with edge density 0.128, whereas the long Old Secretariat facade in
*Stagnation—03* has edge density 0.025. Across the supplied files, pale sky,
near-black recesses, brown-gray building material, muted blue, and occasional
yellow or pink accents recur in the measured palettes. Those readings describe
the display files’ visible structure, not the original exposure or print.
