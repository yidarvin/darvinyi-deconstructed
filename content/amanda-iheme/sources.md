# Sources — Amanda Iheme

## Acquisition and rights note

The twelve unaltered image files listed below were retrieved on 2026-08-02 from the publicly accessible, artist-controlled Amanda Iheme portfolio. Each opens as a JPEG and has a long edge of 1,800–2,500 px. The portfolio identifies the pictured building and its project cycle; its *The Way of Life* overview groups the work as **Death — Casa de Fernandez**, **Stagnation — Old Secretariat**, **Preservation — Waterhouse**, and **Restoration — Jaekel House**. MoMA's *New Photography 2023* checklist independently identifies the latter three cycles as *The Way of Life* works and credits them “Courtesy the artist”; MoMA's work page for *Casa de Fernandez—Death—14* gives the explicit notice “Courtesy of the artist. © Amanda Iheme.” No open licence is supplied by either source.

All selections are therefore used as **fair-use**, not open material. For each entry, the assessment is: (1) this nonprofit chapter transforms the photograph into criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; (2) the photograph is a highly creative work, weighing against use; (3) the entire uncropped frame is necessary to analyze its spatial relationships, but the project uses only a web-sized copy; and (4) the work is credited and linked to the artist's public page and does not replace the artist's original, the museum record, or a licensed reproduction. The artist's page and public CDN response were accessed without authentication, paywall, technical bypass, or alteration.
