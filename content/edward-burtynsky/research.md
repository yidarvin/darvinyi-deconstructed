# Edward Burtynsky — research note

## Scope and chronology

The source record identifies Edward Burtynsky (b. 1955) and documents this twelve-image selection through the artist's public Photographs Index, with an additional Library of Congress record for *Nickel Tailings #34*, the artist's *Shipbreaking* project page, and the artist's *Water* book/project page. The documented dates run from *Rock of Ages #1* (1991) through *Salt Pans #32* (2018). The index groups the works within bodies including *Quarries*, *Oil*, *China*, *Water*, *Anthropocene*, and *African Studies*. Titles, places, dates, image routes, credits, and individual fair-use rationales are preserved in [sources.md](sources.md).

The sequence makes a defensible visual chronology: early quarry, tailings, waste, tire, oil, and shipbreaking photographs describe extraction and industrial residue from elevated or measured viewpoints; the China works expand that view into urban rebuilding and factory labor; *Water*, *Anthropocene*, and *African Studies* turn farming, marble, and salt production into broad aerial or elevated systems. This is a reading of the supplied selection, not a claim that the twelve works exhaust the artist's practice.

## Technique, material, and settings

The consulted source pages establish the displayed photographs, their titles, locations, years, projects, and copyright credit © Edward Burtynsky. They do not establish a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, negative or sensor format, print process, paper, edition, or individual exposure settings for these twelve photographs. The ingested web JPEGs provide no reliable capture EXIF. Accordingly, the chapter does not infer a camera setup or printing technique from the look of a web image.

It instead treats measured geometry, luminance, palette, and edge density as features of the supplied web renditions. The images are used under the per-image fair-use reasoning in [sources.md](sources.md): credited, public, web-sized complete frames are necessary to teach the compositional systems at issue and do not substitute for a print, licensed reproduction, or the artist's record.
