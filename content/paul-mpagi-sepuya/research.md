# Paul Mpagi Sepuya — research notes

## Scope of the record

The supplied source record documents eleven works shown by Galerie Peter Kilchmann, dated 2018–2024, and identifies Paul Mpagi Sepuya as the artist on each linked work page. Those pages document the finished works’ print media: for example, *Daylight Studio (0X5A4577)* is an archival pigment print, while *Daylight Studio Mirror (_DSF2371)* is an archival pigment print on dibond on a wheeled wooden frame. The pages do not display an artwork-specific copyright notice, so this record does not infer a rightsholder. They also do not document a common camera body, lens, exposure, film stock, capture process, lighting diagram, or post-production method. The normalized files contain no retained EXIF metadata. None of those capture or production particulars is therefore attributed here.

## Working observations

Across the selected works, the studio is not concealed as a place of production. A tripod or camera can become a central subject; mirrors introduce a second frame and a second viewpoint; portable screens, curtains, stools, cushions, and photographs turn the room into a set of movable parts. The 2018–2024 sequence also moves between red darkroom light, bright white studio light, and twilight without turning those conditions into claims about the artist’s general lighting practice.

The chapter’s analysis treats these visible structures as compositional evidence, not biographical evidence. Color, tonal range, and edge density cited in the chapter are deterministic measurements of the delivered web images; they describe those files rather than the artist’s original prints or intended color management.
