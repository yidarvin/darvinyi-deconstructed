# Paul Mpagi Sepuya — source notes

All eleven files below were downloaded from the publicly accessible work pages and display-image service of Galerie Peter Kilchmann. The gallery's linked work pages identify Paul Mpagi Sepuya as the artist and provide the work titles, dates, and finished-print details; they do not display an artwork-specific copyright notice, so no rightsholder is asserted here. The public display files were delivered as WebP by the service and converted without cropping or image alteration to JPEG for the required `raw/` filenames. Each file opens successfully; the long edge is 960–2368 px (so all exceed the 640 px floor). The gallery's [artist index](https://www.peterkilchmann.com/artists/paul-mpagi-sepuya/) provides the cross-checkable selected-works context.
