# Gideon Mendel — research note

## Documented record

The artist's public *Submerged Portraits* gallery identifies this selection as
part of Gideon Mendel's *Drowning World*, begun after flooding in the UK and
India in 2007. Its captions supply the sitters' names, locations, and dates:
the retained photographs range from the 2007 India and UK visits to work made
in Nigeria and Pakistan in 2022. The series makes a repeated, square-framed
portrait across several flood events and locations; that repetition is visible
in the set, but it does not make the sitters or the events interchangeable.
See the individual artist-gallery records and the per-image fair-use rationale
in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Technique and limits

The retained source record establishes the artist, series, captions, dates, and
public display route. It does not establish the original print process, camera
body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting arrangement,
capture format, or post-production workflow for these twelve photographs. The
available 660 × 660px display JPEGs carry no EXIF metadata, as recorded in
[manifest.json](manifest.json). This chapter therefore does not infer a camera
or printing method from the photographs' direct address, flash-like illumination,
or square format.

## Measured evidence

The normalized display files share a wide tonal range but not one fixed surface
quality. Plate 04 is the darkest on average (mean luminance 0.241), making the
orange shirt and pale arch unusually emphatic against black water; plate 09 is
similarly low-key (mean 0.206, p95 0.478). Plate 08 has the highest measured
edge density (0.162), where signage, balconies, cables, ripples, and the held
child keep the city view active. Plate 05 is much smoother (edge density 0.022),
so its single shirted figure reads as a calm vertical and reflection.

The analyzer selected phi or thirds scaffolds as useful geometric references,
not claims about Mendel's intention. Final overlays retain only visually
grounded structures: doors, arches, figure groupings, actual hand and shoulder
relations, wall bands, railings, and street recession. Automatic horizon and
vanishing-point proposals were excluded whenever they did not name a visible
photographic structure.
