# Gideon Mendel — source shortlist

Gideon Mendel’s *Drowning World* turns the flood-survivor portrait into a global typology. The square, eye-level frames give each sitter an unusually direct exchange with the camera, while a waterline, interior architecture, and floating debris divide the frame into a persistent set of horizontal and vertical relationships. This 12-image selection follows the artist’s public *Submerged Portraits* gallery across India, Pakistan, Thailand, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK, and the United States, so that repetition can be studied without reducing its subjects to a single location or event.

Each is a 660px public display JPEG retrieved directly from the artist’s publicly accessible gallery, without login or access-control bypass.
