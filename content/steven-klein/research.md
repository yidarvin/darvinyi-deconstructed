# Steven Klein — research notes

## Scope and sources

This chapter considers ten public Vogue display renditions, documented individually
in [sources.md](sources.md). The captioned set spans 2004–14: *Frozen Flesh #2*,
two Brad Pitt pictures, a 2005 pool scene, *Suburbia* pictures from 2007 and 2008,
Rihanna's 2009 *Vogue Italia* portrait, *Toxic Bloom* (2012), *Out of This World*
(2013), and *Unseen & Unheard* (2014). Vogue credits Steven Klein for each image.
The record establishes titles or supplied titles, dates, sitters where named, and
the public web source; it does not establish a complete production account.

## Method and visual language

The pictures repeatedly make their settings read as scenes rather than neutral
locations. Bodies are placed against a pool, road, butchery, tiled apparatus,
film-set ground, greenhouse, tablet screen, or domestic interior. The visual
evidence supports discussion of framing, visible mise-en-scène, palette, repeated
props, directional lines, and the relation between figure and setting. It does not
support asserting how much was built, altered, or directed beyond what is visible.

## Technique, printing, and settings

Neither the Vogue feature nor the ten supplied display JPEGs identifies a print
process, edition, paper, camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed,
ISO, capture format, lighting plan, retouching sequence, or post-production
workflow. The ingested manifest records empty EXIF objects for every file. This
chapter therefore treats the images as web renditions and confines its technical
discussion to visible tonal range, palette, spatial arrangement, and staging.

## Evidence limits

Dates, work titles, sitter names where given, image credit, source routes, and the
per-image fair-use rationale are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md). Claims about
broader technique, printing, camera settings, or studio process are not generalized
from this small editorial selection.
