# Cho Gi-Seok — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This set follows the four series identified in Fotografiska Shanghai's 2025–26
*The Coexistence of Imperfection* exhibition: *Flower Study*, *Bad Dream*,
*Love & Hate*, and *These Days*. The exhibition captions identify every selected
work as © Cho Gi-Seok. The museum has made the complete display renditions
publicly reachable on its exhibition page and CDN; the six unaltered JPEGs below
were downloaded from those public responses at a 1,600px long edge, opened
successfully, and remain web-sized reference copies.

All six uses are **fair-use** assessments. They are not offered as open-licensed
works: Cho Gi-Seok is the creator and the displayed notice is © Cho Gi-Seok.
For each work, the purpose is criticism, scholarship, and transformative
composition teaching; the creative nature of the image weighs against use; the
complete frame is retained only because the relationships within that frame are
the subject of the analysis; and the credited, linked, web-sized copy does not
substitute for the original, the Fotografiska record, a poster, or a licensed
reproduction.

## Acquired shortlist

### 01 — *Love These Days*, from *These Days* (2022)

- **Why it matters:** Two cropped profiles form a near-symmetrical face-to-face
  field, while the red wireframe body and the thin flower stem bind the sides
  across the central gap. It is a concise lesson in bilateral tension and a
  digitally drawn line that reorganizes photographic space.

- **Use basis: fair-use.** (1) This complete image is used for criticism,
  scholarship, and transformative teaching about opposed profiles, the central
  void, and the overlaid grid; (2) it is a highly creative contemporary
