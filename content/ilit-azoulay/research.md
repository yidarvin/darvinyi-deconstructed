# Ilit Azoulay — research note

## Documented method and period

*No Thing Dies* was developed between 2014 and 2017 from Azoulay’s exclusive
access to the Israel Museum’s archives and storerooms. The artist’s project page
describes a 753-image archive that culminated in twenty photomontages in the form
of vitrines. The individual works in this selection are dated 2017 and are listed
there as inkjet prints; Vitrines 1–4 are additionally listed with gold leaf. The
Israel Museum describes the project as large-scale collages assembled from
photographs of collection artifacts and hidden museum spaces. These are documented
production facts, not deductions from the display JPEGs. [Source notes](sources.md)

## Technical limits

The supplied artist-page renditions retain no camera EXIF, and the cited records do
not document a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed,
sensitivity, film or sensor format, digital-editing software, or the precise
printing workflow for each work. This chapter therefore does not infer capture
settings or manufacture from appearance. Its observations are limited to the
documented photomontage/inkjet-print method, visible construction, and deterministic
measurements of the ingested web images.
