# Ilit Azoulay — source record

## Selection and rights note

This ten-image shortlist focuses on *No Thing Dies* (2017), Azoulay’s twenty-work
photomontage project made from a three-year investigation of the Israel Museum’s
archives and storerooms. The artist’s public project page identifies the complete
works as inkjet prints (some with gold leaf), gives their individual Vitrine titles,
and serves the full web renditions used here. The Israel Museum’s exhibition page
corroborates that the project grew from photographs of collection objects and hidden
museum spaces, assembled into large-scale collages. All ten downloaded artist-archive
files opened successfully; their long edges range from 2,139px to 2,900px, well above
the 640px floor without upscaling.

All entries use **fair-use** rather than an open licence. The public artist archive
does not grant an open licence; the creator/rightsholder is **Ilit Azoulay** unless a
different notice is supplied by the source. Each assessment below weighs the four
factors: (1) this is transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching;
(2) the underlying works are creative photographs/photomontages, which weighs against
use; (3) the whole bounded frame is necessary to examine Azoulay’s composited spatial
logic; and (4) the chapter uses a web-sized, credited, source-linked copy that does
not replace an original, exhibition, book, or licensed reproduction.
