# Sources — Anastasia Samoylova

## Acquisition and rights note

All 12 files below were acquired as publicly accessible JPEG display copies from
Samoylova’s official portfolio (the linked project page supplies the title,
series, and context; the direct JPEG link is the retrieved file). The portfolio
does not grant an open licence, and these contemporary works remain copyrighted:
credit each image `© Anastasia Samoylova, courtesy of the artist`, link to the
portfolio record, and use only as a web-sized critical reproduction. Each use is
therefore documented as **fair-use**, not open content. The assessment is made
separately below: this book uses each complete frame for criticism, scholarship,
and transformative composition teaching; its particular whole-frame arrangement
is necessary to that analysis; the supplied copy is web-sized; and the chapter
credits and links to the artist’s record rather than substituting for an original,
museum record, or licensed reproduction. The artist’s *FloodZone* statement
identifies its subject as rising sea levels and climate risk, while the *Floridas*
and *Image Cities* pages establish the linked inquiries into Florida’s image
economy and urban advertising.

All downloads were opened and checked locally. Long edge: 1,500–3,000px (all
above the 640px normal floor). No image was generated, upscaled, retouched, or
cropped.

   complete frame is needed to teach the diagonal, submerged silhouette, and

2. **Fountain, 2017** — *FloodZone*. A bright tourist-palette fountain becomes

   · [JPEG, 1500×1200](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/595b8208579fb352b648962a/1605625224725-V7J93MX7US10WAC3ZUU7/Fountain+2017.jpg?format=3000w).

   replace the artist’s original or a licensed reproduction.
