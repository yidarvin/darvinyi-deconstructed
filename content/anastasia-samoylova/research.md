# Anastasia Samoylova — research note

## What the supplied records establish

The supplied artist portfolio records this selection in three named projects: *FloodZone* (the 2017 flood and hurricane photographs), *Floridas* (including the 2018–21 Florida images), and *Image Cities* (the Moscow and Times Square works from 2021–22). The source notes identify the titles, years, project pages, artist credit, and the project context: *FloodZone* addresses rising sea levels and climate risk; *Floridas* examines Florida's image economy; and *Image Cities* considers advertising as an urban surface. The records also establish that the files used here are publicly accessible, web-sized portfolio JPEGs, reproduced under the itemized fair-use rationales in [sources.md](sources.md).

Across those projects, Samoylova works in color with photographed sites, reflective surfaces, printed advertising, architecture, water, and signage. That description is grounded in the visible supplied frames and the series descriptions; it does not assert a hidden production method. The chapter treats flooding, reflection, and billboard imagery as pictorial structures that are visible in these particular renderings, not as universal explanations of the artist's work.

## Technical limits

Neither the supplied portfolio records nor the ingested display JPEGs establish a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, capture medium, lighting plan, post-production workflow, print process, paper, edition, or print dimensions for these works. The ingest manifest records no usable EXIF capture settings. The chapter therefore makes no claim about analogue versus digital capture, printing, or camera settings. Its tonal and palette figures describe the supplied web renditions only, while its observations of edges, rails, fence mesh, panel seams, and reflected stripes remain visible claims about the frames themselves.
