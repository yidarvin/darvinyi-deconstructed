# Zanele Muholi — sourced photographs

All eleven selected images are published collection display images of copyrighted
works, not open-licence files.  Each is used under an individual, documented
fair-use assessment for a criticism and scholarship chapter. The local files are
web-sized public display copies (long edge 843–1299px for Art Institute images;
850px for SFMOMA images), never a substitute for a print, licence, or source
record. The chapter will credit Muholi and link to the holding museum.

## Coverage note

The seven *Faces & Phases* works establish Muholi's collaborative, serial
portrait archive; the four *Somnyama Ngonyama* self-portraits supply the
high-contrast, costume-based self-representation named in the chapter brief.
All files were opened and dimension-checked after acquisition. No image is
public domain or offered here as an open-licence asset.
