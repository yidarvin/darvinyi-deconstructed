# Walead Beshty — research notes

## Scope and sources

This chapter uses seven *Travel Pictures* from 2006–08 in the Whitney Museum of
American Art collection and three later photogram works published as public display
renditions by Thomas Dane Gallery. Item-level titles, dates, media, credits, links,
and the project’s fair-use rationale are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Documented methods

Whitney’s titles identify the *Travel Pictures* as chromogenic prints made with a
Contax G-2 and processed through named airport X-ray machines (L-3 Communications
eXaminer 3DX 6000 and InVision Technologies CTX 5000). The accompanying collection
context describes the abandoned Iraqi Diplomatic Mission in Berlin and the airport
screening system through which the negatives travelled. These descriptions support
reading the colour veils and exposure shifts as part of the work’s logistical system,
not as neutral scene lighting.

The gallery identifies *Fold (45º/135º/225º/315º directional light sources)* as a
2012 work using Ilford Multigrade IV MGF.1K paper. It identifies *Six Magnet, Three
Color Curl* as a 2009 work using Fuji Crystal Archive Type C, and identifies *Picture
Made by My Hand with the Assistance of Light* as 2011. MoMA’s 2009 discussion of
Beshty’s photograms explains a related rule-bound procedure: rolled colour photographic
paper is exposed to additive cyan, magenta, and yellow light. The supplied records do
not establish an exposure recipe for every individual work, so the chapter does not
generalize that account beyond the documented context.

## What is not known

The *Travel Pictures* titles document the Contax G-2 and the screening devices, but
the available collection and gallery records do not give a reliable lens, focal length,
aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, or development settings for the individual
images. The web delivery JPEGs contain later Hasselblad reproduction EXIF for several
Whitney renditions; this is digitization metadata, not evidence about Beshty’s original
camera settings. The three photogram display renditions provide no camera settings,
which is consistent with their paper-and-light procedures.

## Reading the supplied files

The analyzer measurements in this chapter describe the supplied web renditions only.
The *Travel Pictures* range from very low measured edge density (0.0001–0.0564) and
restricted, colour-cast palettes to the more contrasty *Travel Picture Marsh* (p05
0.145, p95 0.780). The photograms show a different material register: *Fold* spans
p05 0.004 to p95 0.886, while *Picture Made by My Hand* has the set’s highest edge
density (0.2065). These values are useful descriptions of the accessible files, not
claims about the physical prints’ colour or density.
