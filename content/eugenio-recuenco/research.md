# Eugenio Recuenco — research notes

## Scope and evidence

This chapter studies twelve images from the artist's public *Dreams* portfolio,
identified on the official page as the **Infantil** sequence. The portfolio does
not supply individual titles, dates, process notes, exhibition captions, or
technical metadata for these images; the chapter therefore uses the stable
on-page identifiers (*Dreams, Infantil 1* through *13*, with the available
numbers) and treats each work as undated. Source URLs, copyright notice, and
per-image fair-use rationale are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md).

## What the record supports

The photographs are contemporary, staged colour images. Their pictured
arrangements make costumes, props, interiors, window displays, and theatrical
light central to the compositional reading. That is an observation about the
visible photographs, not a claim about a particular production workflow.

The official portfolio and the acquired web files do **not** establish a camera
model, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, printing
process, edition, retouching method, or date for this selection. The normalized
chapter files retain no original capture metadata that could support those
claims. It would be misleading to infer analogue or digital process, composite
construction, or post-production technique from the finished image alone.

## Visual terms used here

The analysis uses only directly visible structures: frame and sill, bed edge,
balloon, circular backdrop, stripe rhythm, cast shadow, figure, and the
directions made by furniture or bicycle tubing. The measured palette and tonal
statistics in each `analysis/*.analysis.json` describe these downloaded
reference copies; they are not claims about the colours or densities of an
artist's print or display.
