# Chris Jordan — source shortlist

This set focuses on ten canonical works from Chris Jordan’s *Running the Numbers: An American Self-Portrait* (2006–2014), a photo-based series that turns mass-consumption statistics into large composite pictures. The artist’s own public gallery provides the titles, dates, descriptions, and a Deep Zoom view of each complete work. It does not expose a single downloadable whole-image file, so the acquired files are clean web-sized browser captures of those publicly visible full-frame views, with neutral viewer letterboxing removed and no site UI. Each opens successfully and clears the 640px long-edge floor.

**Rights basis:** Every selection is **fair-use**. Credit/rightsholder: **Chris Jordan / Chris Jordan Photographic Arts**; no public-domain or open licence is stated on the artist’s gallery. For each work, (1) this nonprofit book uses a credited web-sized copy for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; (2) the work is a creative contemporary composite photograph; (3) the complete frame is necessary to analyze the shift between individual object and aggregate image; and (4) the web-sized capture links to the artist’s display and does not replace an artwork, print, source page, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

- Chris Jordan’s [artist statement](https://www.chrisjordan.com/Artist-Statement/1) identifies his concern with the difficult-to-see scale of mass consumption and the tension between beauty and horror.
- The [Griffin Museum’s 2026 exhibition record](https://griffinmuseum.org/show/chris-jordan/) confirms the *Running the Numbers* dates and describes the works as large images assembled from thousands of smaller photographs.
- The [Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art exhibition record](https://jsma.uoregon.edu/art/exhibition/chris-jordan) independently describes the series’ consumer-object composites and their relation to sustainability, health, and consumerism.
