# Chris Jordan: when a number becomes a field

Chris Jordan's *Running the Numbers: An American Self-Portrait* makes statistics visible by making them difficult to take in. At a distance, these are landscapes, park scenes, circular machines, striped fields, and compressed panoramas. Closer looking converts each apparent whole into bottles, cans, barrels, phones, uniforms, logos, cups, bags, cigarettes, or containers. The supplied complete-frame display renditions and documented statistics are collected in the [source notes](../../content/chris-jordan/sources.md).

## the whole before the unit

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="01-plastic-bottles" label="Plastic Bottles, 2007" caption="A pale, wide field tightens into a crowded foreground, so scale arrives as a change in density." />

*Plastic Bottles* refuses a heroic single bottle. Its density break falls at y0.697 on a slight 8° tilt, changing from pale upper field to packed lower field. Mean luminance is 0.508, p05–p95 is 0.341–0.647, and edge density is 0.190. Gray-green samples from #575955 to #a8a49d hold the distant view together until individual plastic forms interrupt it.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="02-cans-seurat" label="Cans Seurat, 2007" caption="A broad park stage and clustered figures preserve the quoted leisure scene while its surface turns into cans." />

In *Cans Seurat*, the distant bank at y0.159, central leisure group, and route from the near figure toward the clearing keep the quoted park scene legible. Mean luminance 0.557, p05–p95 0.306–0.776, and edge density 0.206 retain the separation sampled in #c7af75, #9d886c, and #777779.

## repetition given a center

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="03-oil-barrels" label="Oil Barrels, 2008" caption="Concentric barrel courses run inward toward a bright cap, concentrating an enormous count in a single visual sink." />

*Oil Barrels* turns serial units into a radial machine. Two barrel courses converge on the central cap at x0.500, y0.503, and the vertical axis at x0.500 makes the inward pressure explicit. This is the chapter's darkest rendition: mean luminance 0.080, p05 0.000, p95 0.243, and edge density 0.241. The palette moves from #020202 to a small #817b73 highlight.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="04-cell-phones" label="Cell Phones, 2007" caption="A horizontal field of retired phones makes modular repetition, rather than a singled-out device, the image's subject." />

*Cell Phones* refuses a conventional focal point. A mid-field break at y0.535 and a long module course give the eye a way to scan an almost uniform expanse. Its p05–p95 range is 0.533–0.761 with mean luminance 0.650; #8f8e8b through #babab7 keep the panorama close in value. The analyzer reports edge density 0.000 at working scale: its modular pattern reads as aggregate texture before it resolves into phones.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="05-prison-uniforms" label="Prison Uniforms, 2007" caption="Six orange panel masses make institutional multiplication read as a continuous wall." />

*Prison Uniforms* is compartmentalized before it is individualized. The six-panel field is nearly bilateral around x0.500, and its midline at y0.418 makes the panels feel administrative. #a46e44 and #a87248 dominate the p05–p95 range of 0.467–0.733; edge density 0.015 shows how small uniforms have compressed into broad colour masses.

## landscape reconstructed from signs

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="06-denali-denial" label="Denali Denial, 2006" caption="A logo-built mountain crest and its reflection use a narrow shoreline to connect corporate marking to a sublime landscape." />

*Denali Denial* gives the aggregate method a legible landscape skeleton. Crest, reflection, and shoreline at y0.625 let Yukon Denali logo units serve two readings at once: branding and mountain tone. It has the widest tonal range here, p05 0.047 to p95 0.945; #1b1c20, #95969a, and #e8e9ed separate dark shore, gray sky, and snow-bright mountain.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="07-paper-cups" label="Paper Cups, 2008" caption="Pale vertical cup columns make repetition into a deliberately measured rhythm." />

In *Paper Cups*, vertical columns do the compositional work. The overlay follows one column and the lower cup edge instead of fabricating depth in a nearly frontal field. Mean luminance 0.858 and p05–p95 0.757–0.937 leave #c5c1be through #eee9e6 to carry the rhythm through repeated vertical interruptions.

## the ethics of no focal point

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="08-plastic-bags" label="Plastic Bags, 2007" caption="An all-over bag field resists a directional reading; the conservative overlay marks only a dense region." />

*Plastic Bags* is deliberately hard to diagram as a route. The analyzer proposed diagonal convergence, but the visual check could not support those lines, so the overlay retains only the conservative measured anchor. Mean luminance 0.757, p05–p95 0.533–0.941, and edge density 0.231 describe a dense surface, not a scene with an honest vanishing point. The beige-white #94866b to #e8e4e2 range makes the material feel both light and oppressively full.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="09-cigarettes" label="Cigarettes, 2007" caption="Fine horizontal courses organize a nearly white field into an image of relentless serial accumulation." />

The low-contrast field of *Cigarettes* becomes legible by course rather than object. A cigarette course at y0.220 and lower band break at y0.852 make the grid-like order visible without claiming a scene behind it. Mean luminance is 0.858, p05–p95 is 0.808–0.929, and edge density is 0.000; #d0d0cd, #dadad6, and #ebeae5 are close enough that numerical abundance does the expressive work.

<Plate slug="chris-jordan" id="10-shipping-containers" label="Shipping Containers, 2007" caption="Small container modules flatten the lateral system of trade into one broad, colored aggregate field." />

*Shipping Containers* ends the sequence with a coloured system rather than a landscape. Two container courses and the aggregate-balance axis make the field scan as stacked infrastructure. The measured field centre at x0.648, y0.498 is an aggregate pull, not a claim of classical linear perspective. Mean luminance 0.593, p05–p95 0.475–0.729, and edge density 0.096 preserve shifts sampled as #977e65, #85917f, and #97a799.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make distance change the picture">
Arrange one repeated household object into a broad field, then photograph it from a distance where the whole reads before the unit does. Test whether reducing contrast makes the first reading stronger, or merely makes it disappear.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Find the structure you can defend">
Choose an all-over surface and draft three overlay claims. Remove any line that does not land on a visible organization. Keep a grid or single anchor if that is all the photograph can honestly support.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Turn an inventory into a composition">
Make a picture from a counted group of objects. Try one version with a radial center, one with vertical courses, and one with no focal point. Compare which structure makes quantity legible without turning the objects into decoration.
</ExerciseCard>
