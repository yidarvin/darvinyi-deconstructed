# Charlie Engman — research notes

## Scope and record

Charlie Engman is represented here by two connected but distinct public records. The International Center of Photography's 2024 AI exhibition, distributed through Google Arts & Culture, identifies five 2024 works by Engman: *Available Material*, *Algorithmic Altruism*, *Extended Producer Responsibility*, *Kantamanto Registry*, and *Projection Mapping*. ICP describes the commission as concerning human relationships and the social outcomes of AI. CONTACT Photography Festival's 2018 public-installation record identifies *Mom* as an ongoing, quasi-collaborative project with Engman's mother; it supplies the seven displayed views used here, including *Mom on the Ground 2* and *Mom with Plum*. [sources.md](sources.md) preserves the institutional records, credits, image links, and image-specific fair-use assessments.

## Method and visual conditions

The 2024 works join people, goods, textiles, water, and altered or improbable objects in staged-looking, shallow photographic spaces. The *Mom* installation views make a different operation visible: photographs of Engman's mother are enlarged as billboards and confronted again through street, factory, curb, wire, and traffic infrastructure. The chapter therefore treats each supplied rendition as a composition to be read at its displayed scale. Claims about AI mediation, staged relations, and the series context come from the institutional records; claims about line, framing, hierarchy, tonal range, and palette come from direct inspection and the deterministic analyses of these supplied files.

## Process, printing, and settings: what is and is not established

The source records establish creator, titles, exhibition context, and display dates. They do not establish a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting setup, capture format, editing workflow, original print process, edition, or the materials used for the CONTACT installation. The normalized manifest records no retained EXIF settings for any of the twelve supplied web files. Accordingly, this chapter does not infer camera settings or printing process from the web renditions. Its tonal and palette measurements describe these specific display JPEGs, not original files or prints.
