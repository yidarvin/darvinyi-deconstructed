# Charlie Engman — source notes

## Acquisition summary

Twelve real photographs were acquired to `raw/charlie-engman/` and opened successfully. The five 2024 works are presented by the International Center of Photography (ICP) through Google Arts & Culture; the seven *Mom* works are presented by CONTACT Photography Festival. All are publicly visible, direct web-sized institutional display copies. Neither record grants an open licence: ICP’s item metadata explicitly says “Copyright Charlie Engman,” and CONTACT identifies the artist and work titles. Each use below is therefore a documented, limited **fair-use** copy for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching—not an open-licence assertion or a substitute for an artist print, book, or licensed reproduction.

## Source investigation notes

- The ICP record identifies each 2024 work’s title, creator, date, and rights, and presents the exhibition as a 2024 commission concerning human relationships and social outcomes of AI.
- CONTACT’s 2018 record describes *Mom* as an ongoing, quasi-collaborative project with Engman’s mother, and supplies the public image files and artist/work captions. The page gives series and exhibition dates rather than separate creation dates for its individual images; this source record does not license them.
- All twelve files meet the current 640px normal long-edge floor (the CONTACT derivatives are exactly 640px on the long edge). No upscaling, generation, material retouching, authentication bypass, or capture was used.
