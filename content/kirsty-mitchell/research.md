# Kirsty Mitchell — research note

## Scope and period

The plates are drawn from *Wonderland*, a 74-photograph project made from
2009–2014. The official project record identifies the series and dates; the
work-by-work record in [sources.md](sources.md) supplies each title, year,
credit, public display-file link, and fair-use basis used here.

## Documented method

Mitchell's official project and about pages state that she hand-made costumes
and props and assembled the scenes at true scale in the English landscape.
That supports treating the selection as constructed tableaux, where costume,
prop, vegetation, and placement are photographically present elements. It does
not establish the exact degree or kind of post-production in any individual
finished image, so the chapter describes visible spatial and colour relations
rather than inferring a digital-composite workflow.

## Printing and capture limits

The official artwork records describe the works as signed, limited-edition
prints, but the supplied pages do not document a common print process, paper,
printer, edition dimensions, or edition number for these web-display files.
The eleven ingested JPEGs contain no capture EXIF settings. No consulted source
identifies camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film
stock, lighting diagram, or post-production software for an individual plate.
The chapter therefore treats the display images as evidence of finished
composition only and leaves camera and exposure claims unstated.
