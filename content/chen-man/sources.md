# Chen Man — source record

## Selection and rights note

This initial source set centers on four complete, publicly served Chen Man
renditions documented by L.A. Louver's *East–West* exhibition coverage. The
gallery identifies the early *Vision* frame, the two panels of *Five Elements:
Water*, and the commissioned Guess image; its exhibition materials identify
Chen Man as the artist and state copyright Chen Man for the associated works.
Chen Man's own site also carries the footer “Courtesy of Chen Man.” No open
licence is supplied, so each image is used under the documented **fair-use**
basis below. The files are unaltered public JPEG responses and opened
successfully after download.

The first three files are rare, compositionally essential public display
renditions whose long edges are 650px, 650px, and 650px respectively. Each
meets the 640px acquisition floor without upscaling, even though the short
edges are 621px, 557px, and 615px. The Guess campaign frame is 1280 × 884px.

## Acquired shortlist

### 01 — *Vision: Year of Monkey* (2003)

- **Why it matters:** A frontal face is made almost architectural by the pale
  monkey-mask overlay, crystalline headdress, and radiating field; it is a clear
  study of central symmetry disrupted by texture and graphic layering.
- **Source:** L.A. - **Use basis: fair-use.** Creator/rightsholder: Chen Man; copyright notice:
  copyright Chen Man in the L.A. Louver exhibition materials. **Factor 1:** this
  use is transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching about
  bilateral construction and digital layering. **Factor 2:** this is a creative
  contemporary photograph. **Factor 3:** the complete square frame is necessary
  to assess the centered face, perimeter ornament, and radiating background.
  **Factor 4:** this credited, linked 621 × 650px display copy is web-sized and
  does not replace the original c-print or a licensed reproduction.
- **File:** `raw/chen-man/01-vision-year-of-monkey.jpg` (621 × 650px).

## Research context

The documented canonical core also includes *The Astronaut* (2004), held by the
Victoria and Albert Museum; *Long Live the Motherland* (including Shanghai No.
1); *Four Seasons: Spring* (2011); and the other *Five Elements* panels. The
L.A. Louver press material describes the *Vision*, *New China*, *Four Seasons*,
and *Five Elements* bodies of work, while the Asian Art Museum acquisition
record identifies *Long Live the Motherland, Shanghai No. 1* and *Four Seasons:
Spring*. This pass uses the four named works/panels for which a complete,
publicly accessible, non-upscaled web rendition was directly available.
