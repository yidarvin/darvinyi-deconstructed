# Chen Man — research note

## What the source record establishes

Chen Man (b. 1980) gained recognition in 2003 through covers for *Vision*.
The L.A. Louver material identifies *Vision: Year of Monkey* (2003) as a
c-print and *Five Elements: Water* (2011) as a c-print diptych. It describes
Chen's digital treatment of the initial photograph as painterly layering of
strokes, texture, and imagery, and connects that approach to a fusion of
traditional Chinese iconography and modern subjects. The supplied campaign
record identifies the fourth plate only as an image from a 2014 Guess holiday
campaign. [Source notes](sources.md)

The selected works therefore support discussion of a constructed,
post-produced fashion/editorial image: photographic figures are integrated
with graphic, painterly, or composited fields rather than presented as
unmodified reportage. The sources do not identify particular software,
retouching steps, lighting setups, cameras, lenses, sensors, film stock,
apertures, shutter speeds, sensitivities, or editions for these individual
frames. They also do not establish a print process or dimensions for the Guess
campaign image.

## Method for this chapter

The four ingested public-display JPEGs contain no EXIF camera metadata. This
chapter limits technical claims to the documented c-print designations and the
documented digital, painterly method. Its composition claims come from the
visible complete frames and the accompanying deterministic analyses: measured
symmetry, stable horizontal breaks, tonal distribution, edge density, and
dominant palette. The image-specific fair-use rationale and credits remain in
the [source notes](sources.md).
