# Jon Rafman — research note

## Scope and documented process

The source record identifies the images as Jon Rafman's *Nine Eyes of Google Street View* (2008–ongoing). The artist's [9-Eyes archive](https://9-eyes.com/) names the project; the [Louisiana Museum of Modern Art exhibition record](https://louisiana.dk/udstilling/jon-rafman/) describes Rafman's practice as selecting motifs from a very large Street View archive. The selected twelve images are publicly displayed Guardian renditions from its 2012 gallery, each credited there as “Photograph: Courtesy of Jon Rafman.” The exact titles, locations, and any supplied dates are preserved in [sources.md](sources.md).

The pertinent authorship is selection and framing from the supplied Street View image stream. These are not documented as direct camera photographs made by Rafman at the locations. The images visibly retain Google interface fragments, seams, blur, and stitching artifacts; those are properties of the supplied web renditions, not evidence of an individual camera setup.

## Technique, material, and settings

The consulted records do not establish a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, negative format, print process, paper, edition, post-production recipe, or capture date for the individual selections. The ingested JPEGs contain no usable capture EXIF. The chapter therefore discusses only the recorded Street View/selection context and the measured geometry, luminance, palette, and visible interface artifacts of the supplied files. It does not infer conventional photographic settings.

## Era and source limits

The project began in 2008 and remains ongoing; the supplied selections are dated 2009–2012 where the Guardian record provides dates, while four captions give no date. The analysis uses complete-frame 760-pixel-long-edge public display files documented in [sources.md](sources.md). They are all used under the stated per-image fair-use rationale for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; they are not substitutes for the artist's project, an exhibition, a print, or a licensed reproduction.
