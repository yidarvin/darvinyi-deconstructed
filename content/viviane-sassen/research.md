# Viviane Sassen — research notes

## Record and scope

The supplied source record identifies ten photographs from *Flamboya* (2006–07),
*Parasomnia* (2010–11), *Pikin Slee* (2013), and *UMBRA* (2014). They are
public web renditions from Sassen's archive, with the title, series, and date
recorded there; the *100 Years* record is also corroborated by Fotomuseum
Winterthur, and *Testament* by MoMA. See [source notes](sources.md).

The record does not establish a single material process, edition, print type,
film stock, lighting setup, or post-production workflow for this selection.
This chapter consequently describes only visible photographic effects, not
unverified production technique.

## Retained technical metadata

fuchsia* retain camera EXIF in the supplied web files. This is file metadata,
not an artist-published account of process, so it is reported narrowly rather
than generalized to the series:

No usable camera settings survived in the five other delivered files. The
metadata does not document printing; no printing claim is made.

## Visual method evidenced by the selection

Across these series, bodies, paper, mirrors, vegetation, and reflections are
used as graphic material. The archive descriptions and the supplied frames
support an account of hard shadow, saturated colour, cropping, and occlusion;
they do not support claims about a universal technical recipe. The measured
analysis therefore remains subordinate to the visible image: it records palette,
tonal distribution, and spatial marks, while the chapter names the particular
body, mirror, trunk, or colour plane those marks actually follow.
