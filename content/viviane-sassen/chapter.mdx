Viviane Sassen makes a photograph feel less like a window than a field of decisions. A head can be a dark oval, a mirror a blade, a sheet of fuchsia a wall; bodies are cropped, doubled, hidden, or made to continue as shadow. These ten pictures move between *Flamboya*, *Parasomnia*, *UMBRA*, and *Pikin Slee*, but they share a refusal to let recognition settle too easily. The overlays identify concrete hinges: paired heads, mirror seam, tree trunks, flame, reflected plane, and color sheet, rather than treating detector output as the subject. ## bodies made graphic

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="01-totem" label="Totem, 2007" caption="Two heads and crossing arms compress a portrait into a nearly architectural dark mass." />

In *Totem*, the oval around the paired heads makes the most important fact plain: the two sitters first register as one heavy, shadowed form. The near and far arm lines then force the eye down through the orange-edged garment. The analyzer's palette moves from near-black #100d0e to pale gray #adabb1, and its broad tonal spread (p05 0.027; p95 0.706) explains why the light ground can make the bodies seem cut out without losing their texture.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="02-mirror-man" label="Mirror Man, 2007" caption="A narrow mirror cleaves a standing figure into a body and its reflected continuation." />

The small framed rectangle follows the mirror, not an abstract central axis. Its reflective seam converts the yellow shoulder into a hinge: one arm comes forward while another descends inside the reflection. Warm wall grays (#9d9695 and #807975) and the dark #161819 garment keep the mirror's split legible without making it a simple bilateral symmetry.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="03-dora-and-viola" label="Dora & Viola, 2006" caption="Two tree trunks bracket an embrace that vegetation and patterned cloth repeatedly conceal." />

The ellipse is deliberately generous because the image is about the pair becoming a single interrupted vertical shape, not about locating a face. The left and right trunks make a loose enclosure, while branches break the bodies into fragments. This is the densest image in the selection by measured edge density (0.175); its deep palette, beginning at #0b0a0f, keeps that abundance from becoming visually weightless.

## shadows, mirrors, and staged ground

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="04-jf" label="J.F., 2010" caption="A white shirt, bent body, green mirror, and deep shadow turn pavement into a shallow stage." />

The two body paths meet at the green mirror rather than at a portrait face. That small reflective field is the picture's pivot: it makes the leaning figures look like related cut-paper shapes. The analyzer finds a high tonal spread (p05 0.039; p95 0.816) but very sparse edges (0.012), a useful description of the photograph's unusually clean, graphic pressure.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="05-100-years" label="100 Years, 2010" caption="A white shirt and a bent body bridge a blue ground plane inside a large rust-colored field." />

Here the paths name one body rather than inventing two figures. The white shirt and bent torso pull in opposite diagonals; the blue ground plane becomes a cool counterweight below them. The palette is unusually compressed: #915e4a, #774834, #726d87, and #2c252b. Its p05-to-p95 range of 0.204 to 0.478 confirms how color, not luminance contrast alone, carries the construction.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="06-testament" label="Testament, 2011" caption="A small flame and a sheet of paper hold the lower threshold of a heavily shadowed portrait." />

The face ellipse does not promise transparency; it names how much of the sitter remains withheld by leaf shadow. Paper and flame form a second, lower event, giving the eye a bright passage through the near-black field. The analysis records the darkest average luminance of the selection (0.109) and a palette from #020305 to #d1c6bf. Retained file metadata records a Canon EOS 5D Mark II at 70mm, 1/250 s, f/11, ISO 200; it does not establish the artist's broader process.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="07-axiom-g03" label="Axiom G03, 2014" caption="An upright mirror produces a black wedge and a green cast-shadow plane on sand." />

The overlay treats the mirror edge, black reflected plane, and green cast shadow as three distinct propositions. They all meet at the same lower point, so a small physical object reorganizes the entire field. The analyzer's sparse edge density (0.004) and sand-green palette (#ca9471, #c08a69, #264231) accord with the image's almost constructed simplicity. The delivered file retains EOS 5D Mark III metadata: 70mm, 1/200 s, f/10, ISO 100.

## cropped surfaces and color planes

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="08-marte-01" label="Marte #01, 2014" caption="A cropped curved back, a kneeling body, and pool-colored ground turn a figure into a soft sculptural arc." />

The larger ellipse holds the broad curved back without pretending the body is whole. The lower line follows the kneeling body into the cool field. With an edge density of only 0.005 and a palette of blue-greens (#6b8377, #83a499) against skin and sand (#c8ab90), the image allows surface and crop to do more work than anatomy. Its retained metadata records an EOS 5D Mark III at 27mm, 1/100 s, f/9, ISO 100.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="09-mirari-2" label="Mirari #2, 2013" caption="Two dark trunks interrupt a broad, luminous water reflection and make the frame breathe sideways." />

This is the rare monochrome pause in the group. The separate trunk paths avoid a false symmetry: they nearly meet, but each changes direction and thickness. The broad ellipse simply marks the bright water field that opens underneath them. The grayscale palette runs from #141414 through #646464 and #a5a5a5, and its moderate 0.159 tonal deviation lets reflection carry the image without erasing the dark interruptions. Retained EXIF records an EOS 5D Mark III at 70mm, 1/200 s, f/8, ISO 100.

<Plate slug="viviane-sassen" id="10-almando-fuchsia" label="Almando fuchsia, 2013" caption="A fuchsia sheet spans between a holding hand and a profile, making color an active plane." />

The visible top and lower sheet edges establish a tilted physical plane, while the hand and profile give it two pressures: one offers the paper and the other receives its reflected color. Magenta is not an accent here; #de019d and #bc0069 occupy the center against a pale #d3d9e8 ground and a near-black face. The wide tonal spread (p05 0.051; p95 0.886) gives that sheet the force of a luminous partition. The supplied file retains EOS 5D Mark III metadata: 70mm, 1/125 s, f/9, ISO 125.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make an object reorganize a body">
Photograph a figure with a reflective, colored, or sharply edged object. Move until the object changes how one reads a limb or torso. Keep the frame only if the object is a structural hinge rather than a prop.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Let shadow become a second shape">
Work in direct light and make a photograph in which the cast shadow has a different direction or scale from the body. Check that both shapes remain legible before you add any narrative explanation.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Use one saturated plane">
Place a sheet, wall, or garment of one strong color between two quieter areas. Frame it so color redirects attention across the photograph instead of merely decorating it.
</ExerciseCard>
