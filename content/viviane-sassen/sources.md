# Viviane Sassen — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Viviane Sassen is a living artist; this copyrighted corpus is therefore sourced
from the public web renditions on [Sassen's official artist archive](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/).
The selection spans *Flamboya*, *Parasomnia*, *UMBRA*, and *Pikin Slee* so that the
chapter can trace her recurring use of hard shadow, saturated colour, sculptural
bodies, mirrors, and deliberate occlusion. Each downloaded image opens
successfully. Its shortest long edge is 1,500px, well above the 640px floor; no
file was upscaled or materially altered.

All entries below use **fair-use**, assessed per image. Creator/rightsholder:
**© Viviane Sassen**, as stated in the artist site's footer (and, for *100 Years*,
also on the [Fotomuseum Winterthur collection record](https://www.fotomuseum.ch/en/collection-post/100-years/)).
Factor 1: the uses are transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition
teaching, not illustration or decoration. Factor 2: the photographs are creative,
which weighs against use. Factor 3: the complete frame is necessary for the
specific spatial, tonal, and cropping analysis described for each work. Factor 4:
each copy is a limited, web-sized reference that credits and links to the artist's
record; it does not replace an original, exhibition, monograph, or licensed print.

## Acquired shortlist

1. **Totem** (2007), from *Flamboya* — `raw/viviane-sassen/01-totem.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** A compressed dark figure and bright ground turn a portrait
     into a near-silhouette, making contour and negative space the primary subject.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Flamboya* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/flamboya/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3364/totem.jpg`.
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use**. The complete vertical frame is needed to teach
     figure/ground reversal and edge pressure; this credited, linked, web-sized
     critical copy is transformative and non-substitutive under the four factors
     stated above.

2. **Mirror Man** (2007), from *Flamboya* — `raw/viviane-sassen/02-mirror-man.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** Reflection splits the body into planes, making the mirror
     a compositional hinge rather than a descriptive prop.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Flamboya* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/flamboya/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3366/mirrorman.jpg`.
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use**. The full frame is required to analyze the
     reflection's spatial ambiguity; this small credited teaching copy is for
     transformative criticism and does not substitute for the artist's work or a
     licensed reproduction.

3. **Dora & Viola** (2006), from *Flamboya* — `raw/viviane-sassen/03-dora-and-viola.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** The paired bodies make overlap and shadow read as a single
     sculptural mass, with identity deliberately withheld.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Flamboya* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/flamboya/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3407/doraandviola.jpg`.
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use**. The whole frame is necessary to discuss bodily
     fusion and occlusion; this limited, credited, linked copy supports criticism
     and scholarship without replacing a print, book, or museum record.

4. **J.F.** (2010), from *Parasomnia* — `raw/viviane-sassen/04-jf.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** A masked or turned figure and emphatic colour field show
     how Sassen lets a portrait become a graphic, psychologically unsettled shape.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Parasomnia* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/parasomnia/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3304/jf.jpg`.
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use**. The complete frame is needed to study withheld
     facial information and colour hierarchy; this web-sized analytical use is
     transformative, credited, and non-substitutive.

5. **100 Years** (2010), from *Parasomnia* — `raw/viviane-sassen/05-100-years.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** The image uses saturated colour, a hard cast shadow, and a
     carefully staged body to make the scene oscillate between portrait and abstract
     geometry.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Parasomnia* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/parasomnia/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3323/100years.jpg`. The title, date, medium, collection, and © notice are independently confirmed by [Fotomuseum Winterthur](https://www.fotomuseum.ch/en/collection-post/100-years/).
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use**. The full landscape frame is necessary to analyze
     the staged shadow/body relation; this small, credited, linked critical copy is
     transformative and does not replace the original, collection record, or print.

6. **Testament** (2011), from *Parasomnia* — `raw/viviane-sassen/06-testament.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** The photograph turns a body and shadow into an unstable
     emblem, showing how Sassen uses darkness to withhold narrative certainty.
   - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Parasomnia* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/parasomnia/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3342/testament.jpg`.      criticism and scholarship without market substitution.

7. **Axiom G03** (2014), from *UMBRA* — `raw/viviane-sassen/07-axiom-g03.jpg`

8. **Marte #01** (2014), from *UMBRA* — `raw/viviane-sassen/08-marte-01.jpg`
   - **Why it matters:** The fragmented reflected body makes the frame a puzzle of

10. **Almando fuchsia** (2013), from *Pikin Slee* — `raw/viviane-sassen/10-almando-fuchsia.jpg`
    - **Why it matters:** High-key pink, a diagonal body, and clustered organic form

    - **Collection/source:** Viviane Sassen, [*Pikin Slee* project page](https://www.vivianesassen.com/works/pikin-slee/); direct public web rendition: `https://www.vivianesassen.com/site/assets/files/3509/suriname_2013_1472def.jpg`.
