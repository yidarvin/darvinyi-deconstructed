# Erik Madigan Heck — research note

## What the source record establishes

The selection is documented by Staley-Wise Gallery's public selected-works page,
as recorded in [sources.md](sources.md). It identifies Erik Madigan Heck as the
artist and supplies the titles, dates, and venues/series used here. The present
set spans *The Blue Hat, Old Future* (2007), *Old Future* works dated 2013–14,
*MUSE* (2014), *Harper's Bazaar UK* editorials (2015), and *Without A Face*
works dated 2018. The supplied public JPEGs are the basis for the twelve plates;
the source record documents their dimensions and the per-image fair-use rationale.

## Technique, production, and settings

The gallery record establishes photographs and editorial/series affiliations, but
does not identify camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO,
film or sensor format, lighting setup, digital post-production workflow, paper,
ink, printing process, edition, or print dimensions for these images. None of
those facts is inferred from the visible pictures. The chapter therefore discusses
only observable organization—silhouette, crop, patterned fabric, colored ground,
and tonal separation—and treats the analyzer's palette and luminance readings as
descriptions of these web renditions, not as evidence of the original prints or
capture settings.

## Scope of visual claims

The overlays make modest, image-specific claims: masked or deliberately withheld
faces, large hat and garment masses, shoulders and profile contours, flower or
petal fields, and the line separating the white floor from the blue field in *The
Red Socks*. They do not claim camera perspective, lighting direction, or a shared
production method. The high/low-key contrast and palette observations cited in
the chapter are reproducible measurements from the ingested files in `analysis/`.
