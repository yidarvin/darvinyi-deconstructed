# Erik Madigan Heck — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This is a twelve-image cross-section of the artist's *Old Future* work and
fashion-editorial practice. It concentrates on the distinctive flattening of
figure, animal, garment, and floral field that the chapter needs to teach. The
titles, dates, artist attribution, and public display images were taken from
Staley-Wise Gallery's [Erik Madigan Heck selected-works page](https://www.staleywise.com/artists/erik-madigan-heck), a representing-gallery presentation.
The gallery supplies no open licence. The page's public image responses were
downloaded directly, opened successfully after acquisition, and measure from
768 × 1152px to 1600 × 1067px; none was upscaled or altered.

Every entry below is a **fair-use** assessment. The creator/rightsholder
identified by the gallery is Erik Madigan Heck; no separate licence or
copyright notice is displayed with these records. **Factor 1:** this book uses
each image for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching,
not decoration. **Factor 2:** each is a creative, contemporary photograph, a
factor that weighs against use. **Factor 3:** the complete frame is necessary
to teach the stated relation of figure, ground, pattern, and crop. **Factor 4:**
each is a limited, web-sized, credited copy that links to the public gallery
record and does not replace an original, editioned print, editorial commission,
or licensed reproduction.
