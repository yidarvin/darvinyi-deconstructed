Sara Cwynar’s *Flat Death* photographs put pictures, objects, labels, and supports
on the same unstable plane. Across this 2013–18 selection, a reproduced toucan, a
display board, a clock, and a column of cups never settle into neutral subject
matter: each is made to show the traffic between objecthood and reproduction. MoMA
lists these works as chromogenic prints; the available records do not establish
camera or exposure settings, so the reading here follows the visible arrangements
rather than inventing a capture recipe. [Source notes](../../content/sara-cwynar/sources.md)

## screens and printed bodies

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="01-toucan-in-nature-post-it-notes" label="Toucan in Nature (Post it Notes), 2013" caption="A toucan and its sweeping branch remain visible through a loose screen of green stickers and tape tabs." />

In *Toucan in Nature*, the bird’s pale beak and dark body make a low, legible mass,
but the sticker screen continually refuses an unobstructed view. The overlay’s
branch sweep follows the light diagonal that carries the bird rightward; it is not a
false perspective diagram. With mean luminance 0.381 and edge density 0.135, the
image can stay dense without becoming a single dark thicket. The analyzer’s palette
of brown-black #231810, leafy #6f994e, and ochre #cbaa73 lets printed jungle and
hand-applied interruption occupy the same color world.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="02-print-test-panel-darkroom-manuals" label="Print Test Panel (Darkroom Manuals), 2014" caption="A stepped halftone panel is held in a quiet gray field while colored outlines visibly misregister around its edges." />

The panel in *Print Test Panel* is a serial object, not a scene. The two overlay
lines name its offset top and base, where cyan, magenta, yellow, and red contours
make registration error into a rhythm. Its large gray surround helps the compact
form read as a specimen. The image is relatively bright (mean 0.634) yet retains a
wide 0.153–0.761 tonal interval; pale #b6b7ba is punctured by teal #4c8d8c,
near-black #222234, and magenta #83345c.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="03-display-stand-no-64" label="Display Stand, No. 64 CONS H. 8 1/4 W. 24 D. 16 1/2, 2014" caption="Tall package stacks bracket a shallow display field, with product rows held above the printed dimension strip." />

*Display Stand* makes a retail fixture into an image of distributed attention.
Certs and Clorets stacks brace a packed central register; the overlay’s two package
bands follow the shelf-like cadence rather than treating package corners as a
vanishing point. Blue #7ea9ba and #4f7f92 create the broad container, while
red-orange #c06353 and white packaging activate its center. The low textual strip
outside the marked field gives the photograph a measured, catalogue-like baseline.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="04-vases-encyclopedia-pictures" label="Vases (Encyclopedia Pictures), 2014" caption="Rotated picture fragments collect into a broad collage field and a lower spill, leaving black negative space active." />

In *Vases*, reproduction fragments fall into clusters without becoming a grid. The
overlay’s large field and lower spill preserve that difference: they describe
concentration, not a literal vessel or horizon. The black ground has mean luminance
0.143 and a p05 of 0.047, making white margins and curled paper feel unusually
bright. A restrained palette of #131013, #1d1b1a, gray #716c6a, and #9b9898 keeps the
scattered printed vessels suspended in a shallow, material dark.

## procedures made visible

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="05-cut-picturing-times-of-your-life" label="Cut (from Picturing the Times of Your Life), 2013" caption="A taped three-panel image and a thin table edge divide a near-monochrome red room." />

The narrow image strip in *Cut* concentrates the only small action: hands and
scissors work just left of center. Beneath it, the thin tabletop opens a large void
between the legs. The overlay names those supports, not the analyzer’s spurious
“horizon.” This is the smoothest plate in the set (edge density 0.011): its limited
0.122–0.337 tonal span and close reds #900503, #a41709, and #6b100b force value and
construction, rather than hue contrast, to organize the frame.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="06-our-natural-world-books-1" label="Our Natural World (Books 1), 2013" caption="A literal pegboard grid holds a crowded book field, with the central title and lower stacks making a shifting inventory." />

The pegboard in *Our Natural World* supplies an honest frontal grid, while covers,
spines, and shadows resist its regularity. The central title is marked as a local
anchor, and the lower polyline follows a book-stack rhythm rather than asserting
depth. Edge density 0.113 reflects the dense overlap. The gray scale runs from
#2c2d2f through #848485 to #c9c9cb, making typography, book edges, and pegboard
holes compete without a single color cue.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="07-man-from-contact-sheet-2" label="Man From Contact Sheet 2 (Darkroom Manuals), 2018" caption="A centered portrait is intermittently revealed through irregular vertical registration streaks." />

*Man From Contact Sheet 2* makes the portrait difficult precisely by keeping it
there. The paired overlay lines identify color-registration streaks, and the central
frame identifies the occluded portrait, not architectural lines or a conventional
portrait background. It has the chapter’s highest edge density, 0.193, but only a
0.110–0.510 tonal span; #242325, #343648, gray #7a747a, green #427046, and magenta
#78284e turn procedural noise into a materially vivid screen.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="08-gold-nyt-april-22-1979" label="Gold - NYT April 22, 1979 (Alphabet Stickers), 2013" caption="Clock faces form a loose triangular rhythm inside a gold-toned collage crowded by alphabet stickers and a handwritten date." />

The two marked clocks in *Gold* are enough to show the picture’s loose timekeeping
rhythm; the many sticker rows are a second, denser reading layer. The handwritten
date keeps the collage from floating free of its dated paper support. Mean luminance is
0.423 with a compressed 0.243–0.643 span, and the palette stays within brown-orange
#8f4514, #a76225, #b97e40, #70300e, and paper-like #c3a076. The result is frontal,
not a shallow room receding behind the objects.

## stacks, measures, and monuments

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="09-time-is-up-2" label="Time Is Up 2 (Darkroom Manuals), 2018" caption="A stopwatch rim and raised hand are fractured by colored registration smears above a text-bearing footer." />

The large stopwatch in *Time Is Up 2* occupies nearly the whole vertical field. Its
rim and hand are stable enough for the overlay to name, while the text footer cuts
against the dial rather than becoming a false horizon. The broad 0.078–0.714 tonal
range preserves white numerals against #1f1f1e and gray #a8a8a8, with blue #2e3546,
green #688461, and red #812e38 supplied by the broken channels.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="10-color-bars-2" label="Color Bars 2 (Darkroom Manuals), 2018" caption="Black margins contain an uneven stack of color bars whose misregistered edges vibrate rather than align." />

*Color Bars 2* is a centralized technical field without a conventional subject.
The overlay frames the stack and gives two sample band edges where the channel
offsets are most legible; it does not turn the jagged contours into dozens of
separate objects. The image is dark (mean 0.217, p05 0.020) and comparatively smooth
(edge density 0.039), so cyan #167795, magenta-red #990c2e, yellow #ab9b42, and
green #174c40 feel like broad blocks pressing outward from black #0c070d.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="11-man-and-space-books-2" label="Man and Space (Books 2), 2013" caption="A pegboard archive distributes overlapping books around the upright Man and Space volume and a middle book band." />

The second book picture treats its pegboard as a grid and the books as a sustained
counter-rhythm. *Man and Space* is a local anchor within an all-over archive, while
the middle book band connects separate piles without claiming one perspective
system. Its relatively bright 0.613 mean and 0.192–0.863 range make the layered
grays (#313033, #606060, #8c8c8c, #b3b3b3, #cfd1d1) carry both scale and overlap.

<Plate slug="sara-cwynar" id="12-corinthian-column-plastic-cups" label="Corinthian Column (Plastic Cups), 2014" caption="A central cup column is set against a backdrop seam and an off-axis printed capital reference." />

*Corinthian Column* turns its central stack into a deliberately provisional
monument. The marked backdrop seam divides the stages behind it, and the upper-left
capital reference counterweights the main axial construction without pretending the
whole scene is perfectly symmetrical. Mean luminance 0.294 and edge density 0.069
keep the arrangement hushed: blue-gray #30383a, #494e51, and #696768 are interrupted
by near-black #16181c and the small pink taped accents visible in the photograph.

<Callout>
  Cwynar’s *Flat Death* works make reproduction a compositional material. Their
  photographs do not merely show images and objects; they stage the seams, grids,
  registration shifts, and supports that make those images and objects circulate.
</Callout>
