# Sara Cwynar — source shortlist

This set focuses on twelve works in Sara Cwynar’s *Flat Death* series (2013–18), a sustained investigation of photographs, printed matter, color calibration, display furniture, and commercial-looking objects. The Museum of Modern Art serves the complete-frame JPEG display files publicly from each collection record. Every file below opened successfully at 1500–2000px on its long edge; the files are web-display derivatives, not print-quality substitutes.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The works are copyrighted contemporary photographs by Sara Cwynar; MoMA’s records identify the notice as “© Sara Cwynar” and direct reproduction requests to Art Resource / Scala. Each use is a limited, transformative reproduction for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The chapter needs the complete published frame to study Cwynar’s arranged relations of object, image, color, and support; a detail crop would eliminate that argument. Each copy is only the publicly served web-size image, credits and links to MoMA, and does not replace the original photograph, the collection record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

MoMA’s [Sara Cwynar artist record](https://www.moma.org/artists/40166-sara-cwynar) lists seventeen collection works. Its [*Search Engines* gallery record](https://www.moma.org/calendar/galleries/5254) places several *Darkroom Manuals* works among artists responding to image search, appropriation, and the contrast between rapid digital circulation and slowed or obsolete photographic processes. MoMA’s 2021 [interview on *Red Film*](https://www.moma.org/magazine/articles/578) further describes Cwynar’s interest in studio staging, reproduced and reassembled images, consumer objects, and color as a visual connector. Together these sources make the selected *Flat Death* works a focused foundation for a chapter on digital construction through photographic leftovers.
