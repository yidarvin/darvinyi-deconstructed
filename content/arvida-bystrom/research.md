# Arvida Byström — research note

## Scope of the source record

The supplied source set establishes a 2013 *Self Portrait*, three 2018
*Inflated Fiction* press images, and six 2022–25 gallery records. Fotografiska's
press records name Byström as photographer. Galerie Kandlhofer identifies
*Coexist*, *Pietà*, and *The end of the world* as photographic prints, and
identifies the 2025 works by artist, title, and year. The source set does not
document an original camera, lens, film or sensor, lighting setup, aperture,
shutter speed, sensitivity, printing process beyond the gallery's generic
"photographic print" wording, edition details, or post-production workflow.

## What the images support

Across the supplied plates, Byström uses an intentionally restricted pink,
white, red, and flesh-tone palette; shallow staged grounds; and crops that let
an object, a screen, a face, or a body fragment become a single strong graphic
event. The 2018 pictures turn domestic textiles, cherries, and an intimate
phone-camera address into controlled props rather than incidental lifestyle
details. The later gallery works shift that tactic toward objects and reliefs:
an image can be nested inside a hinged display, a small screen can crown a
machine, and a bodily form can be held by a manufactured oval frame.

## EXIF caution

The downloaded public-display JPEGs for plates 01–04 retain EXIF fields. They
name Canon bodies for 01–03 and an iPhone 6s front camera for 04, alongside
dates and exposure values. Those fields travel with web copies and are not
independently verified by the exhibition or gallery records as original capture
metadata. They are therefore not used as claims about Byström's camera settings
or process in the chapter.
