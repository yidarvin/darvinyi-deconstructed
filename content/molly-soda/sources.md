# Molly Soda — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This source pass centers the seven published photographs from Molly Soda's 2015
project *Should I Send This?*. The [Dazed gallery](https://www.dazeddigital.com/photography/gallery/19859/0/molly-soda)
identifies the project and credits every frame **“Photography by Molly Soda.”**
They are a deliberately coherent seven-image sequence rather than a false
cross-section of Soda's video, GIF, and installation practice: the changing
self-portrait poses let the chapter teach webcam distance, vertical framing,
eye-line, private-room staging, and the tension between body and interface.

The artist's [own archive](https://www.mollysoda.exposed/) confirms the broader
practice—webcam and social-platform work spanning *Inbox Full*, *That’s Me In
The Corner*, *Desktop Dump Archive*, and other projects—while the [2015 Annka
Kultys exhibition record](https://www.annkakultys.com/exhibitions/from-my-bedroom-to-yours/)
describes the bedroom/workspace and direct-to-camera performance central to this
period. The public Dazed gallery is the best lawful public source located for
complete still frames from this canonical project.

All files were downloaded directly from the public gallery image service, opened
after acquisition, and are unaltered JPEG renditions. Their long edges range
from 2,000px to 2,600px, exceeding the 640px floor.

## Acquisition result

Seven distinct, complete published frames are present in `raw/molly-soda/`.
The set exceeds the registry default of four images and does not use limited
source mode. Other important works located in the artist archive are primarily
time-based, interactive, or installation works; their thumbnails/documentation
are research context rather than a reason to extract arbitrary video frames.
