# Molly Soda — research note

## Documented method and period

This chapter considers the seven published still images from Molly Soda’s 2015
*Should I Send This?* sequence. Dazed identifies the project and credits every
frame “Photography by Molly Soda.” The [artist archive](https://www.mollysoda.exposed/)
locates the sequence within a broader practice that uses webcams, social platforms,
desktop material, video, GIFs, and installation. The [Annka Kultys exhibition
record](https://www.annkakultys.com/exhibitions/from-my-bedroom-to-yours/) describes
the 2015 bedroom/workspace context and direct-to-camera performance. These records
support describing the seven frames as staged, domestic, self-made photographic
addresses, rather than treating them as accidental phone snapshots. Individual
titles, dates, credits, display derivatives, and fair-use records are in
[sources.md](sources.md).

## What is not documented

The consulted source material does not establish a camera or phone model, lens,
focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO/sensitivity, file-processing workflow,
lighting plan, or print process for these seven frames. The normalized JPEGs retain
no capture EXIF. This chapter therefore discusses the visible mirror surfaces,
phone gestures, room elements, cropping, color, and measured tonal/palette findings
without inferring technical settings.
