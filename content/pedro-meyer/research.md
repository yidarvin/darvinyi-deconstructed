# Pedro Meyer — research note

## Scope of the supplied record

The source record identifies *Colonia Ajusco* (1974) and *Virgilio* (2024) as
the two artist-archive books used here, with *El Velo Negro* (1975) supplied by
LACMA. The selected pages move from black-and-white social documentary and
book-spread sequencing to a recent color cathedral interior. They demonstrate a
practice attentive to ordinary spaces, close social distance, and the way a
published page changes a photograph's frame.

## Technique and material limits

The supplied records establish public web renditions, book pages, dates where
printed, and rights credits. They do not establish camera body, lens, film stock,
negative format, exposure, processing, print process, digitization method, or
capture settings for these pictures. No such detail is inferred from the display
JPEGs. The discussion therefore concerns visible framing, tonal organization,
and page structure rather than undocumented equipment or darkroom claims.

## Recovery correction

The original download filenames for several book renders did not match their
visible subjects. Visual inspection corrected the chapter labels: `05` is a
crowded children photograph, `06` is *El Arca de Noé / Noah's Ark*, `07` is
*The Bedroom*, `08` is a *Viva México* flag photograph, and `10` is St.
Patrick's Cathedral. `11` is only a quotation page and is excluded. The source
record retains the original paths so the correction remains auditable.
