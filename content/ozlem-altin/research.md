# Özlem Altın — research notes

## Scope and evidence

The supplied source record documents a 2018 installation view, a 2022 museum
collection work, and eight 2025 works publicly displayed by THE PILL. It
describes Altın's method as working with her own and found photographs by
cutting, concealing, reproducing, and painting over archival imagery. These
notes distinguish that documented artistic method from the camera or printing
history of the web reproductions.

## Technique, material, and era

Altın's selected works belong to a contemporary, materially layered practice:
reproduced photographic fragments are combined with painted and ink-like marks
instead of being presented as transparent photographic windows. The 2022
*Pulse and Grief (encapsulated, reactivated)* is specifically identified by
Istanbul Modern as a photo print on canvas with ink and oil, measuring 112 ×
280 cm. The source record identifies *Psyche (grow deep)* as a 2018 work and
the remaining works as 2025 works; it does not provide a uniform medium or
physical dimensions for those eight gallery images.

The ten supplied files are public display renditions, not original artworks or
production files. Their palette, tonal range, and edge-density measurements
therefore describe the normalized web images used in this chapter. They do not
establish original paint color, paper or canvas surface, illumination, or print
quality.

## Camera and printing information

No supplied record documents camera body, lens, focal length, film or sensor,
exposure, lighting setup, capture date (apart from the installation photograph's
embedded 2018 timestamp), or a shared printing process. The 2018 installation
view is credited to Timo Ohler; it records the exhibition setting rather than
authorizing technical claims about Altın's own photographic capture. Apart from
the documented photo print on canvas with ink and oil for *Pulse and Grief*,
this chapter makes no claim about print process, paper, or settings.
