# Özlem Altın — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Özlem Altın is a living artist and the registry classifies this corpus as
copyrighted. This ten-image set follows her use of photographs as material:
cut or reproduced fragments, paint and ink, paired images, and exhibition-scale
constellations. It combines eight 2025 works identified and publicly displayed
by [THE PILL](https://www.thepill.co/artists/25-ozlem-altin/), a 2022 museum
collection record, and a 2018 biennial installation view. THE PILL describes
Altın's practice as using her own and found photographs, and as cutting,
concealing, reproducing, and painting over archival imagery; that makes these
complete public views particularly useful for close analysis of framing,
intervals, and layered gesture.

No source supplies an open licence. Every entry is therefore assessed as
**fair-use**, not as an open download. The creator/rightsholder is Özlem Altın
unless a credit below identifies the photographer of an installation view. The
public display pages carry no reuse licence. For each image: **factor 1:** this
book uses it for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition
teaching; **factor 2:** it is a creative contemporary work, weighing against
use; **factor 3:** the complete displayed frame is needed to analyze the
artist's cropping, layering, and spatial relations; **factor 4:** the copy is
web-sized, credited, linked to its public record, and does not replace the
artwork, museum record, exhibition, or licensed reproduction.

All files below were retrieved as direct public display renditions and opened
successfully. Their long edges range from 640px to 3,543px. The 640 × 251px
museum rendition of *Pulse and Grief* meets the normal long-edge floor; its
wide, segmented composition remains legible at that size. No image was
upscaled, generated, or materially altered.
