# Maisie Cousins — source shortlist

This set centers on *Dipping Sauce* (2018), Cousins’s densely staged still-life series of food, flowers, jelly, toys, and bodily-looking textures. It is a particularly strong entry point to the chapter’s concern with feed-era excess: *AnOther*’s feature describes the series as an investigation of delight and repulsion around food, while the gallery publishes fifteen complete, credited images from it. Twelve distinct public display JPEGs were downloaded from that gallery, opened successfully, and retain the complete published frame. Individual artwork titles were not supplied by the publisher, so the gallery’s file labels are retained as disambiguating identifiers rather than invented titles.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The gallery credits the images “Photography by Maisie Cousins”; no open licence or public-domain statement is supplied, and the artist’s copyright is therefore retained. Each assessment below is limited to this nonprofit book’s transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The complete frame is needed to teach Cousins’s arrangements of texture, scale, crop, color, and focal disruption; each file is a web-sized public display copy, credited and linked to the publisher’s gallery, and does not replace an original work, print, book, gallery record, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

AnOther’s 2018 feature identifies *Dipping Sauce* as the new series exhibited at Elephant West and reports Cousins’s attention to color, texture, food, decay, and the photographed urge to preserve meals: https://www.anothermag.com/art-photography/11314/maisie-cousins-on-the-weird-and-wonderful-world-of-food. Fotografiska’s 2020 exhibition record situates it alongside *Rubbish* and *Grass, Peonie, Bum*, describing Cousins’s macro photography of food, flowers, bodies, insects, and discarded domestic material: https://stockholm.fotografiska.com/en/exhibitions/maisie-cousins.
