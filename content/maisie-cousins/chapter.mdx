Maisie Cousins makes still life feel less like an arrangement than a bodily
condition. In *Dipping Sauce* (2018), food, flowers, gel, fabric, toys, and
packaging are brought so close that scale and category begin to fail. A seed can
read as an eye, an orchid as a face, a bowl as a stage. The complete web frames
turn the downward feed scroll into a sequence of dense, wet encounters. The
overlays name visible structures in those renditions; credits, access routes, and
fair-use rationale remain in the [source notes](../../content/maisie-cousins/sources.md).

## a field can make a spill directional

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="01-dipping-sauce-mg-0624" label="Dipping Sauce, _MG_0624, 2018" caption="Crumbs descend through satin folds, making a soft textile field unexpectedly directional." />

In \`_MG_0624\`, crumbs move down and right through the main satin fold, making
the pink textile a channel rather than a neutral ground. The overlay separates
that descending scatter from the dense upper spill. At 0.039 edge density and a
p05–p95 luminance range of 0.251–0.686, pinks \`#b3679e\`, \`#9a4a7f\`, and
\`#c98dbb\` make the orange crumbs an interruption rather than a separate object.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="02-dipping-sauce-mg-2325" label="Dipping Sauce, _MG_2325, 2018" caption="A kiwi, translucent pulp, and a flower mass flatten into one glossy pink depth." />

\`_MG_2325\` presses its cut kiwi against a broad petal mass until fruit, flower,
and watery gel cannot be securely sorted into foreground and background. At only
0.024 edge density, it relies on luminous surfaces rather than descriptive
outlines; pinks \`#d3a5ba\` and \`#c26585\` meet green-brown \`#b9984f\` in a
0.369–0.780 tonal span.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="03-dipping-sauce-dinner" label="Dipping Sauce, dinner, 2018" caption="A prawn cuts across flower canopy and noodle loop, refusing a single hierarchy." />

The prawn in \`dinner\` lies laterally across a vertical collision of flowers,
jelly, and noodles. Its long body obstructs the field while the lower noodle loop
makes a smaller, unstable circuit. Pale pink \`#e0cccd\`, rose \`#b06f89\`, and
red \`#78131e\` span 0.180–0.867, yet the modest 0.045 edge density preserves a
feeling of soft collision.

## anchors are often edible and absurd

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="04-dipping-sauce-dsc00461" label="Dipping Sauce, DSC00461, 2018" caption="Two yolks hold a flower-and-plastic arrangement together against a black field." />

In \`DSC00461\`, two yolks are bright anchors but do not stabilize the scene into
a conventional still life. The flower-stem sweep passes between them, and blue
plastic at right makes the surrounding objects feel almost synthetic. The p05 is
0.000 and mean luminance only 0.308, so orange \`#de971e\` and pink \`#d6a390\`
flare against \`#090403\`; 0.065 edge density keeps the fragments active.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="05-dipping-sauce-img-0423" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_0423, 2018" caption="Two tiny figures turn noodles and orange oil into a comic, unstable miniature world." />

The two doll figures in \`IMG_0423\` change the scale of everything around them.
One is caught in noodles above; the other floats in the oily pool below. The
anchored upper figure is the analyzer's strongest subject signal, while the
set-high 0.078 edge density keeps every noodle loop restless against reds
\`#bb561e\`, \`#b46254\`, and near-black \`#2a0908\`.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="06-dipping-sauce-img-0719" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_0719, 2018" caption="Petals and bubbles radiate from a warm flower centre submerged in cyan." />

\`IMG_0719\` is a reprieve from clutter. A submerged flower and warm center turn
pale petals into a radial bloom, while bubbles keep that order from becoming
botanical illustration. Its 0.011 edge density is the set's lowest: cyan
\`#28a9a0\`, pale green \`#9dbf8d\`, and yellow-brown \`#9e760f\` do the structural
work across a limited 0.388–0.733 range.

## material has its own cast of characters

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="07-dipping-sauce-img-0573" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_0573, 2018" caption="A pale translucent mass is held in place by scattered dark berries and a pink ground seam." />

In \`IMG_0573\`, the pale mass is deliberately hard to name: fruit, jelly, skin,
or membrane depending on where the eye settles. Dark berry accents provide enough
punctuation to keep the near-monochrome surface from dissolving. The 0.014 edge
density and palette from \`#cbb9c7\` and \`#c68e57\` to \`#1f0909\` support that
economy; the overlay's seam is a division, not an invented horizon.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="08-dipping-sauce-img-1051" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_1051, 2018" caption="A printed color strip diagonally interrupts wet fruit and berry material." />

The printed strip in \`IMG_1051\` is an aggressively artificial edge inside a wet,
organic field. Its parallel borders form the clearest diagonal and pin the dark
berry cluster against it. The 0.071 edge density and 0.071–0.761 tonal span make
that interruption crisp: red \`#ad2e29\`, blue \`#27758a\`, and pale \`#ceb3b9\`
sit beside the strip's dense printed blocks.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="09-dipping-sauce-img-1416" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_1416, 2018" caption="A grapefruit half, dark seed cluster, and plastic packaging compete inside the same candy-colored field." />

\`IMG_1416\` gives the grapefruit an expected radial pull, then spoils that order
with dark seeds, plastic, and a pink leaf-like fragment. The fruit ellipse shows
how much of the frame is claimed by anatomy; the plastic path keeps it from
resolving into a natural specimen. Red-pinks \`#b84d3e\`, \`#942c2c\`, and
\`#be7174\` dominate a 0.059–0.761 span, while 0.064 edge density keeps membranes
and interruptions active.

## miniature scenes keep opening inside the food

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="10-dipping-sauce-img-1529" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_1529, 2018" caption="White mushroom stems build a vertical column through blue gel and red threads." />

The white mushroom stems in \`IMG_1529\` make an upright column through blue gel.
The red thread tangle to their right resists it with loose lateral movement. The
overlay tracks only that visible column and tangle, not a presumed technique.
Blue \`#1172a5\`, off-white \`#d3d1cd\`, and red \`#953a3d\` span 0.255–0.843,
with 0.052 edge density making the stems crisp against the surrounding surface.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="11-dipping-sauce-img-1609" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_1609, 2018" caption="An orchid face and patterned throat pull a scattered food field into a small, frontal encounter." />

The orchid in \`IMG_1609\` offers the closest thing to a face in the sequence.
Its patterned throat and lower lip make a small frontal event amid petals, noodles,
and orange liquid. That apparent face is a visual effect, not an inference of
intention. Pinks \`#b67b70\` and \`#9a4d4a\`, dark red \`#7a200f\`, and orange
\`#ba5f19\` occupy a 0.176–0.714 range; low 0.031 edge density lets pattern carry
the encounter.

<Plate slug="maisie-cousins" id="12-dipping-sauce-img-8141" label="Dipping Sauce, IMG_8141, 2018" caption="A blue mermaid turns the red bowl into a contained scene, counterweighted by a prawn at lower right." />

\`IMG_8141\` places a blue mermaid toy inside a round red bowl and thereby opens a
second scene inside the larger meal. The bowl is both container and frame; the
mermaid establishes the small drama, while the lower-right prawn counterbalances
it. Red \`#a13313\`, orange \`#e56b1f\`, blue \`#254365\`, and a moderate 0.041
edge density separate the figure from an otherwise orange field without
simplifying the surrounding food.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Let one material reroute another">
Place a saturated crumb, seed, or fragment on folded textile. Make the folds
create a directional route, then move the fragment until it either joins or
interrupts that route. Keep the frame close enough that texture is active.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Build a tiny scene in a larger mess">
Use a bowl, glass, or other container as an internal frame. Place one small toy,
flower, or food form inside it, then surround it with unrelated materials. The
smaller scene should remain legible without cleaning up the larger field.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Use an artificial edge honestly">
Introduce a visible printed strip, label, plastic edge, or packaging seam into a
food or flower arrangement. Let its straightness change the reading of the
organic forms around it, and identify the two edges doing the most work.
</ExerciseCard>
