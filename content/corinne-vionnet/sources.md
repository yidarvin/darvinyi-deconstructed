# Corinne Vionnet — source record

## Acquisition and rights approach

The twelve files in `raw/corinne-vionnet/` are direct 800px public display
renditions from Vionnet's [*Photo Opportunities* portfolio](https://corinnevionnet.com/Photo-Opportunities), downloaded on 2026-07-25. The artist describes the series as a 2005–ongoing project made by finding and layering crowdsourced tourist views; the page's longer account calls the body of work *Photo Opportunities* (2005–2014) and identifies the Taj Mahal, Giza Pyramids, Hollywood sign, and Parthenon among its subjects. The portfolio captions do not assign individual titles to its gallery images, so the landmark names below are descriptive locators, not asserted formal titles.

These are contemporary, copyrighted artworks by Corinne Vionnet; the public display files are **not** presented as open licensed. Each selection below is a separate, deliberately web-sized **fair-use** reproduction for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. Credit: © Corinne Vionnet; retain the artist and portfolio link. The source page is the authoritative artist presentation and a 2011 Kehrer Verlag monograph documents 42 images from the series.
