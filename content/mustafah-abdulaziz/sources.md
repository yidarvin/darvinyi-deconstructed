# Mustafah Abdulaziz — source shortlist

This twelve-image selection uses the Leica Oskar Barnack Award’s public 2019
finalist presentation of Abdulaziz’s long-term *Water* project. The award page
identifies the project as begun in 2011 and supplies the place-based captions
used below; where that record does not supply an individual exposure year, the
date is recorded as **2011–2019 (project period; individual date not stated)**
rather than invented. It makes a compact global sequence of engineered scarcity,
religious and domestic water use, disaster, and altered coastlines.

All files were retrieved directly from the award’s publicly reachable display
renditions, opened successfully, and measure 990–1,015px on the long edge. No
source grants an open licence. The use basis for every entry is therefore
**fair-use**: a web-sized, credited complete frame used for nonprofit criticism,
scholarship, and transformative composition teaching. The author/rightsholder is
**Mustafah Abdulaziz (copyrighted; no open licence stated)**. The direct display
file is linked with the authoritative finalist record and does not replace an
original, exhibition print, book, or licensed reproduction.

## Selection evidence

The source is a 12-image, publicly accessible finalist sequence rather than an
unverified aggregation. Its page records the project’s 2011 start, names the
artist, identifies its support from the United Nations, WaterAid, and WWF, and
supplies every place-based caption above. The artist’s [current *Water*
portfolio](https://www.mustafahabdulaziz.com/photographs/water) independently
publishes a larger body of works from the project. The acquired group clears the
registry minimum of four images, so no canonical-image access failure remains to
record in `NEEDED.md` and limited mode is not applicable.
