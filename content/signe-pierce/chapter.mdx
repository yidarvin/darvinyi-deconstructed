# Signe Pierce: colour makes the ordinary behave like an interface

Signe Pierce's *Faux Realities* turns familiar American spaces and objects into surfaces that seem already to have passed through a screen. A donut, mirror, shop sign, restaurant mural, plant, motorcycle tank, laundromat, and CRT become luminous devices for directing attention. Saturation is structural: it binds disparate things, makes reflections usable, and leaves lived space hovering beside its own image. The twelve credited gallery display renditions are used for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching under the documented fair-use assessments in the [source notes](../../content/signe-pierce/sources.md).

## objects become stages

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="01-cheap-thrills" label="Cheap Thrills, 2015" caption="A glossy donut becomes a pedestal for a fan of dollar bills." />

The donut's pink oval gives the rising bills a theatrical base; the overlay follows its contour and the turn of a bill fold, not an invented perspective. Mean luminance is 0.612 but edge density just 0.0313, so lavender #c67cf0, pink #c73e9d, and pale #dbcfed make the small still life read like a glossy display.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="02-mirrorgaze" label="Mirrorgaze, 2017" caption="A car mirror frames a reflected face inside a dense, diagonally cut surround." />

The windshield edge cuts through dense plants and bodywork until the car mirror opens a smaller portrait field at lower right. At edge density 0.1174, maroon #3b2334, hot pink #cc1b6d, violet #5a18ac, and pale grey #9eacae keep the reflected face from dissolving into the screen-like environment.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="03-3am-donut-shop" label="3 AM: Donut Shop on the Edge of the Hollywood Hills (Night of the Muslim Ban), January 28, 2017, 2017" caption="A continuous neon run spans the empty shop while table edges lead through it." />

Menus, cases, stools, tiles, and a fan remain legible because the neon sign runs continuously across the picture. The overlay uses that run and one foreground table edge rather than treating every tile as perspective. P05–p95 is 0.086–0.835; mauve #814577, near-black #352233, and pale #f3e8f1 make commercial light feel both inviting and insistent.

## a room can contain another room

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="04-chinese-takeout-ii" label="Chinese Takeout II (Elmsford, NY), 2016" caption="The Great Wall mural creates a second landscape over the restaurant seating." />

The mural's wooden boundary and painted wall path establish a landscape inside the photograph, while the tables below make a quieter counter-rhythm. The measured mural-base line at y0.678 names that threshold. Blue-violet #62496a, #837eb5, and #2a659b carry the supplied rendition across painting, tile, and furniture.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="05-column-rail" label="Column Rail (SoHo, NYC), 2016" caption="A black handrail cuts across a lit fluted column." />

The rail supplies the decisive diagonal and the column replies with a dense vertical mass. The overlay traces those actual features rather than the analyzer's incidental convergence. This is among the darkest and most articulated plates (mean 0.202; edge density 0.1273): black #0f0b0d, red #c5454b, and pale blue #92bbdb make projected light feel solid.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="06-heightened-realism" label="Heightened Realism, 2016" caption="A wall clock, lit blinds, and foreground fronds layer an empty interior." />

The clock is a small inset target, the blinds a bright rectangle, and the foreground frond a screen over the lower field. A moderate mean luminance of 0.351 and #1668a9, #684ea7, #2f1c86, and #cf8acd make domestic space appear lit for display rather than occupation.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="07-hyperspace" label="Hyperspace (Beverly Hills), 2017" caption="Ceiling seams and parallel fluorescent tubes make a tiled corner a chromatic diagram." />

A ceiling seam cuts toward the tiled corner while two fluorescent tubes interrupt it at right; the lower tile edge stops the view from becoming free-floating abstraction. Edge density is only 0.0275, but #8f53d0, #6a65d6, #ca55d7, and near-navy #15216a give the large planes precise direction.

## images of circulation

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="08-kawasaki-angel" label="Kawasaki Angel, 2017" caption="A blue fuel tank anchors the close view while handlebars thrust the eye upward." />

The tank first reads as a blue volume, only then as a vehicle component. The handlebar run and low measured convergence (x0.077, y0.687) make the crop feel propelled. Blue #0b73d9, red-orange #eb6446, and near-black #151c2e divide a mean luminance of 0.335 into hot and cool force.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="09-strip-mall-of-the-gods" label="Strip Mall of the Gods (Hollywood, CA), 2017" caption="Statuary forms a proscenium around the laundromat sign and lot." />

Saints, angels, columns, and fragments turn the retail façade into a stage. The statuary balance around x0.538 is not literal symmetry, but it holds the sign and opens onto a measured parking-lot convergence near x0.516, y0.651. P05 0.012 and p95 0.831 let white statuary glow against #05060e night and blue-violet asphalt.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="10-neon-snow-i" label="Neon Snow Pt. I (SoHo, NYC), 2017" caption="A dark snow boundary and cyan melt channel make coloured snow an all-over surface." />

The dark upper boundary and cyan channel give the field only a loose direction; their main job is to make depth uncertain. This is the least edge-dense image (0.0110) and one of the brightest (mean 0.619), so #eca2d6, #e6598d, and #3993a4 read as luminous matter rather than local colour.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="11-neon-palm" label="Neon Palm (Studio City), 2015" caption="A palm spine crosses a dotted pink field and a lampshade halo." />

The palm is not a simple cutout: its spine bends through the lampshade's halo and dotted field, making an organic silhouette share a pixel-like rhythm. Low mean luminance (0.246) and p95 0.482 keep #63145d, #93127d, and #d4109b layered by the dark frond.

<Plate slug="signe-pierce" id="12-self-parallaxxx" label="Self-Parallaxxx, 2017" caption="A CRT, its reflection, flowers, and camera mount create a recursive apparatus of looking." />

The CRT and its reflection make nested frames; flowers supply a soft bright layer, while the camera mount points back to the apparatus that sees. The overlay stays with these visible structures rather than treating the screen image as evidence about its subject. Near-black #140c10, red #dc294c, pink #f381af, pale blue #b8d6df, and cyan #11aada span p05 0.016 to p95 0.835.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make colour assign a role">
Photograph one ordinary object under a dominant coloured light. Make the colour turn it into a pedestal, signal, reflection, or barrier; then compare a neutral-light version.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Put a portrait inside an apparatus">
Use a mirror, screen, window, or phone to create a smaller portrait field. Keep one surrounding edge active so the inner image competes with its apparatus.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Let a commercial space become a set">
In a shop, restaurant, car park, or laundromat, find one sign, tile edge, counter, or stripe that can organize an empty frame after dark.
</ExerciseCard>
