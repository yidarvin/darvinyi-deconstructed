# Signe Pierce — research notes

## Scope and record

Signe Pierce (b. 1988) is represented through twelve publicly displayed web renditions from Annka Kultys Gallery's *Faux Realities* exhibition (London, 2017). The gallery record identifies the selection by title and date, describes the exhibition as more than thirty original photographs, and identifies C-type prints. [sources.md](sources.md) preserves the gallery records, direct display URLs, credit, and image-specific fair-use assessments.

## Method and visual conditions

The exhibition record supports describing highly saturated, hyperreal photographs of palms, strip malls, surveillance, and ordinary spaces made strange by mediation and fabrication. A later *American Reflexxx* gallery record, linked in the source notes, describes Pierce's broader postdigital practice in relation to gender, identity, and the boundary between simulation and the real. This chapter limits its claims to visible structure in the supplied renditions: framed reflections, seams, silhouettes, commercial signage, screens, and measured tonal/palette relations.

## Process and settings: what is and is not established

The gallery record establishes exhibition context, titles, dates, and C-type print status. It does not establish camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting, capture format, or editing workflow. Some display JPEGs retain embedded device metadata: 05-column-rail.jpg records a Canon EOS 7D, EF-S 18–135mm lens, 44mm, 1/13 s, f/5, ISO 1000; 07-hyperspace.jpg records an iPhone 5s, 4.12mm, 1/60 s, f/2.2, ISO 40; 09-strip-mall-of-the-gods.jpg records an iPhone 6s, 4.15mm, 1/20 s, f/2.2, ISO 200; and 11-neon-palm.jpg records an iPhone 5s, 4.12mm, 1/20 s, f/2.2, ISO 320. The gallery does not authenticate these tags as original capture records, so they are not treated as facts about Pierce's process. All visual measurements concern supplied web renditions, not editioned prints.
