# Signe Pierce — source shortlist

This selection draws from Signe Pierce’s *Faux Realities* exhibition at Annka Kultys Gallery (London, 2017). The gallery’s public checklist identifies the 30 exhibited C-type prints by title, date, and edition, and its exhibition text characterizes the project as hyper-saturated photographs of palms, strip malls, surveillance, and other spaces that invite questions about mediation and fabrication. The twelve selected gallery-served JPEGs below were downloaded directly from the exhibition’s publicly visible artwork displays, opened successfully, and retain the complete published frames. All exceed the 640px long-edge floor.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The gallery presents the works as limited-edition C-type prints; no open licence or public-domain statement is supplied. ## Research context

Annka Kultys Gallery’s *Faux Realities* exhibition record describes Pierce’s images as photographs of highly saturated, hyperreal spaces and identifies the 2017 show as containing more than thirty original photographs: https://www.annkakultys.com/exhibitions/faux-realities/. Its artist information identifies Pierce as born in Arizona in 1988. The gallery’s later *American Reflexxx* record describes Pierce’s broader postdigital practice as exploring gender, identity, and the boundary between simulation and the real: https://www.annkakultys.com/exhibitions/signe-pierce-american-reflexxx/.
