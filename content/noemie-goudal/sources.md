# Noémie Goudal — source shortlist

This set concentrates on *Phoenix* (2021) and *Tropiques* (2020), two concise, canonical sequences in which Goudal photographs constructed landscape scenes and lets their printed architecture, ruptures, and impossible depth remain visible. The artist’s public studio archive supplies the titles, dates, media, dimensions, and the complete-frame JPEG display derivatives used below. Each file opens successfully and is 1,494 × 2,000px (*Phoenix*) or 1,600 × 2,000px (*Tropiques*), exceeding the 640px source floor.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. Noémie Goudal is the credited artist; the studio archive presents public display images but no open licence or distinct copyright notice, so these are not treated as open. Each assessment is deliberately limited: this nonprofit book uses a credited, record-linked, web-sized complete-frame image for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The underlying works are highly creative contemporary photographs; retaining the whole frame is necessary to examine their constructed scenes and spatial deception; and the display derivatives do not replace an original print, artist record, or licensed reproduction.
