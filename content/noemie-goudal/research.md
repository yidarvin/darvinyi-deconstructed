# Noémie Goudal — research note

## Scope and evidence

This chapter covers *Phoenix* (2021), plates II–VIII, and *Tropiques* (2020), plates I–V. The artist's public archive is the primary record for the selected works' titles, dates, display images, stated dimensions, and the practice's use of staged illusionistic installations in landscape. The source record does not establish a uniform original camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, lighting setup, film stock, exposure method, digital workflow, or print process for these individual works. The normalized chapter JPEGs contain no retained EXIF settings. None is inferred here.

## Method and period

Goudal's 2020–21 sequences belong to a contemporary practice in which an installation and its photographic image deliberately remain entangled. The available studio context describes ambitious staged, illusionistic installations in landscape, documented with film and photography. In the selected *Phoenix* images, the pictured palms are materially split into narrow hanging panels and crosswise strips; clips, gaps, and panel borders are part of the picture rather than production accidents. In *Tropiques*, foliage, water, rock, and apparent recession are similarly presented as a bounded, suspended field. The visible construction prevents the photograph from settling into a single, transparent view of a place.

The local source record gives *Phoenix* as 2021, with a 200 × 150 cm vertical format for the cited plate descriptions, and *Tropiques* as 2020, with cited 200 × 160 cm frames. It does not establish a material or printing process for each individual selected work; the chapter therefore calls them photographs or images, not a particular print type. A Frieze account of Goudal's related *Southern Light Stations* identifies recurring architectural and atmospheric forms in the practice, useful context for this sustained concern with sets and unstable photographic space, but it is not evidence for technical settings in these plates.

## Chapter-use implications

The overlays name only what the complete frames visibly support: hanging image boundaries, panel divisions, strip joins, enclosed foliage, central openings, and water paths. They deliberately do not promote detector-proposed horizons or vanishing points into claims when those readings would mistake a torn, clipped, or layered screen for an unbroken landscape. Tonal statistics describe the normalized web derivatives, not the original works or their prints.
