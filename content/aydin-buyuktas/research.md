# Aydın Büyüktaş — research note

## What the source record establishes

This chapter studies eleven credited public display JPEGs from Büyüktaş's *Flatland*
series, made from Istanbul scenes in 2015. The chapter's source record identifies
the works, the 2016 Bant Mag presentation through which the files were accessed,
and the artist credit. Its research context also cites a Marmara Urban Forum report
describing drone photographs of Istanbul sites made from differing angles and
heights and combined into one image. Those sources support describing the finished
works as constructed aerial cityscapes with a deliberately folded viewpoint; they
do not establish the capture or editing sequence for any individual plate. See
[sources.md](sources.md).

## Visual method and era

The eleven works make urban plan, landmark, infrastructure, and dense roof texture
share a continuous-looking surface while changing the orientation of that surface
inside a vertical frame. Mosques, stadiums, a market, a skate park, bridge, roads,
and the Grand Bazaar keep their local identities, which makes the impossible turn
legible rather than arbitrary. The method considered here is therefore the visible
construction of the final image: route, grid, landmark, and fold. It does not
attribute a particular software process, drone model, or ground-camera workflow
where the source record does not name one.

## Printing and camera settings

The supplied records do not document print process, paper, edition, printer,
camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, drone model,
capture date, or per-work post-production. The normalized web-display JPEGs have
no capture EXIF. No camera setting, printing method, or technical workflow is
inferred from the completed image's perspective effects.

## Evidence limits

The tonal values, edge densities, palette swatches, and detected geometries used
in the chapter are measurements of the supplied display JPEGs, not evidence about
the original artworks, prints, or capture files. The credit, date, title, source
route, and fair-use rationale for each reproduction remain in [sources.md](sources.md).
