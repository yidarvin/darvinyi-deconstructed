Aydın Büyüktaş makes an aerial view misbehave without giving up the things that
normally let us read one. In the 2015 *Flatland* images, a mosque, stadium, bridge,
market roof, or road remains recognizable, yet its surrounding city becomes a
continuous surface that appears to turn upright. The point is not a fake aerial
view but a change of orientation made legible through real urban structure. The
overlays follow only that visible structure: a fold transition, a road or bridge
route, a stadium axis, or a roof-grid convergence. [Source notes](../../content/aydin-buyuktas/sources.md)

## landmarks make the bend believable

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="01-sultanahmet" label="SultanAhmet, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The mosque precinct moves from an aerial courtyard into an upright civic field, organized around a measured fold transition and courtyard convergence." />

In *SultanAhmet*, the mosque is not simply an object at the top of an aerial frame.
The square route rises into its façade, and the overlay's transition at y=0.451
names the hinge that makes these incompatible viewpoints feel attached. The
convergence at x=0.410, y=0.509 comes from actual precinct diagonals rather than
an invented one-point perspective. The broad tonal span, p05 0.094 to p95 0.776,
and high 0.198 edge density keep stone, trees, crowd, and streets distinguishable;
gray-green #4b483f and #616878 counter the pale stone #c7af9a.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="03-maltepe-stadi" label="Maltepe Stadi, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The centered pitch, curving track edge, and near-central balance make an unusually legible aerial geometric field." />

The stadium is a useful test of the project because the pitch, track, and their
curving outer edge arrive with their own stable geometry. The overlay keeps
that claim modest: a centered balance and the track edge, not a fictional optical
trick. The image is the selection's comparatively smooth plate, with edge density 0.119 and a narrow
p05–p95 range of 0.220–0.663. Green #606c34 and #89a339, red #ad5246, and dark
#4a3d26 make the athletic diagram persist even as the photograph denies it a
normal ground plane.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="07-fenerbahce-stadium" label="Fenerbahce Stadium, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="A centered pitch axis and terrace edge stabilize the field while the city surface continues beyond it." />

*Fenerbahce Stadium* makes the same principle more emphatic. Its vertical symmetry
is the strongest measured here, 0.933 on x=0.488, so the centered pitch functions
as a visual datum against the frame's larger turn. The terrace edge and convergence
at x=0.556, y=0.243 retain a real directional order. Dark green #2a3b28 and muted
gray-green #4c5649 carry the field and stands, while the p05–p95 range of
0.141–0.769 and 0.161 edge density keep the stadium from becoming a flat icon.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="11-yeni-cami" label="Yeni Cami, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The mosque's central dome and the plaza below remain readable as local facts while the district shifts between aerial and frontal views." />

At *Yeni Cami*, the mosque is a focal landmark but not an isolated monument. Its
central dome and the mosque-to-plaza transition at y=0.415 bind the building to a
surrounding street field, so the image has to be read as a district before it can
be read as a spectacle. This is one of the brightest plates: mean luminance 0.561,
with #ded4cb and #baad9f against #39362e. Its moderate edge density of 0.108 keeps
the plaza and mosque fabric legible without competing with the landmark.

## routes keep a city in motion

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="02-sali-pazari" label="Sali Pazari, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="A market lattice and diagonal aisle make dense repetition continue across the city's change of orientation." />

The dense market in *Sali Pazari* succeeds because its repetitions are not
interrupted by the bend. The diagonal aisle and a convergence at x=0.629,
y=0.409 let individual roof modules read as a directional lattice, not merely as
texture. With 0.193 edge density, p05 0.133, p95 0.839, and a palette of brown
#63534a, tan #99795e, and light #e4cebb, the crowded field keeps enough tonal
separation to remain navigable.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="04-kurbagli-dere" label="Kurbagli Dere, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="A creek-side route and street convergence turn a winding watercourse into a contour across the constructed city surface." />

*Kurbagli Dere* turns a creek into a drawn line. The overlay follows its
creek-side route instead of claiming that the water is a conventional perspective
line; its value is to move the eye from the urban fabric into the low fold. This
is a notably dark image, mean luminance 0.318 with p05 0.063, but its 0.204 edge
density preserves lanes and roofs in #313833 and #5d5441 against light stone
#ccb39f.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="06-galata-bridge" label="Galata Bridge, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The bridge deck cuts vertically through water, fixing the two shorelines and the lifted city in one frame." />

The bridge supplies a stronger directional promise than the water around it. Its
central deck runs vertically through the frame, while the shoreline transition at
y=0.149 keeps water, quay, and built edge distinct. The restrained palette of
near-black #282e2d, gray #4b4946, and warm #ceb5a2 matches a mean luminance of
0.338. The bridge reads as a route first, and only then as an engineering object.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="09-basibuyuk" label="Basibuyuk, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The cross-town carriageway and upper carriageway band keep the interchange readable as a connected map." />

In *Basibuyuk*, the cross-town carriageway is the counterforce to the image's
upward pull, while the upper carriageway band holds the road network across the
top of the frame. Together they let the interchange retain the idea of travel
even when the frame changes its orientation. It is among the most detailed fields
in the selection, at 0.212 edge density; dark #20231d and #4d4f3a contain the
light #d6cfc7 highlights across a p05–p95 range of 0.082–0.863.

## grids, arcs, and crowded surfaces

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="05-kaykaypist" label="Kaykaypist, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="The skate park's ramps echo the series' larger bend, turning a local curve into a city-scale rhyme." />

The skate park gives the series a compact self-portrait: its ramps already model
the arcs that *Flatland* extends to urban scale. The overlay marks the park-to-city
turn at y=0.733 and a restrained convergence near x=0.565, y=0.252. This is the
smoothest, lightest-feeling city surface here, with mean luminance 0.583 and edge density
0.063; pink-tan #b48968, pale #d4c0ae, and dark #1f181b let the ramps remain
clear as simplified volumes.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="08-demirciler" label="Demirciler, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="A central street and repeating roof-edge rhythm make dense urban fabric read as one coherent surface." />

*Demirciler* has no single landmark to absorb the view. Instead, its central street
and repeated roof-edge rhythm make density itself the
argument. Its p05–p95 span of 0.082–0.875 and 0.179 edge density explain why pale #e0d0c2 roofs can
remain discrete against #2a2225 and #5b4c47 shadows rather than dissolving into
an indistinct mass.

<Plate slug="aydin-buyuktas" id="10-grand-bazaar" label="Grand Bazaar, *Flatland*, 2015" caption="A roof-grid diagonal and measured convergence turn enclosure and urban expanse into one directional system." />

The Grand Bazaar works by making enclosure open outward. Its roof-grid diagonal
does not lead into an ordinary distance; it continues through the turn toward a
convergence at x=0.401, y=0.498. The plate has the selection's highest edge density,
0.269, which is exactly what makes the grid convincing as a dense, material
surface. Brown #351b12 and #663d27 keep the field warm and deep, while #dec4aa
lets individual roofs and streets remain visible.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make a route survive a change of view">
Photograph a road, walkway, or water edge from above. Make one version that treats it
as a descriptive line and another that makes it carry attention through a strong
change in scale or orientation. Keep enough local detail that the route remains a
place rather than an abstract stroke.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Use a landmark as a datum">
Choose a stadium, bridge, building, or other form with a stable geometry. Compose it
against a more irregular surrounding field. Afterward, identify the smallest set of
axes, curves, or repeated edges that let the familiar form make the larger space
believable.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Let texture carry a spatial claim">
Work with roofs, pavement, fields, or a market grid. Make the image depend on a
repeated surface, then change only the direction in which that repetition travels.
Check whether the surface still feels continuous before adding any additional effect.
</ExerciseCard>
