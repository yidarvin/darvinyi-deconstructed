# Lorna Simpson — source record

## Selection and rights note

This twelve-work set concentrates on Simpson's foundational 1985–89 photo-text
installations: pictures that make serial repetition, the withheld face, cropping,
and the physical interval between image and language into their composition. The
official [Lorna Simpson Studio photographic archive](https://lsimpsonstudio.com/photographic-works/1985-88)
and its [1989 portfolio](https://lsimpsonstudio.com/photographic-works/1989)
publicly supply the display renditions and object captions. The Whitney's
[collection record](https://whitney.org/collection/works/37959) identifies the
rights notice for Simpson's work as **© Lorna Simpson**. Each file below was
downloaded directly from the public artist-archive display, opened successfully,
and retained unaltered; all long edges are 800px or larger.

Every entry is a **fair-use** assessment, not a claim of open licensing. (1) This
book uses the web-sized complete work for transformative criticism, scholarship,
and composition teaching; (2) the work is a creative copyrighted photograph or
photo-text installation, a factor weighing against use; (3) the complete frame is
needed because its image/text architecture is the subject of analysis; and (4) the
credited, linked, web-sized rendition does not substitute for the artist's work,
collection record, or a licensed reproduction. The item-specific notes below state
the particular whole-frame teaching purpose for each assessment.
