# Lorna Simpson — research note

## Scope and evidence

This chapter studies twelve foundational 1985–89 photo-text installations from
the public photographic-works pages of the [Lorna Simpson Studio](https://lsimpsonstudio.com/photographic-works/1985-88)
and its [1989 portfolio](https://lsimpsonstudio.com/photographic-works/1989).
The supplied files are unaltered web display renditions, not substitutes for the
physical works. Titles, dates, display-photo credits where applicable, and the
project’s image-specific fair-use rationale are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Materials and installation method

The Studio archive captions identify *Waterbearer* (1986) as a silver gelatin
print with vinyl lettering, and *Gestures and Reenactments* (1985) as six silver
gelatin prints with seven engraved text plaques. The 1988 works include ten
black-and-white dye-diffusion Polaroids and ten plaques in *Stereo Styles*, five
silver gelatin prints and fifteen plaques in *Five Day Forecast*, and four
dye-diffusion color Polaroids, fifteen plaques, and ceramic letters in *You're
Fine*.

The 1989 works extend the grammar: *Guarded Conditions* uses eighteen
dye-diffusion color Polaroids, twenty-one plaques, and seventeen plastic letters;
*Kid Gloves* five dye-diffusion color Polaroids with five plaques; *Necklines*
three silver gelatin prints with two plaques; *Three Seated Figures* three
dye-diffusion color Polaroids with five plaques; *Untitled (2 Necklines)* two
silver gelatin prints with eleven plaques; *Dividing Lines* two dye-diffusion
color Polaroids with eight plaques; and *Easy for Who To Say* five such Polaroids
with ten plaques. These material facts describe the works, not the compression or
color profile of the downloaded display JPEGs; their corresponding archive links
are preserved item by item in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Documented limits

The evidence supports a mid- to late-1980s photo-text installation practice:
serial panels, deliberately withheld or cropped faces, and the physical interval
between image and language. It does not document a camera body, lens, focal
length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, exposure, film stock, or lighting setup.
Ingestion found no capture EXIF in any supplied display file. The chapter makes
claims about visible framing, repetition, values, and material structures—not
invented camera settings.

## Analysis limits

The analyzer measures the supplied whole-frame display images. In installation
views it may detect a gallery-wall edge, frame shadow, or plaque baseline. The
specifications retain only meaningful panel fields, body crops, lettering,
plaques, and repeated intervals; none uses a compositional grid.
