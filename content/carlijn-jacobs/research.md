# Carlijn Jacobs — research notes

## Scope and sources

This chapter studies twelve credited, public display renditions documented
individually in [sources.md](sources.md): the 2018 *The masked man, Vogue
Netherlands, Paris*; *Cherry, Paris* (2022); and ten pictures published in
2023, including the *Eyes* and *Sleeping Beauty* projects. The source record
places the group in Jacobs’s editorial and book/exhibition practice: the
artist biography names Surrealism, Art Deco, and Camp as touchstones, while the
Vogue Italia feature identifies *Eyes* as a 2023 Note Note Éditions
publication and describes *Sleeping Beauty* as a book/exhibition project.
These copyrighted, credited display copies are used under the individually
documented fair-use rationale for criticism and composition teaching.

## Method and visual language

Across this particular selection, a body fragment, cosmetic intervention, or
prop becomes the image’s main organizing device. Feet become a doubled red
motif; an eye becomes a diagram inside cloth, lips, pins, or a tilted square;
a veil makes a portrait’s boundary unstable; and a coat or cherry concentrates
color in a dark field. The chapter’s claims about axes, interruption, tonal
range, and palette are close readings of the supplied web renditions, supported
by the accompanying composition analyses. They do not assert an artist
intention beyond what the source record establishes.

## Technique, printing, and settings

The available artist and editorial records do not identify a camera body, lens,
focal length, exposure, aperture, ISO, lighting plan, post-production workflow,
print process, paper, edition, or installation specification for these twelve
works. The ingested display files retain no usable EXIF camera fields.
Accordingly, the chapter makes no unsupported technical or printing claim.

## Evidence limits

Titles, dates, project associations, credits, public-display links, and the
fair-use analysis are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md). Numerical tonal and
palette values describe normalized web renditions, not original files, prints,
or an intended colour grade.
