# Carlijn Jacobs — source shortlist

This set draws from the openly readable Vogue Italia feature ["Il lato più intimo della fotografia, negli occhi e con gli occhi di Carlijn Jacobs"](https://www.vogue.it/article/carlijn-jacobs-fotografia-intervista), which reproduces these credited works at public web size and identifies the *Eyes* and *Sleeping Beauty* projects. The twelve selected photographs make a tightly useful study of Jacobs’s recurrent methods: isolating a body fragment, turning cosmetics and props into graphic objects, using mask-like faces, and staging a subject against a deliberately spare or impossible-looking field. Each direct publisher rendition was downloaded without authentication, opens successfully, and retains the published frame.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. Jacobs’s works are copyrighted; the artist site and Vogue article credit her, and Vogue’s page carries a Condé Nast copyright notice. Each assessment below is individual and limited: this nonprofit book uses a credited, linked, web-sized copy for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The complete frame is needed to analyze each photograph’s spatial and graphic construction; the files are only display-sized and do not replace the artist’s work, the Vogue record, a print, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Jacobs’s artist [biography](https://carlijnjacobs.com/Info) describes a practice shaped by Surrealism, Art Deco, and Camp, and identifies *Mannequins* (Art Paper Editions, 2021) alongside editorial work for Vogue, Vogue France, Vogue Italia, Dazed, Pop, *D Repubblica*, *M le Monde*, and *AnOther*. Vogue’s feature identifies *Eyes* as a 2023 Note Note Éditions publication and describes *Sleeping Beauty* as a book/exhibition project; its image captions supply the named Paris works and dates used above. The artist archive was also checked, but its direct Cargo files returned hotlink protection; no protected direct URL was used or bypassed.
