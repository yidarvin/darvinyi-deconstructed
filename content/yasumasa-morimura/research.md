# Yasumasa Morimura — research note

## Scope and sources

This chapter studies four works supplied as public Art Institute of Chicago IIIF
renditions and documented individually in [sources.md](sources.md). They span
Morimura's 1996–97 actress/persona studies and *Mysterious Illusion of
KAINOSHO* (2023/24), a later art-historical recasting. The selection is not a
claim to cover the oeuvre: the source record also identifies earlier and
otherwise canonical works including *Portrait (Van Gogh)* (1985), *Portrait
(Futago)* (1988/89), and the paired *Daughter of Art History, Theater* works
(1989), while explaining why this chapter uses the four directly delivered,
web-sized museum renditions instead.

## Material and technical record

The Art Institute records identify *M Holding a Cigarette* (1997) and *M
Wearing a Military Cap 2* (1997) as internal dye diffusion transfer prints:
their image areas are approximately 11.3 × 8.5 cm, on approximately 13 × 10 cm
paper. The museum identifies *Self-portrait (Actress) after Greta Garbo 1*
(1996) as a 120 × 95 cm chromogenic print, and *Mysterious Illusion of
KAINOSHO* (2023/24) as a 120 × 75 cm inkjet print. These are collection-record
medium descriptions, not deductions from the browser-sized files.

The supplied sources document no camera body, lens, focal length, aperture,
shutter speed, ISO/film speed, lighting setup, negative format, digital camera,
or post-production workflow for these individual works. The normalized files
have no capture EXIF. This chapter therefore addresses only what is documented
about print medium and what is visible in the staged frames—costume, makeup,
pose, props, backdrop, borders, and tone—without inventing settings or a
technical process behind them.
