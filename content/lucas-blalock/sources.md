# Lucas Blalock — source shortlist

This set follows Blalock's conspicuously constructed photographs from the early still lifes through later image-making that leaves masking, cloning, and combination visible. All ten files were retrieved directly from Galerie Eva Presenhuber's publicly accessible artist catalogue and opened successfully as complete, web-sized JPEG display renditions (their long edges are 1000–1266px). The catalogue identifies the title, date, medium, and the notice “© Lucas Blalock” for each work. No open-licensed or public-domain rendition was located for these copyrighted contemporary photographs.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. Each is an individual, limited assessment: this nonprofit book uses a credited, record-linked public display image for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; the full published frame is necessary to analyze Blalock's deliberate digital interventions and spatial construction; and the web-sized copy neither replaces an original, a gallery record, nor a licensed reproduction. The source remains linked below.

## Research context

MoMA identifies Blalock's *Shoe* and *Right Shoe* (both 2013) as pictures in which he photographed a tabletop arrangement and used software to make a bag appear to carry shoe treads; it characterizes this and his other intentionally obvious edits as challenges to photographic purity: https://www.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2016/01/08/new-photography-and-momas-collection-part-1/.
