# Source shortlist — Amalia Ulman

## Scope and acquisition record

This chapter draws from *Excellences & Perfections* (Instagram performance, 19 April–14 September 2014). The artist’s [project index](https://amaliaulman.eu/) directs readers to Rhizome’s *Excellences & Perfections* archive, and the [New Museum’s project record](https://archive.newmuseum.org/exhibitions/2487) identifies it as a five-month scripted performance presented in First Look and archived by Rhizome. Rhizome’s public replay preserves both the posts and their original Instagram image responses. The twelve files below were fetched from those public replay pages into `raw/amalia-ulman/`; each opens and is 640 × 640 px.

No open licence or public-domain statement is supplied for these images. Every selection is therefore used under the documented **fair-use** basis below. The copyright notice is: © Amalia Ulman, for *Excellences & Perfections* (2014), as published by the artist and preserved by Rhizome; the underlying source of a reposted meme, where applicable, is not credited in the archived post. The archive is an access route, not a licence.
