# Amalia Ulman — research note

## Scope of the source record

The supplied source record identifies *Excellences & Perfections* as an
Instagram performance made between 19 April and 14 September 2014. The artist's
project index points to Rhizome's public replay; the New Museum's project record
describes the work as a five-month scripted performance and identifies its First
Look presentation and Rhizome archive. The selected posts are therefore read as
a deliberately sequenced photographic performance, not as a recovered private
diary. Titles, dates, captions, and individual frames are documented in
[the source notes](sources.md).

## Technique, medium, and settings

The supplied records establish Instagram posts preserved through a web archive.
They do not establish a camera body, lens, sensor, lighting arrangement,
aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, original file dimensions, post-production
workflow, print process, edition, or physical printing history for these twelve
posts. The normalized study JPEGs are 640 × 640 px and contain no retained
capture EXIF. Their present web compression and color are evidence of the
archived access copies, not a basis for attributing particular equipment or
settings to Ulman.

## What the sequence supports

The set moves from a nearly blank *Part I* card through mirror selfies and
tightly cropped fashion signs, then uses *Single, Taken, Busy Getting Money* as
a text-only transition. It next borrows the visual vocabulary of medical
documentation—bathroom mirror, tile, gown, apparatus, bandage, close crop—before
the apology heart, the floral-crowned cat, and the monochrome rose close the
sequence with emblems and surrogates. The changing visual languages make a feed
read as authored serial form: each square is legible on its own, but its framing
also sets up the next role and mood.
