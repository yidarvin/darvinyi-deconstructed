# Sølve Sundsbø — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This is a focused teaching set drawn from publicly displayed, web-sized
renditions in Sundsbø's own archive.  The archive describes the Norway-born,
London-based photographer as working with 3-D scanning, hand-painted
retouching, and other new technologies; the selected frames make those
operations legible as compositional decisions rather than as effects alone.
The ten files below were downloaded directly from the public image responses
linked by that archive, opened after acquisition, and measure 1,200px on their
long edge (or larger in the other direction).  No file has been upscaled,
altered, or taken from a login, paywall, or restricted service.

The works are copyrighted.  The artist archive gives the photographer's credit
as **Sølve Sundsbø** and lists Art + Commerce as representation; individual
editorial and fashion contributors may hold additional rights.  Each use below
is therefore a separate **fair-use** assessment, not an assertion that the
public display is open licensed.  In each case: (1) this non-commercial book
uses the frame for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition
teaching; (2) the work is a highly creative fashion photograph, weighing
against use; (3) the whole, web-sized frame is needed to analyse the stated
spatial relation rather than to decorate the chapter; and (4) the credited,
linked web copy is not a substitute for the artist's work, an editorial issue,
or a licensed reproduction.  The individual entries specify the particular
teaching need and source response.

  stare exits left; the sharp horizontal shoulder line arrests an otherwise
  diagonal portrait, making cropped negative space do the structural work.

  by Art + Commerce on the artist archive.  The complete portrait is necessary
  to teach the off-axis gaze, shoulder horizon, and black field; this credited
  web copy supports criticism and does not replace the archive or a licensed
  reproduction.
- **File:** `raw/solve-sundsbo/01-out-of-order-aw-2017.jpg` (920 × 1200px).

## Additional research leads

The official archive also identifies Sundsbø's work for the *Alexander
McQueen: Savage Beauty* catalogue, fashion film, and other *V*, *Vogue*, and
*Love* projects.  The current set already exceeds the four-image threshold and
offers an intentionally broad route through portrait, beauty close-up, graphic
retouching, staged ensemble, and screen-like overlay.  Later build work should
choose the strongest sequence from these acquired frames rather than acquire
an indiscriminate larger set.
