# Zhong Lin — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Zhong Lin is a living Malaysian-born photographer whose work is represented by Art + Commerce. The twelve frames below are a deliberately compact survey of the two
most useful public editorial presentations of her personal work: IGNANT's 2020
selection from *Project 365* and 1854 Photography's 2022 eight-image feature.  The
former explicitly says that Lin shared the Project 365 selection with the magazine;
the latter credits every gallery image **© Zhong Lin**.  Neither presentation offers
an open licence.  The files were therefore acquired as publicly displayed,
web-resolution copies for documented **fair-use** criticism and composition teaching,
not as open content.  Each opened successfully; the smallest long edge is 1,280px
(all others are 1,600px or 1,800px), and none was enlarged or retouched.

The work is sometimes published without individual titles.  Bracketed descriptive
titles below are cataloguing aids only; the source title remains *Untitled*.

## Other verified context

[Art + Commerce's artist profile](https://artandcommerce.com/artists/zhong-lin/)
describes Lin as Malaysian-born and identifies her work as experimental fashion and
portraiture using bold color, unusual composition, and innovative lighting.  It is a
useful authority for attribution and context, but its portfolio is not treated as an
open licence or a source for unrecorded image acquisition.
