# Zhong Lin — research notes

## Scope and sources

This chapter studies twelve publicly displayed web renditions documented
individually in [sources.md](sources.md): eight *Untitled* pictures from the
2020 *Project 365* selection published by IGNANT and four later *Untitled*
works published by 1854 Photography in 2022. The source record identifies Lin
as Malaysian-born and, through Art + Commerce, places her practice in
experimental fashion and portraiture marked by bold colour, unusual composition,
and innovative lighting. These copyrighted, credited display copies are used
for the documented per-image fair-use purpose of criticism and composition
teaching.

## Method and visual language

Across these particular pictures, portraiture is made through interruption:
gel drops, a butterfly, a bubble, flowers, stones, braids, cloth, and a frozen
object alter how a face can be read. The source record supports the dates,
publication contexts, credits, and visible motifs. The chapter's further claims
about arcs, occlusion, repeated bodies, tonal range, and palette are close
readings of these supplied renditions, supported by their composition-analysis
measurements; they are not claims about an artist statement or all of Lin's
practice.

## Technique, printing, and settings

The editorial source records do not identify a print process, paper, edition,
camera body, lens, lighting plan, post-production workflow, or capture settings
for the twelve works. Three supplied *Project 365* JPEGs retain embedded camera
metadata: [Tears] records a Leica Q2 with a 28 mm Summilux lens at f/1.7,
1/500 s, ISO 1600 (2019-05-19); [Butterfly] records the same body/lens at
f/1.7, 1/500 s, ISO 400 (2019-09-15); and [Bubblegum Profile] records a Leica
SL2 with a 24–90 mm Vario-Elmarit-SL lens at 34 mm, f/5.6, 1/125 s, ISO 200
(2020-04-22). Those embedded fields are retained as metadata from the supplied
files, not expanded into claims about the unrecorded photographs. The remaining
nine supplied files contain no usable capture EXIF, and no printing process is
asserted.

## Evidence limits

The titles in brackets are cataloguing aids for otherwise *Untitled* works; the
source record preserves the publication title. Numerical tonal and palette
values describe the normalized web renditions, not original prints, exhibition
files, or an intended colour grade.
