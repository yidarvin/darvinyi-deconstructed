# Research — Mert Alas & Marcus Piggott

## Scope of the record

The source set documents a 2002 pool-and-mirror photograph, a 2005 close-up titled *Lips*, *Kate Moss for Vogue Japan* (May 2011), *Smoke* (2011), and Vogue Paris covers from 2008 through 2016. Vogue France credits the photographs to Mert Alas & Marcus Piggott; the exhibition record identifies the 2016 exhibition *Mert & Marcus, Works 2001–2014*. These are public web-display renditions, not production files or print-object records. See [sources.md](sources.md).

## Technique and printing

The supplied records establish editorial and exhibition circulation, posed sitters, close crop, staged light, reflection, color, and cover typography visible in the images. They do not identify a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, file format, lighting package, retouching workflow, printer, paper, ink, edition, or print process for these particular works. The web JPEGs contain no capture EXIF after normalized ingest. This chapter therefore treats saturated color, controlled highlights, mirrored staging, and highly finished surfaces as visible properties of these reproductions, not as undocumented technical specifications.

## Working observation

Across the dated material, the frame is often built as a field for a face or figure: an extreme close crop; a body anchored by a couch, mirror, or chair; or a cover portrait whose masthead and sell lines become part of the picture's geometry. The selected reproductions support analysis of these visible decisions. They do not support claims about exact camera settings or printing technique.
