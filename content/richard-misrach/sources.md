# Richard Misrach — source shortlist

Richard Misrach's 1998 commission for the High Museum of Art's *Picturing the South* project made the industrial corridor between Baton Rouge and New Orleans—often called Cancer Alley—into a sustained lesson in seductive, disquieting landscape. This selection stays with that body of work, then adds a single *On the Beach* image to show how his measured distance can turn an apparently idyllic field into an anxious one. All twelve JPEGs were downloaded directly from the High Museum's publicly visible collection-image responses, open successfully, and have a 2400px long edge.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The High Museum collection records identify the photographs as © Richard Misrach (with the noted gallery credits where supplied); no open licence is asserted. Each item below is used as a credited, public, web-sized copy for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching. The analysis needs the complete frame: cropping would remove the relation between domestic/civic foreground, industrial horizon, and empty or inhabited space. The files link to the High Museum's authoritative record and do not substitute for an original print, a licensed reproduction, or the museum record. The individual four-factor assessments appear with each image.
