# Boris Mikhailov — research note

## Scope and sources

The sourced twelve-image sequence is credited to Boris Mikhailov through the
Kharkiv School of Photography (KSP) project pages, which provide the series
titles and date spans used in this chapter. KSP's artist overview places
Mikhailov within the Kharkiv School and documents the progression represented
here: *Red Series*, *Overlays*, *Luriki*, *Unfinished Dissertation*, *I Am Not
I*, *At Dusk*, *Case History*, *Tea Coffee Cappuccino*, *Industrial Zone*, and
*Temptation of Death*. MoMA describes *Case History* (1997–98) as an extended
project concerning people made homeless in post-Soviet Kharkiv; that context
matters for the chapter's discussion of direct address without resolving the
photographs into simple evidence. See [sources.md](sources.md) for the exact
records, credits, and fair-use rationales.

## Method and material evidence

The supplied records substantiate several visibly different working methods:
serial color emphasis in *Red Series* (1965–78); superimposition in *Overlays*
(1968–81); hand coloring in *Luriki* (1971–85); self-performance in *I Am Not
I* (1992); blue hand-toning in *At Dusk* (1993); and staged late images in
*Temptation of Death* (2017–19). They do not document a camera body, lens,
focal length, aperture, shutter speed, film stock, negative format, lighting
setup, or original printing process for these individual reproductions. The
normalized display files contain no capture EXIF. This chapter therefore reads
visible framing, sequence, color, tone, surface, and the measured overlay
geometry, and does not infer historical settings from web files.

One supplied file, `06-by-the-ground-01.jpg`, visibly depicts an installation
view with Mikhailov before a wall of photographs rather than the low street
view described in its source-shortlist note. The chapter identifies that
discrepancy and analyzes the supplied reproduction only for what it visibly
contains.
