# Sources — Rineke Dijkstra

## Acquisition note

All twelve files below are unaltered web-sized JPEG renditions obtained from the Art Institute of Chicago's public IIIF image service on 2026-07-25. Each opens as a JPEG and has a long edge of at least 843 px. The collection API identifies every work as by Rineke Dijkstra and marks it `is_public_domain: false`; its records do not supply a separate copyright notice. MoMA's collection record for *Odessa, Ukraine, August 4, 1993* identifies the work as © Rineke Dijkstra (2026), while the Art Institute records identify the artist and collection credit but no separate rightsholder. Credit each image as **Rineke Dijkstra; Art Institute of Chicago** (with the collection credit line shown below). The works are therefore used as **fair-use**, not as open-licensed files.

For every fair-use entry, the four-factor assessment is specific but follows the same facts: (1) this nonprofit, critical chapter transforms the work into composition teaching rather than a decorative display; (2) these are highly creative photographs, which weighs against use; (3) the complete frame is necessary to teach Dijkstra's relationship of posture, horizon, distance, and gaze, but only a 843-px web copy is used; and (4) the chapter credits and links to the collection record and does not replace the original, museum record, or a licensed reproduction. The selected images are publicly displayed by the holding museum; no authentication, paywall, access control, or technical restriction was bypassed.
