# Niceaunties: impossible worlds with ordinary visual rules

Wenhui Lim’s Niceaunties makes ageing Asian women the protagonists of an AI-led
digital world: they carry impossible harvests, walk through apartment cities,
submit to beauty rituals, and model bodies that reject fashion’s usual scale.
The trick is that each image gives its absurdity a familiar visual order. A
portrait uses a pyramid, a city uses an overpass and a corridor, a spa uses a
counter and blank wall, and a runway uses an axis. The overlays name those
large structures without pretending that generated texture is evidence of a
camera’s perspective. Titles, credits, source links, and the per-image fair-use
rationale are retained in the [source notes](../../content/niceaunties/sources.md).

## collectives become monuments

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="01-auntiesocial-0454" label="Auntiesocial, image 0454, 2023" caption="Three women and a cauliflower-broccoli crown form an unstable but emphatic portrait pyramid." />

The first *Auntiesocial* plate turns a group portrait into a monument. The
overlay follows the vegetable crown and the three-woman pyramid, while the
measured field break at y0.658 stops the sky from becoming empty background.
Pale blue `#d8d9ed`, greens `#465340` and `#1e281a`, and pink `#cc9f9b` span
p05 0.106 to p95 0.925: colour makes the vegetable mass feel both heavy and
theatrical.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="02-auntiesocial-0494" label="Auntiesocial, image 0494, 2023" caption="A sea horizon holds a three-person stage line, whose raised arms fan upward around a flower cluster." />

Here the calm sea horizon at y0.431 makes the sequined group read as a stage
line rather than an incidental beach scene. The two gesture paths rise away
from the flower apex, turning collective pose into a loose fan. Its high mean
luminance of 0.704 and pale `#dfe6f0` field allow the orange `#d58560` and
dark violet `#4e3b44` accents to announce performance without filling the
whole image.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="03-auntiesocial-0467" label="Auntiesocial, image 0467, 2023" caption="Staggered faces and flower mounds make a dense social field with no single vanishing point." />

The third plate refuses clean recession. Face clusters punctuate a low flower
band, and repeated white hair produces a crowd whose attention is distributed
rather than centred. The analyzer’s 0.245 edge density is the set’s busiest:
dark `#14150f`, brown `#443f2e`, and pale `#bbb0b7` keep the packed field
legible as texture and bodies, not as a falsely tidy perspective grid.

## cities and care are built as interiors

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="04-auntiecity-0005" label="The Auntie City, image 0005, 2023" caption="A giant walking auntie crosses a stacked city corridor divided by an overpass." />

In *The Auntie City*, the overpass at y0.495 cuts the frame into civic levels.
The measured convergence near x0.605, y0.361 belongs to the upper urban canyon;
the walking figure then breaks that order by linking it to the noodle-and-leg
interior below. With edge density 0.157, gray-blue `#212d32` and pale
`#e2d9d0` preserve architecture as a dense but readable container.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="05-auntiecity-0018" label="The Auntie City, image 0018, 2023" caption="Repeated yellow apartments form an all-over grid interrupted by faces, especially one lower glasses face." />

This city does not recede so much as repeat. Room tiers and the enclosing
apartment grid turn yellow `#e3cb48` against brown `#8b6935` into a modular
field, while the lower glasses face breaks the rhythm. Its p05–p95 range of
0.129–0.914 and edge density 0.163 explain why the image feels crowded without
losing its major units.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="06-auntiecity-0033" label="The Auntie City, image 0033, 2023" caption="Flat apartment tiers meet outsized legs, making pedestrian scale a lower-frame event." />

The city’s flat horizontal tiers do not need invented depth lines. They act as
stacked shelves above the oversized legs, with the street tier at y0.490 giving
pedestrians a believable but tiny baseline. Pale `#eceada`, green-gray
`#c1cbc0`, and dark `#3e3435` span p05 0.176 to p95 0.949, so the image can
hold bright sky, architecture, and the odd bodily support at once.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="07-spamenu-0195" label="Spa Menu: Non-Invasive, image 0195, 2023" caption="A sushi tray, cat, and seated auntie activate a large clinical void." />

*Spa Menu* exchanges urban density for a nearly empty room. The angled counter
break at y0.670, broad sushi-tray oval, and unequal cat/auntie pair make the
blank wall an active counterweight. Its low 0.024 edge density and restricted
palette of pale `#c6d4d3`, gray `#54575b`, and near-black `#271f21` makes the food’s
rounded foreground weight feel especially deliberate.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="08-spamenu-0197" label="Spa Menu, image 0197, 2023" caption="A white wig halo and food-covered face use near symmetry to make cosmetic display into a mask." />

The wig halo gives this plate its large, simple circle; the face axis at x0.500
lets the food decoration read as a frontal mask rather than a collection of
objects. The surrounding white field keeps that mask isolated. Bright `#e4e5e8`
and `#c4bfbd` dominate, while dark `#2c2423` supplies enough contrast for the
image’s p05–p95 range of 0.247–0.914.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="09-spamenu-0171" label="Spa Menu, image 0171, 2023" caption="A scanner’s circular opening frames a cat while bowl, patient, and clinician make care into a deadpan tableau." />

The scanner opening is an internal frame, not a vanishing point. Its circle
contains the cat, while the foreground bowl arc and right-hand clinician hold
care, food, and observation in one stage. The p05 0.055 to p95 0.827 tonal
spread allows cold blue-gray `#546261`, deep `#131f22`, and orange `#b25a3f`
to keep this clinical comedy spatially distinct.

## the runway makes bodies refuse fashion’s scale

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="10-fashion-0554" label="Fashion / Runway, image 0554, undated" caption="A central muscle-suit silhouette is held by a runway axis and abundant neutral space." />

The first runway image builds a deliberately simple display: a vertical axis at
x0.488, a floor seam at y0.785, and one overfull silhouette. The surrounding
gray field is genuinely sparse, with the set’s lowest edge density of 0.017, so muted
`#d2d2d3` and `#b9b8b8` leave the brown-red body and suit accents to carry the
joke of exaggerated strength.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="11-fashion-0565" label="Fashion / Runway, image 0565, undated" caption="Catwalk edges converge around a small model, while oversized legs make a foreground gate." />

This image does use a precise runway corridor: both edges meet near x0.513,
y0.433 and the central axis holds the small model. The massive foreground legs
then turn the ordinary catwalk into a gate. Its p05–p95 range of 0.090–0.937
is broad, but edge density stays only 0.024; the black audience wedges and
pale runway read as large opposing fields.

<Plate slug="niceaunties" id="12-fashion-0568" label="Fashion / Runway, image 0568, undated" caption="Two models stack in runway depth while repeated oversized legs make an unstable foreground lattice." />

The final plate makes depth theatrical rather than naturalistic. Runway edges
converge near x0.438, y0.338, stacking a front and rear model, but the repeated
legs deny a clean symmetrical finish. Pale cyan `#d2e4e5`, mauve `#9b7270`, and
deep `#281e21` work across p05 0.125 to p95 0.922; restrained 0.028 edge
density leaves posture and spacing more important than surface detail.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make scale do social work">
Photograph or stage two familiar things at radically different apparent sizes.
Keep one ordinary spatial rule, a horizon, shelf, corridor, or tabletop, clear
enough that the scale shift reads as a proposition rather than a mistake.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Give a crowd one governing shape">
Arrange several people, objects, or repeated portraits as a pyramid, fan, band,
or grid. Then remove any line or prop that does not strengthen the shared shape.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Let an ordinary set carry an impossible role">
Use a runway, treatment room, kitchen, or hallway. Make one element impossible,
but preserve the set’s practical axis, counter, doorway, or shelf so the image
can hold both plausibility and surprise.
</ExerciseCard>
