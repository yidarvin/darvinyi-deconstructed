# Niceaunties — research note

## Record and method

Niceaunties is the Singapore-based artist Wenhui Lim. The supplied source
record describes *Auntieverse* as an AI-led project; the artist describes her
broader practice as digital art, mixed media, and technology. That supports
describing these plates as AI-led digital images, but not naming a model,
prompting process, compositing workflow, or post-production method. The
selection joins 2023 *Auntiesocial*, *The Auntie City*, and *Spa Menu* images
with three undated *Runway* images. [Source notes](sources.md)

The source pages frame the projects as speculative social worlds: community and
sisterhood in *Auntiesocial*; mobile, Archigram-inspired civic life in *The
Auntie City*; beauty, skin, and the pressure of youth in *Spa Menu*; and a
runway where domestic objects and food become costume. These are the artist's
public project contexts, not evidence of a specific photographic capture.

## What is not documented

Neither the source record nor the ingested files establishes a camera, lens,
exposure, ISO, film stock, lighting plan, printer, paper, physical edition, or
print process. The files are credited public display renditions, not capture
masters; their direct-download dimensions document delivery size only. The
normalized files contain no usable camera EXIF. Accordingly, the chapter treats
tonal, palette, and geometry readings as measurements of these supplied web
renditions and does not infer photographic settings or a physical print history.
