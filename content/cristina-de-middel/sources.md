# Cristina de Middel — source shortlist

This source set centers on *The Afronauts* (2012), the project through which Cristina de Middel uses staged, handmade space-program imagery to test documentary photography's claim to factual authority. ICP describes the series as staged re-enactments of the unsuccessful 1960s Zambian space program; that is the chapter's central compositional and conceptual argument. Five full-frame works were retrieved from Candela Books + Gallery's public exhibition page, where their captions identify the artist, series, title, date, and print format. A sixth, *Jambo*, was retrieved from The Guardian's public interview page, which gives the work's title, series, date, and the credit “Photograph: Cristina De Middel.” All files opened successfully and retain the complete published image; the two 750px files exceed the project's normal 640px floor.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The photographs are copyrighted contemporary works by Cristina de Middel (Magnum Photos represents the photographer; the relevant public presentation credits de Middel and, where stated, Dillon Gallery). These are limited, transformative uses for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching—not a claim of open licensing. Each complete frame is needed to analyze the constructed props, body placement, artificial space, and documentary cues; every retained file is a public web display copy, credited and linked below, and does not replace an original print, gallery record, artist publication, or licensed reproduction.

## Research notes

ICP identifies *The Afronauts* as de Middel's acclaimed 2012 project about the failed Zambian space program, made through staged reenactments that challenge familiar depictions of Africa: https://www.icp.org/users/cristinademiddel. The artist's own account in The Guardian explains the project’s encounter with the historical Zambian program and confirms *Jambo* as a work from the series: https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2014/jun/11/my-best-shot-cristina-de-middel-zambia-space-programme. Candela's exhibition page also documents related bodies of work—*The Party*, *Poly Spam*, and *Antipodes*—but its public display renditions for those works are predominantly 600px-long-edge or smaller; this pass prioritizes six complete, legible, 750px-or-larger *Afronauts* images rather than treating an avoidable low-resolution rendition as a normal selection.
