Flóra Borsi’s *Animeyed* portraits suspend the choice between animal and human. An eye is repeated, an animal crosses a face, and makeup, hair, fur, feather, or colour closes the seam. The source record documents constructed self-portraits made through complementary makeup and lighting and joined in digital post-production. The overlays name only the visible organization: eye, contour, overlap, axis, and field, not a hidden technical recipe. [Source notes](../../content/flora-borsi/sources.md)

## a shared eye changes the portrait

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="01-animeyed-black-cat" label="*Animeyed*: [Black Cat]" caption="A cat's dark left-side mass interrupts a near-centered face, while repeated blue eyes hold the two halves in one gaze." />

In [Black Cat], the axis runs through the human face rather than perfectly bisecting the combined figure. The cat’s nearly black body supplies the left weight; pale skin and the open shoulder counter it on the right. The overlay contour follows that mass, and the human-eye anchor answers the cat’s blue eye. Mean luminance is 0.243, with #191717 through #c4bfb5 making the blue irises unusually exact.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="02-animeyed-white-swan" label="*Animeyed*: [White Swan]" caption="The swan's neck rises through a frontal portrait, then its beak cuts diagonally toward the face." />

The white swan changes a vertical portrait into a curve-and-diagonal arrangement. Its neck rises from the lower left, pauses at the head, and sends the beak across the face. With mean luminance 0.370, p95 0.851, and a grey-pink, charcoal, and pale-feather palette, contour rather than a hard background division does the separating.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="03-animeyed-black-swan" label="*Animeyed*: Black Swan" caption="A dark swan neck makes a heavy left counterweight, while the red beak crosses toward the red lips." />

*Black Swan* reverses the white-bird plate’s tonal logic. The animal’s vertical dark neck pins the left edge, while its red beak reaches toward the red mouth and eye accents. Its low 0.206 mean luminance and #090a0c / #212227 palette make that small red bridge decisive.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="04-animeyed-dalmatian" label="*Animeyed*: [Dalmatian]" caption="A dog head overlaps the face, and repeated black spots turn animal, skin, and body into one pattern field." />

The Dalmatian plate makes surface pattern carry the argument. The dog ellipse names the overlapping head and the lower line holds the continuous spotted field across neck and shoulder. A 0.574 mean luminance and wide 0.012–0.851 tonal range keep the black marks legible against the pale ground.

## contour becomes a collaborator

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="05-animeyed-puffin" label="*Animeyed*: [Puffin]" caption="The puffin's broad dark body occupies the left half, and its orange beak becomes a sharp bridge into the portrait." />

In [Puffin], a broad dark bird mass presses into an otherwise pale frontal portrait. The overlay separates that mass from the short orange-beak wedge, which points across to the human eye and lip colour. Low edge density (0.0095) and a 0.489 mean luminance leave the warm accent especially legible.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="06-animeyed-beetle" label="*Animeyed*: [Beetle]" caption="A tiny beetle and its surrounding face make scale and near-monochrome separation the entire composition." />

[Beetle] works by withholding contrast. The pale face field is nearly symmetrical, but the small beetle interrupts it without becoming a conventional central subject. Mean luminance rises to 0.669 while edge density falls to 0.0021, so freckles, insect, and eye carry a scene deliberately short on dramatic edges.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="07-animeyed-bunny" label="*Animeyed*: Bunny" caption="A small rabbit climbs into the portrait while two oversized ears make an airy, pink upper counterweight." />

In *Bunny*, the rabbit’s rise is answered by the enormous ears above the hairline. The overlay follows both contours rather than claiming false symmetry, since the animal is intentionally offset from the face. The high 0.802 mean luminance, 0.0030 edge density, and pink palette (#e7c8c7 to #b55c65) leave silhouette and matched eyes to carry depth.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="08-animeyed-poodle" label="*Animeyed*: [Poodle]" caption="White curls merge into a pale face at a narrow shadow seam, leaving the paired brown eyes to secure the image." />

[Poodle] makes fusion rather than collision its subject. The ellipse describes the broad curly texture and the line marks the seam where animal hair gives way to face. Mean luminance is 0.863 and edge density only 0.0019; the brown eyes and black nose are the few necessary anchors.

## colour closes the seam

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="09-animeyed-chameleon" label="*Animeyed*: Chameleon, 2016" caption="A chameleon crosses the left eye and sends a green tail in a long S-curve through the lower portrait." />

The chameleon plate gives the series its most continuous line. The lizard mass interrupts the left eye, then the tail descends, turns under the chin, and curls into the collarbone area. Palette samples #86ae7f, #629767, #36734b, and #104b2f unite body, hair, lipstick, and field; the 0.224–0.859 tonal span preserves both green body and pale skin.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="10-animeyed-flamingo" label="*Animeyed*: Flamingo, 2016" caption="A flamingo neck brackets the face and turns through the beak into the image's dark mouth." />

In *Flamingo*, the bird’s neck is an oversized internal frame. The bracket line follows its sweep and a short second line identifies the beak-to-mouth join, the image’s tonal hinge. Mean luminance is 0.605, with #da99a4, #c97884, #a55058, and #702d2f making that dark join decisive.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="11-animeyed-crow" label="*Animeyed*: [Crow], 2016" caption="A crow's horizontal body crosses the portrait as a dark mask in an otherwise grey, centered field." />

[Crow] is the series’ most severe lateral interruption. The body line runs from tail through head and the beak makes a small mask across the eye zone. The monochrome palette (#69686d, #545357, #0d0d12) and 0.350 mean luminance remove colour as a linking device, leaving the dark counterweight against the upright face.

<Plate slug="flora-borsi" id="12-animeyed-red-squirrel" label="*Animeyed*: [Red Squirrel], 2016" caption="A warm squirrel mass weights the left half while a shared gaze and brown field make the compound portrait cohere." />

The squirrel plate makes colour do what the black cat did through silhouette. The animal occupies a compact left mass, the face holds a measured central axis, and the two eyes establish the exchange. Mean luminance is 0.276, edge density 0.0639, and #402118, #201312, #ba916a, and #915632 unite fur, freckles, hair, and background.

## process and settings

The record identifies *Animeyed* as constructed digital self-portraiture and documents Borsi’s use of animal studies, complementary makeup and lighting, and post-production. It does not document camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, capture format, print process, or an individual-work retouching sequence. Some supplied JPEGs retain timestamps, but not enough provenance to treat them as original camera settings. Technical claims here therefore stop at documented method and visible tonal, palette, contour, and overlap evidence. [Research note](../../content/flora-borsi/research.md)
