# Flóra Borsi — research notes

The source record identifies this as Flóra Borsi’s ongoing *Animeyed* self-portrait series. The official project statement describes a dialogue between human and animal in which one eye is shared; it names makeup, costume, and digital post-production as parts of the method. A Savina Museum exhibition account adds that Borsi studies each animal’s colour and form, photographs herself with complementary makeup and lighting, and joins the images in post-production. These are documented grounds for describing the work as constructed digital portraiture rather than an unaltered encounter.

The twelve supplied official display files document works dated 2016 where the source record or embedded file data supplies that information; several individual display files have no supplied work date. The ingested JPEGs measure 1200–1600px on the long edge. Some retain `DateTimeOriginal` fields, but those fields do not identify a camera, lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, capture format, or original capture date. Neither the supplied sources nor the manifest establish a print process or a per-image lighting or retouching sequence. Those technical particulars are therefore not inferred here.

What can be observed in the delivered images is a highly controlled portrait grammar: centered or nearly centered faces, a single animal mass crossing one side of the image, matched eyes or colour, and a restricted field that makes the overlap legible. The analyzer records that range from near-white, low-edge images such as *Poodle* (mean luminance 0.863; edge density 0.0019) and *Bunny* (0.802; 0.0030) to the dark, higher-contrast *Black Cat* (0.243; 0.0628) and *Red Squirrel* (0.276; 0.0639). Green, pink, grey, black, and warm brown palettes operate as visible compositional evidence in these web reproductions, not as claims about original prints. [Source notes](sources.md)
