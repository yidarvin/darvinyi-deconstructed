# Elizaveta Porodina — source record

## Selection and rights note

This twelve-image set samples the visual vocabulary of Elizaveta Porodina’s
public portfolio: warped reflection, coloured-light portraiture, shallow staged
space, and an occasional shift into location photography. The files were
retrieved directly from the image routes used by her [official portfolio](https://porodina.com/), whose project index identifies the corresponding editorial or commission. The site's footer bears `©2021` and supplies no reuse licence. The photographs are therefore not open content. Each is retained only as a **fair-use** web copy for this book's criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching.

The portfolio identifies Porodina as the artist and the named editorial or client, but does not provide individual work titles, creation dates, or a separate rightsholder notice. The dates below are consequently recorded as **n.d.** rather than guessed. Credit is Elizaveta Porodina, with the project/editorial named in each entry; associated editorial and client rights may also apply. Each separate assessment applies all four factors: (1) this is nonprofit, transformative visual criticism and scholarship; (2) the underlying fashion photograph is highly creative, weighing against use; (3) the complete frame is necessary because its crop, optical treatment, colour field, and figure-to-setting relationship are the lesson; and (4) the limited, credited, linked web copy does not replace the portfolio, an original, a magazine, or a licensed reproduction.
