# Elizaveta Porodina — research note

## What the supplied record establishes

The supplied official-portfolio record places this selection in late-2010s and
early-2020s international fashion editorial and commission photography. Its
project labels include *Dazed — Chanel Beauty*, *Vogue Russia Creativity Issue
2021*, *Carolina Herrera × Vanity Fair US*, *Vogue China*, *Vogue US*, *Numéro
Netherlands*, and *The Cut*. The portfolio classifies the practice with fashion,
art, fine-art, surreal, editorial, and campaign keywords. Individual work
titles and dates are supplied only where the project page supplies them; see
[sources.md](sources.md) for each credited display file and its fair-use basis.

## Technique, era, and print information

Across the twelve finished frames, warped or reflective portrait surfaces,
coloured-light and colour-field staging, haze or soft focus, shallow space, and
graphic silhouette are visible recurring effects. They are observations of the
completed images, not evidence of a particular behind-the-scenes process. The
available source record does not establish whether distortion, blur, reflection,
or colour treatment was made with lens, set material, lighting, retouching, or
another method.

The source files are public portfolio JPEGs, originally 1,851–3,500px on their
long edges and ingested here at 1,600px. All twelve manifest entries have empty
EXIF objects. Apart from the 2021 date carried in one project title, the record
does not provide individual creation dates, camera body, lens, focal length,
aperture, shutter speed, ISO, capture medium, lighting plan, colour-grading
workflow, original print process, paper, print dimensions, edition, or
exhibition specification. The portfolio footer bears `©2021` and supplies no
reuse licence. This chapter therefore treats the overlays and tonal measurements
as evidence about credited web renditions, not undocumented production settings.
