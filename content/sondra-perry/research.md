# Sondra Perry — research note

## Scope and record

The sourced teaching set spans 2016–2020. It is made from public archive views
of installations, videos, workstations, light boxes, a lenticular installation,
and a wall-mounted print, rather than from a single camera-made photographic
series. The Bridget Donahue archive supplies the work titles, dates, and
installation descriptions; its public display files are the chapter’s primary
visual evidence. MoMA’s artist record corroborates several works, including
*Wet and Wavy Looks—Typhon coming on for a Three Monitor Workstation* and
*Graft and Ash for a Three Monitor Workstation*. Mudam’s collection text
describes *IT’S IN THE GAME*’s use of family photographs, a basketball avatar,
and chroma-key blue in relation to authorship, representation, and cultural
bias. See [source notes](sources.md).

## Media and display

- *flesh, graft, and ash #1* is documented as a light-box work; the three
  illuminated panels and their spacing are therefore part of the work’s visible
  structure, not a photographic triptych inferred from the documentation.
- *Ocean Modifer* is documented as a lenticular installation. Its long,
  horizontal display is treated here as a distributed visual field; this chapter
  does not claim a single fixed lenticular view from a still documentation
  image.
- *Typhoon coming on*, *IT’S IN THE GAME ’17 — Full Installation*, *Eclogue for
  [in]HABITABILITY*, and the bicycle/rowing-machine works are installation or
  workstation views. The gallery walls, floors, projectors, screens, and
  apparatus visible in the supplied files are compositional material.
- *Acrylic Gel Full Set and Post Holy Land Experience Target Photo Studio Print*
  is identified as a print, but the supplied archive record does not establish
  a fuller print-process recipe. The chapter therefore describes its mounted
  frame, portrait cluster, and surrounding wall, not an unverified printing
  technique.

## Settings and limits

The ingested copies contain no capture EXIF. The cited archive and collection
records do not provide a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter
speed, ISO, film stock, exposure method, lighting setup, projection settings,
or display calibration for these particular documentation images. No such
settings are inferred. Tonal statistics and palettes in the chapter are
deterministic measurements of the supplied web JPEGs, not claims about a
work’s original display brightness or colorimetry.

Two files are recorded source-policy exceptions: the 401 × 600px print view and
the 400 × 600px *Graft and Ash* view. Their complete frames remain legible for
the documented compositional purpose; neither is upscaled.
