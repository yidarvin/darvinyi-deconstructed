# Kate Steciw — research note

## Scope and sources

This chapter follows twelve 2010–24 works supplied as public display images from [Kate Steciw’s portfolio](https://katesteciw.me/) and the International Center of Photography’s [*What Is a Photograph?* exhibition record](https://www.icp.org/exhibitions/what-is-a-photograph). The portfolio supplies the titles, dates, and media for eleven selections; ICP identifies the 2013 *Armchair, Background, Basic, Beauty, Bed, Bedside, Bread, Breakfast, Bright, Cereal, Closeup, Cloth, Color, Contemporary, Couch, Crust, Day, Decor, Fox, Frame, Grain, Ingredient, Interior, Invitation, Irregular, Juice, Life, Living, Loaf, Luxury, Macro, Sofa, Speed, Style, Sweet, Texture* installation and credits Kate Steciw and installation photographer Mark Woods. Source routes, rights rationales, and file details are retained in [sources.md](sources.md).

Gallery and press material locates Steciw’s practice in the physical afterlife of circulating digital pictures. [Higher Pictures](https://higherpictures.com/press/kate-steciw/) identifies stock imagery, cutting, layering, building, and tinted-Plexiglas interventions as recurrent concerns. [Galerie Christophe Gaillard](https://galeriegaillard.com/en/artists/8232-kate-steciw/overview/) describes the *Construction* works as rendered from immaterial digital images through cut-outs, overlays, and physical extensions. Those accounts support a reading of visible seams, supports, hardware, and collage as compositional structure; they do not establish a single workflow for every work here.

## Technique and era

The sequence spans an early image-object view from 2010, 2015 *Construction* works, and recent canvas collages from 2023–24. Across it, photographic material is not treated as a transparent window. It is printed, folded, cut, layered, suspended where the view shows suspension, framed, and shown with the room around it. The result is a recurring oscillation between the visible source image and the literal object that carries it.

The records identify the 2015 *Construction* works as dye sublimation on aluminum or satin, with chain and carabiners where listed. They identify the 2023–24 collages as inkjet prints on canvas, collage on panel. Those are recorded media descriptions, not complete production recipes. The chapter therefore describes visible cut edges, overlaps, folds, frames, and installation relations rather than claiming undocumented software steps.

## Print and camera record

No consulted source gives camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting setup, capture date, or the original source-file settings for these works. The ingested web-display JPEGs contain no usable EXIF. The deterministic tonal, palette, and geometry values cited in the chapter characterize those supplied web renditions rather than the original prints, objects, or installations.
