Kate Steciw makes a digital photograph answer to gravity, support, and interruption. The twelve works here begin with an image lying in a room, then move through chains, cut aluminum or satin, framed collage, and a three-part installation. They do not ask us to forget the source picture; they make its physical carrier, edge, fold, frame, seam, or hardware, part of the composition. The work titles, media, credits, and fair-use rationale are documented in the [source notes](../../content/kate-steciw/sources.md).

## a picture can become an object

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="01-the-strangeness-of-this-idea" label="The Strangeness of This Idea, 2010" caption="A low viewpoint turns a flower photograph into a receding, materially present panel." />

The overlay follows the panel’s right edge and its apparent recession, rather than inventing a floral perspective inside the print. The flower field holds attention within the room’s blank field. Mean luminance 0.847 and the pale #f2f3f2 ground give the red #bc5b61 blossoms and blue-gray #50606a foliage a concentrated, object-like presence; the low 0.0067 edge density makes the panel outline do more work than detail.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="02-construction-433" label="Construction 433, 2015" caption="Chain, counterweight, and floor make a hanging image-object read as a vertical force." />

*Construction 433* turns its suspension into the primary route through the frame. The overlay names the chain and counterweight, while the angled floor/baseboard relation gives that vertical pull a physical stop. At mean luminance 0.811 and edge density 0.0111, the white gallery field leaves the dark hardware and small colored fragments unusually exposed.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="03-construction-1029" label="Construction 1029, 2015" caption="A floor-bound folded satin form makes contour and bend more decisive than a flat image field." />

The red textile arc, fold convergence, and outer bend show how the work accumulates volume without becoming a conventional sculpture photograph. Blue, red, violet, and patterned passages travel over a continuous soft form. Its p05–p95 span of 0.239–0.847 and 0.0195 edge density keep the colored surface active against the room’s gray-white surround.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="04-construction-143" label="Construction 143, 2015" caption="Rope, chain, and a small orange fragment keep a suspended construction from resolving as a single silhouette." />

The wall-floor seam and hanging center establish a calm coordinate system; the orange cutout at x0.469, y0.430 disrupts it. The sparse display rendition has mean luminance 0.820 and edge density 0.0088, with #ebebeb and #c1baaf around small near-black and rust accents. That distribution makes hardware and interruption legible at once.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="05-construction-034" label="Construction 034, 2015" caption="A narrow chain axis holds an irregular cutout cluster inside a large unoccupied gallery field." />

The overlay’s suspension path and upper cluster identify a precarious vertical stack rather than a stable figure. The image is almost entirely gallery light, with mean luminance 0.832 and only 0.0133 edge density; black curves, muted flesh tones, and the chain therefore become a compact counterweight to the empty room.

## collage turns depth into a dispute

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="06-monster-mash-up" label="Monster Mash-up, 2023" caption="Curved cutouts collide through the center of a framed panel rather than building one coherent space." />

The upper frame edge, panel center, and dark lower-left cutout are enough to establish the panel’s competing directions. Steciw makes curved blue, red, green, and black forms overlap without granting any one of them a stable foreground. The rendition’s broad p05–p95 range of 0.118–0.941 and 0.0328 edge density support that pressure: bright #e4e5e5, dark #312c35, red #925a52, and blue #629ac4 arrive as separate events.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="07-dwelling-dialogue" label="Dwelling dialogue, 2023" caption="Folds and cut contours convert a crowded panel into a shallow field of obstruction." />

The panel center gives a provisional order, but the left blue fold at x0.283, y0.602 redirects the eye into the overlapping layers. The lower frame edge refuses an imagined continuous space beyond the collage. With p05 0.078, p95 0.953, and edge density 0.0235, light #eaebea, near-black #1b1d29, ochre, and blue keep the dense overlap readable as a succession of surfaces.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="08-clean-machine" label="Clean Machine, 2024" caption="An upper frame edge and lower blue base make the framed collage feel both contained and unstable." />

The frame does not restore symmetry: the lower blue base and cut seams keep pushing it aside. An exceptionally high 0.785 mean luminance and pale #f2f2f2 surround make the dark #3a383d, blue-gray #575b69, and rust #af5e5a fragments feel inserted rather than absorbed. At 0.0109 edge density, color boundaries carry most of the structure.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="09-img-0383-heicxpict0711" label="IMG_0383.HEICXPICT0711.JPG, 2023" caption="A cut tabletop edge and repeated bottle forms make a still life visibly behave like a layered file." />

The overlay treats the panel’s upper edge, center, and slanting tabletop as separate claims: the still life is not one seamless scene. Bottle silhouettes repeat at incompatible scales and overlap sharp inserted rectangles. The high-contrast rendition spans p05 0.118 to p95 1.000, while #f8f9f9, #272223, and orange #e18020 sharpen the sense of fragments colliding.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="10-n192914785151-template015" label="N192914785151_Template015_111295017_10_18_2019_08_55_47_EN, 2023" caption="A looping cut collage sweep crosses reflective objects and prevents their photographed setting from settling." />

The left glass form and diagonal collage sweep make the title’s file-like residue visually credible: a reflective image is visibly edited into another construction. The central axis is only a hinge for that motion, not a symmetry claim. Mean luminance 0.579, p05–p95 0.165–0.922, and edge density 0.0277 leave #35353f, #b7b27e, #8b594d, and #697497 as distinct bands and cuts.

## serial images keep the room in the frame

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="11-adjustment-exercise-0004-pdf-1-3" label="Adjustment_Exercise_0004.pdf 1–3, 2024" caption="Three framed variations make comparison across panels the composition’s central operation." />

The overlay boxes the left and middle panels, marks their common top line, and locates the curl in the right panel. That restraint preserves the real interval between objects: serial variation is the subject, not a fictitious seamless panorama. The bright installation image has mean luminance 0.865 and only 0.0158 edge density; pale #f0f0f0 and #e2e2e4 give the mauve and gray forms room to differentiate themselves.

<Plate slug="kate-steciw" id="12-armchair-background-basic-beauty" label="Armchair, Background, Basic, Beauty, Bed, Bedside, Bread, Breakfast, Bright, Cereal, Closeup, Cloth, Color, Contemporary, Couch, Crust, Day, Decor, Fox, Frame, Grain, Ingredient, Interior, Invitation, Irregular, Juice, Life, Living, Loaf, Luxury, Macro, Sofa, Speed, Style, Sweet, Texture, 2013" caption="An unequal pair of framed panels makes the gap and cutout shapes into an active seam." />

The left panel frame, middle gap, right-panel cutout, and lower installation edge establish a relation rather than force the pair into symmetry. Its p05–p95 span of 0.055–0.988 and the set’s highest edge density, 0.0398, make black, cream, red, striped, and yellow fragments press against the white wall as separate objects. The ICP installation view matters because the spacing outside the frames is part of the work’s logic.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make support visible">
Photograph a print, screen, card, or small object from an angle that reveals its edge and the surface it rests on. Keep enough empty surrounding space that the support changes the reading of the image.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Interrupt a coherent still life">
Start with a familiar tabletop arrangement, then introduce one cut, fold, hard mask, or shifted duplicate. Let the intervention cross a real depth cue so that it changes how the rest of the photograph is read.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Make a series from small adjustments">
Create three versions of one image or object with one change per version: crop, overlap, material, or framing. Hang or place them with enough interval that the spaces between versions become part of the composition.
</ExerciseCard>
