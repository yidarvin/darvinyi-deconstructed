# Campbell Addy — source shortlist

This 12-image set is drawn from the high-resolution display files published in Addy’s own public *Selected Works* portfolio. It balances recent editorial portraiture with the artist’s 2023 *I ♥ Campbell* exhibition photographs, where the saturated studio field, tactile styling, and frontal address are especially clear. The source page serves the complete published frames as public web images; every download opened successfully and exceeds the 640px long-edge floor.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The works are contemporary copyrighted photographs, credited on Addy’s portfolio to Campbell Addy (publication and commissioned-work rights may also apply where indicated by the file name). No open licence or public-domain dedication is stated. Each entry below makes a limited, transformative use for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; it needs the whole published frame to analyze color, pose, gesture, lighting, and spatial construction; it retains only a web-sized copy; and it credits and links to the artist’s portfolio rather than replacing an original, publication, exhibition, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Addy’s biography identifies *Feeling Seen: The Photographs of Campbell Addy* (2022) as his first monograph and identifies the 2023 180 Studios solo exhibition *I ♥ Campbell* as a body of newly conceived images reflecting on his roots and inspirations: https://campbelladdy.com/about. The exhibition catalogue says the show comprised 36 original pieces across photography, painting, film, and spatial design: https://campbelladdy.com/s/I-3-Campbell-3.pdf. The selected images are therefore a focused teaching set, not a claim to reproduce the book or exhibition catalogue.
