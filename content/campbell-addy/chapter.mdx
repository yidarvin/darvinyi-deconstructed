Campbell Addy's photographs make the fashioned body inseparable from the field that holds it. Across these editorial portraits, cover images, and works connected to *I ♥ Campbell*, a branch, a reflective sphere, a masthead, a black void, or a tree is never incidental scenery. Each becomes a piece of graphic pressure. The overlays follow visible hinges: the held branch, silver curve, enclosing trunk, cover edge, and facial axis, rather than treating every machine-detected split as a horizon. ## bodies given a stage

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="01-colman-domingo-time-2025" label="Colman Domingo for Time, 2025" caption="A held branch rises from a curled body, turning the open studio field into a suspension." />

In the Colman Domingo portrait, the branch gives the pose its improbable upward pull while the torso, knees, and raised shoes make a counter-arc across the lower frame. The overlay follows the literal branch and the reclining body rather than treating the low studio boundary as a landscape horizon. This is a spare grayscale construction: mean luminance is 0.289, p95 is only 0.580, and edge density 0.0203, leaving the bright shirt and white socks to activate the textured gray surround.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="02-lewis-hamilton-gq-2023" label="Lewis Hamilton for GQ, 2023" caption="A walking figure is caught between a silver curve and a black ground wedge." />

Lewis Hamilton is not centered as a traditional portrait subject. He walks at the right edge of a tall field while the silver form rises, bends, and disappears behind him; the black wedge at left keeps that curve from becoming merely decorative. The overlay names those two material shapes and the figure's head. A broad tonal span from p05 0.114 to p95 0.843, but a very low 0.0106 edge density, explains the image's almost cut-paper clarity: most of its force comes from large pale, black, green, and gray zones.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="03-vogue-spain-hand-print" label="Vogue Spain, Hand Print, undated" caption="Four faces and the hands between them make one compact, interlocking portrait block." />

The Vogue Spain group refuses a simple row. Faces arrive at four different angles while hands and shoulders lock the bodies into a dense, low cluster. The large overlay ellipse is intentionally collective; the two paths identify the upper face rhythm and the joining gestures, not a false single subject. Its dark blue field and patterned garments yield an edge density of 0.0596, while the palette runs from blue #2c4760 through near-black #27272a to warm skin and textile tones #c4b8ae and #a18979.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="04-vanishing-evenings-vogue-us-2024" label="Vanishing Evenings for Vogue US, 2024" caption="A standing figure, distant shore, and reflection make one vertical axis inside an expansive beach field." />

Here the figure is small enough that the picture first reads as air, water, and a muted horizontal shore. The measured vertical symmetry at x 0.487 becomes meaningful only because the body and its reflection actually occupy it; the overlay also marks the distant shore at y 0.509. With mean luminance 0.665, p95 0.812, and edge density just 0.0083, the high-key beach permits the patterned top and dark face to carry the concentrated local detail.

## fashion as a set of graphic pressures

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="05-harpers-bazaar-cover-story-2021" label="Harper’s Bazaar cover story, frame 008, 2021" caption="A crawling body presses across a sandy stage beneath a dark ridge." />

This compact horizontal frame turns sand into a stage floor and the crawling body into a broad diagonal that crosses it. The overlay follows the limbs and the distant ridge, not the analyzer's tempting but semantically unhelpful convergence near the subject's hair. Dark brown #452a13 and black-brown #171008 meet sand tones #b48347 and #986838; mean luminance is 0.296 and edge density 0.0404, enough texture for the sand to resist becoming a flat backdrop.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="06-liya-kebede-vogue-italia-2023" label="Liya Kebede for Vogue Italia, 2023" caption="A red figure bridges two reflective spheres while the masthead claims the upper field." />

The Vogue Italia cover uses typography as part of the frame rather than as a label pasted on afterward. Liya Kebede's sleeves traverse two reflective spheres, and the ribbed dress drops as a long red counterweight below the face. The overlay stays with those literal relations. It is the darkest image in this selection by mean luminance, 0.197, while its 0.0905 edge density belongs to ribbing, letterforms, and reflections; the palette concentrates deep red #6c141c, #511017, and #9a1e22 around near-black #1e0a0b.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="07-i-heart-campbell-21-2023" label="I ♥ Campbell, exhibition work 21, 2023" caption="A frontal body holds a centered axis while a rising hair silhouette breaks it asymmetrically." />

The pose is frontal, but the image does not settle into a mirror. The face and torso sit on the measured axis at x 0.487 while the hair rises in a long, uneven diagonal and the shoulders open outward below. The nearly white surround makes the black body feel emblematic rather than enclosed. Its tonal range is the widest here, p05 0.024 to p95 0.925, with only 0.0106 edge density, a combination that gives silhouette and specular skin highlights unusual authority.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="08-bukayo-saka-time-2023" label="Bukayo Saka for Time Next Generation Leaders, 2023" caption="A direct gaze is held inside the cover’s bounded image field and active typography." />

The Time cover makes face, shirt, red border, masthead, and blue name work as one designed address. The overlay marks the literal image field and the head rather than turning the cover's outer border into a compositional crop. The background and white jersey produce p95 0.973 and mean luminance 0.619, while blue and red type interrupt a mostly gray palette. The modest 0.0305 edge density is concentrated in hair, eyes, jersey texture, and the cover's lettering.

## portrait, enclosure, and accumulation

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="09-i-heart-campbell-handprint-2023" label="I ♥ Campbell, hand-print work, 2023" caption="A dim chain of bodies occupies the left before the frame releases into black space." />

In the panoramic hand-print work, the light does not reveal a complete scene. It travels across a cluster of bodies and then gives way to a large black right-hand field. The ellipse and two paths deliberately stay broad: they describe the collective chain and reclining sweep without pretending to separate every body. Mean luminance is only 0.027, p95 0.153, yet edge density reaches 0.1254, showing how the few lit limbs and draped contours carry a surprisingly active structure.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="10-i-heart-campbell-06-2023" label="I ♥ Campbell, exhibition work 06, 2023" caption="Five painted faces form a low portrait chorus under a drawn oval and notes." />

The five heads make a deliberately uneven horizontal rhythm; stripes of paint and the surrounding handwritten editorial marks make the image read as both portrait and working surface. The overlay keeps its ellipse on the heads and follows the visible drawn oval rather than asserting a geometric symmetry that the different faces do not support. The field is bright, mean luminance 0.789 and p95 0.961, but the faces and handwriting retain a deep palette from #271f1f through brown and violet accents.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="11-i-heart-campbell-04-2023" label="I ♥ Campbell, exhibition work 04, 2023" caption="A trunk and leaf canopy form a literal enclosure around two figures held in darkness." />

The tree here is a structural device, not atmosphere. Its trunk makes a long vertical beside the figures, the leaf canopy closes above them, and the mossed ground gives the pair a small lit base inside a wide black field. The overlay's frame names that enclosure and its ellipse keeps the two figures together. This is another nearly nocturnal construction: mean luminance 0.126, p05 0.027, p95 0.486, and edge density 0.0944 concentrated in leaves, bark, arms, and the bright contours of skin.

<Plate slug="campbell-addy" id="12-meghan-the-cut-2018" label="Meghan for The Cut, 2018" caption="A near-frontal face and the cover’s type bands enforce a concentrated central axis." />

The Cut portrait demonstrates how a magazine cover can intensify rather than dilute a frontal study. The centered parting and face align precisely with the measured symmetry axis at x 0.500; the masthead above and coverline band below make the portrait's vertical compression explicit. The overlay marks those visible bands. The image moves from p05 0.020 to p95 1.000 with very low 0.0165 edge density: the eye is asked to stay with skin, hair, earrings, and the large letterforms rather than a busy setting.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Give a figure one structural partner">
Photograph a person with one object, branch, reflective form, lettered surface, or architectural edge that changes the body's direction. Keep only frames in which the object is doing compositional work, not simply decorating the portrait.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Let a field do most of the work">
Make a portrait in which more than half the frame is one relatively quiet tone or colour. Use a small number of sharp edges, highlights, or text elements to decide where attention settles.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Treat cover language as structure">
Work with a sign, title, page edge, or graphic band. Frame the person so that the text and image create a shared hierarchy; do not add words after the composition is finished.
</ExerciseCard>
