# Vik Muniz — research note

## Working method and chronology

The supplied sources document a practice in which Vik Muniz makes temporary drawings,
arrangements, or reconstructions from materials that remain visible in the final
photograph. This set spans *Wind* (1995), *The Sugar Children* (1996), *Pictures of
Chocolate* and *Pictures of Junk* (1997), *Pictures of Thread* (1999), and *Pictures
of Garbage* (2008). The series chronology and object-specific descriptions are in
[sources.md](sources.md), primarily through Art Institute of Chicago records and the
artist's gallery chronology.

Documented materials here include wire in *Wind*; sugar in *Jacinthe Loves Orange
Juice*; eggs in *11 Eggs*; chocolate syrup in *Double Elvis*; nails in *Pillow II
(after Dürer)*; thread in *11,000 Yards, The Helmingham Dell, after Constable*;
spaghetti and sauce in *Medusa Marinara*; and refuse in the two *Pictures of
Garbage* plates. The source describes *Self-Portrait* as an assembled mark field but
does not identify its material more specifically. These are photographs about the
relation between an arrangement at close range and a recognizable image at viewing
distance, not direct documentary photographs of their apparent subjects.

## Technical limits

The supplied records establish titles, dates, materials/processes where listed, and
photographic presentation. They do **not** establish camera body, lens, focal length,
aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, film or sensor format, lighting setup, print
process, paper, edition, or print dimensions. The ingested display JPEGs contain no
EXIF data. This chapter therefore makes no camera-setting or printing claim beyond
the documented temporary-material construction and photographic result.
