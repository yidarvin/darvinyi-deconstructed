# Aïda Muluneh — source shortlist

This selection puts the 2018 *Water Life* commission at the chapter’s center: Muluneh’s artist archive says the series addresses water access, women’s liberation, health, sanitation, and education, and locates part of its production in Dallol, Afar, Ethiopia. Four works from the canonical 2016 *The World is 9* series establish the wider visual language behind that argument—painted bodies, saturated primaries, symbolic props, and frontal tableau. The artist’s public pages identify every selected title and date and expose the complete display frame. All ten downloaded files opened successfully and exceed the 640px long-edge floor.

## Research context

Muluneh’s *Water Life* statement explains that the series advocates around water access, particularly its burdens on women in rural regions, and identifies WaterAid and the H&M Foundation as commission/support partners: https://aidamuluneh.com/water-life/. The artist’s *The World is 9* statement describes its work with African body art, time, space, life, love, and history: https://aidamuluneh.com/the-world-is-9/. The artist biography identifies Muluneh as an Addis Ababa-born photographer and visual artist: https://aidamuluneh.com/about/.
