# David Alekhuogie — source shortlist

This set begins with two dated, publicly displayed High Museum collection works, then follows the artist's own public portfolio through the *Constructions* and *Negative_Positive* groups. The portfolio does not supply individual work dates for those numbered images, so they are retained as undated rather than assigned an unsupported date (the pages were publicly posted in 2017 and 2018 respectively). All twelve files were fetched as publicly available JPEG display responses, opened successfully, and retain their complete published frames.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. David Alekhuogie's work is copyrighted; the High Museum records explicitly state “© David Alekhuogie,” and the official portfolio is credited to David O. Alekhuogie. Each use is limited and transformative: this nonprofit book uses the complete, credited frame for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching about linked panels, cropped bodies, architectural fragments, material seams, and negative space. The full published frame is necessary for those spatial relations; the copy is a public web rendition, links to and credits the collection record or artist portfolio, and does not replace an original, a print, the museum record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

The High Museum identifies *Birth Home* and *Ebenezer* as 2013 works by David Alekhuogie and supplies their collection credits and copyright notice. The artist's official portfolio supplies the *Constructions* and *Negative_Positive* sequences directly. Yancey Richardson Gallery's artist page places Alekhuogie in MoMA's 2020 *Companion Pieces* and describes his use of reprise, repetition, and images remixed into multilayered photographic collages: https://www.yanceyrichardson.com/artists/david-alekhuogie/featured-workds?view=thumbnails.
