# Tyler Mitchell — research note

## Scope and sources

The source dossier documents twelve named, dated works displayed by Jack Shainman Gallery for *Tyler Mitchell: I Can Make You Feel Good | Dreaming in Real Time* (2021), including one 2019 work and works dated 2020–21. The gallery’s public JPEGs are the image sources used in this chapter. ICP’s exhibition text is the contextual source for Mitchell’s stated concern with Black freedom, affirmation, intimacy, pleasure, and possibility. See [sources.md](sources.md) for the work-by-work links, credit, and fair-use analysis.

## Process and technique

The sources establish titles, dates, gallery display, and the fact that these are contemporary works by Tyler Mitchell. The selection visibly moves between staged portraiture, fashion-inflected dress, domestic or outdoor settings, close portraiture, and collective scenes; those are descriptions of the pictures, not claims about an undocumented production workflow.

The consulted records do **not** establish camera body, lens, focal length, film or sensor, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting setup, negative, post-production, paper, ink, print dimensions, edition, or printing process for these individual works. The ingested display JPEGs contain no retained EXIF settings. This chapter therefore makes no camera-setting or print-process claims.

## Reading the analyzed files

The analyzer finds a broad tonal range across the selection: the very open, pale *Dragonfly* file has mean luminance 0.842 and edge density 0.002, while the shaded group scene has mean luminance 0.168 and edge density 0.189. That measured difference supports an observation about pictorial structure, not a claim about exposure or printing. Blue-gray, grass green, muted earth, red, and near-black recur in the measured palettes; they are used in the chapter only as visible color relationships.
