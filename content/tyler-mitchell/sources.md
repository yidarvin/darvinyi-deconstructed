# Tyler Mitchell — source shortlist

This chapter uses twelve named works from Jack Shainman Gallery’s public exhibition record for *Tyler Mitchell: I Can Make You Feel Good | Dreaming in Real Time* (2021). The gallery serves the complete images as public 1600px JPEG display renditions, with the title, medium, and date in the corresponding work record. Each downloaded file was opened successfully; its long edge is 1600px. The International Center of Photography’s exhibition text supplies the broader context: Mitchell describes an image-world of Black freedom, intimacy, pleasure, and possibility, made through documentary, portrait, fashion, and art-photographic methods.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. These are contemporary copyrighted photographs by Tyler Mitchell, not open-licensed images. The source record does not supply a different rightsholder, so the credit is **Tyler Mitchell; copyright notice: not stated on the gallery work page**. The assessments below are individual and limited: this nonprofit book uses a credited, linked, web-sized complete frame for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. It neither substitutes for the original archival-pigment print/wall work, the gallery record, nor licensed reproduction.
