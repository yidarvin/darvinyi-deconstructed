# Deana Lawson — source shortlist

This set uses twelve web-sized JPEG renditions publicly displayed by the artist's
representing gallery, [David Kordansky Gallery's Deana Lawson featured-works
page](https://www.davidkordanskygallery.com/artist/deana-lawson/featured-works?view=slider).
The page identifies the artist, titles, dates, and media; its direct image responses
were downloaded and opened as JPEGs. Every file clears the 640px long-edge floor
(the smallest is 1,800px). The gallery's biographical text describes Lawson's
meticulously staged portraits and the relation between figures and domestic space;
the selection follows that compositional through-line from 2007 to 2023.

All twelve selections are **fair-use**, not open works. The source page publicly
displays the photographs but does not grant an open licence, and its captions credit
Deana Lawson. Each entry therefore preserves the artist credit and makes a separate
four-factor assessment: (1) the book uses this web-sized copy for transformative
criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; (2) the underlying work is a
creative authored photograph (or, for *Torus*, a creative hologram), which weighs
against use; (3) the complete displayed frame is necessary to analyze the specific
spatial relations named below; and (4) the credited, linked web rendition does not
substitute for the artist's original, a gallery sale, or a licensed reproduction.

lesson in frontal pairing and the way high-key space can isolate a domestic portrait.

**Use basis: fair-use.** Credit: Deana Lawson; 2023 pigment print. Factor 1: transformative analysis of the paired figures, patterned surfaces, and isolating field. Factor 2: creative authored photograph. Factor 3: the whole frame is necessary to keep the figures' scale and surrounding white space in relation.
