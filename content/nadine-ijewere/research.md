# Nadine Ijewere — research note

## What the supplied sources establish

The supplied record identifies this twelve-image selection as Nadine Ijewere photographs made between 2019 and 2023 in Senegal, Portland, Jamaica, and the Dominican Republic. It identifies each public display rendition by title, date, credit, and copyright notice, and records that the artist's official portfolio carries a © 2020 Nadine Ijewere, all-rights-reserved notice. The record supports discussion of fashion portraiture, color, posing, water, landscape, plant forms, and group arrangements visible in the complete frames. [Source notes](sources.md)

The supplied sources do not identify a camera body, lens, film or sensor format, aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, lighting setup, printing process, paper, edition, retouching workflow, or color-management process for these individual photographs. The web-display JPEGs and their retained EXIF do not establish those facts. This chapter therefore treats the images as credited web-display derivatives and makes its technical claims only about visible composition and the analyzer's measured tonal and palette results.

## Method for this chapter

The chapter uses the full frames where the source record says their relationships of figure, setting, water, and color are necessary to the teaching purpose. Each overlay is limited to visible structures—boat edges, bodies, palms, portraits, rails, fences, and printed surfaces—and its geometry is independently measured against the ingested image. The image-specific fair-use rationale, credit, and original source link remain in the [source notes](sources.md).
