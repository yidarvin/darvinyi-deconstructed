# Nadine Ijewere — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This twelve-image working set follows Ijewere's *Anthesis* selection from the
2023 Senegal works back through the Portland, Jamaican, and Dominican Republic
photographs of 2019–22. It gives the later chapter a useful range of portrait,
water, landscape, and group arrangements while retaining pictures that are
identified by title, date, and credit in one authoritative public gallery.
The Guardian's gallery credits every image **“Photograph: Nadine Ijewere”**;
Ijewere's official portfolio identifies its photographs as **Copyright © 2020
Nadine Ijewere. All rights reserved.** No open licence or public-domain claim
was located, so every selection is a separately assessed **fair-use** use.

For every entry, the source is a publicly accessible Guardian display rendition;
no login, paywall, robots restriction, hotlink workaround, or other technical
access control was bypassed. The complete frame is retained because this book
teaches composition rather than using the pictures decoratively. The later
build will make a web-sized derivative and link back to the credited source;
that criticism/scholarship use does not replace the photographer's original,
gallery record, print, book, editorial publication, or licensed reproduction.
