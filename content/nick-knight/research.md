# Nick Knight — research note

## Scope and record

This selection spans 1997–2019 and is drawn from twelve public portfolio
renditions credited to Nick Knight / NK Image Ltd. It moves between *Flora*,
editorial fashion, and later collaborative fashion images; dates, titles, and
rights routes are recorded image by image in [sources.md](sources.md). The
chapter limits its claims to visible arrangement, color, cropping, pose, and
the source record's stated titles and contexts.

## Technique and production limits

The source record supports that these are photographs made for series, magazines,
and fashion commissions, and it identifies SHOWstudio for *Dolls*. It does not
document a uniform production method, capture medium, retouching workflow,
lighting setup, camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO,
or film format for the selected pictures. The ingested files contain no capture
EXIF. Consequently, the chapter does not infer technical settings from the
finished images.

## Printing limits

No consulted source establishes a printing process, edition specification, paper,
or display method for all twelve works. The web JPEGs are treated as the supplied
public display renditions, not as evidence of the artists' printing choices. The
analysis therefore concerns their visible image structure rather than a presumed
original print appearance.
