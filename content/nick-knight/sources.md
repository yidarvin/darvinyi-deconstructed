# Nick Knight — source record

## Selection and rights note

This twelve-image set follows Knight from the specimen-like verticals of *Flora*
through editorial and digital experiments into later images that remodel the body as
a mutable graphic field. Every file was acquired as an unaltered JPEG directly from
[Nick Knight's official public portfolio](https://www.nickknight.com/), which
identifies the work titles and dates. The site states that its images and films
belong to NK Image Ltd and are protected by copyright. No open licence is supplied,
so every selection below is a documented **fair-use** reproduction.

For each image, the assessment is the same four-factor, work-specific application:
(1) this book uses the complete, credited web copy for transformative criticism,
scholarship, and composition teaching; (2) it is a creative photograph, which weighs
against use; (3) retaining the complete frame is necessary to analyze the particular
spatial and visual decisions named below, not to decorate the chapter; and (4) the
limited web-sized copy, linked to the official portfolio and credited to the
rightsholder, does not replace the artist's original, a portfolio, or a licensed
reproduction. The direct public image responses were requested as JPEGs without
authentication or access-control circumvention. All files opened successfully; their
long edges range from 1,100px to 3,000px, above the 640px normal source floor.
