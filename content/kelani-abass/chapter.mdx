# Kelani Abass: the archive as a physical score

Kelani Abass makes photographs behave less like self-contained windows than like materials that can be stored, divided, printed over, mounted, and made to answer one another. In this 2019–21 selection, family images meet type cases, wood, printing blocks, colored grounds, and figures painted or collaged into broad fields. The result is not a tidy family chronology. It is a set of arrangements in which a frame, divider, or small block can change what a photograph is allowed to mean. These web-sized display derivatives are included for criticism and composition teaching under the individual fair-use rationales in the [source notes](../../content/kelani-abass/sources.md).

## cases and continuous fields

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="01-casing-history-sisters-2" label="Casing History (Sisters 2), 2020–21" caption="A wooden type case divides family photographs into unequal cells, making the large central image and small lower portraits one physical archive." />

The outline and rail in the overlay identify the case as more than a border: they make its compartments a reading system. Large faces, partial figures, and small portraits are held at incompatible scales, so no single image can become a complete account. The analyzer records a broad tonal span (p05 0.125, p95 1.000) and edge density 0.069; #fefdfd, #aa8766, and #302620 separate the pale surround, wood, and dark photographic pockets. The lower portrait anchor is deliberately modest: it shows how even a small compartment can pull the eye back into the archive after the central family group has established its scale.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="02-chronological-remuneration-xi" label="Chronological Remuneration XI, 2020" caption="Figures, numbers, and a suspended circular device are distributed across one ochre field rather than separated into panels." />

The procession line follows the low register of people, animals, and equipment; the vertical mark follows the actual central seam and hanging device. Together they make a continuous surface legible as a sequence of uneven episodes. The central figure’s feet, marked by the anchor, give the dispersed field bodily weight. Mean luminance 0.592, p05 0.267, p95 0.749, and edge density 0.028 support that reading: #eaac47, #c09850, and #da832f keep the field warm and continuous while dark figures interrupt it.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="03-chronological-remuneration-xiii" label="Chronological Remuneration XIII, 2020" caption="A band of foreground figures crosses a mural-like field of repeated and ghosted portraits, using a central join as a hinge rather than a panel break." />

The overlay does not call the ghosted rectangles separate frames. Its angled path names the rhythm of the foreground bodies, while the central join holds the work’s two sides in productive tension. The low anchor lands where the crowded figures meet the bottom edge. The analyzer’s phi grid is the closer fit; mean luminance 0.526, p05 0.220, p95 0.671, and edge density 0.023 fit its muted #bb9a8e, #a1877f, and #5e4b4a palette of layered rather than sharply bounded images.

## frames that make evidence

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="04-scrap-of-evidence-orogun" label="Scrap of Evidence (Orogun), 2020" caption="Three gold-framed vertical panels pace a woman, a repeated-portrait field, and a round portrait with a lower printing element." />

The outer grouping and middle-panel outline acknowledge a literal three-column cadence. The ellipse isolates the round portrait at right, not as a free-floating face but as a different contained memory beside the central repeat. Its anchor makes the competing right-hand center visible. With p05 0.204, p95 0.882, edge density 0.024, and #dad9dc, #8a7552, #493929, the pale wall and gold frames keep the three interior grounds distinct.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="05-stamping-history-making-time-3" label="Stamping History (Making Time 3), 2019" caption="Nine image fragments, separated by white gutters, use a strict tiled field to turn number-made marks into a photographic rhythm." />

The enclosing box and gutter lines locate the governing structure: not a single sheet, but nine neighboring tiles with pauses between them. A face, wheel, machine, and cropped body are made from repeated characters, and the lower anchor keeps the yellow tile from becoming an afterthought. Mean luminance 0.596, p05 0.235, p95 1.000, and edge density 0.058 match the contrast among #afa996, #8e8982, and #fcfcfc. Repetition is both image-making method and composition.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="06-scrap-of-evidence-abiye" label="Scrap of Evidence (Abiye), 2019" caption="A narrow frame divides a green family field from a red field containing a recessed printing block." />

The vertical division is a material color boundary, not an imaginary horizon. It directs attention from the cutout family group at lower left to the framed block on the red side. The group and block are unequal counterweights. The display is pale overall (mean 0.884; p05 0.369; p95 1.000; edge density 0.006), so #fefefe, #61775e, and #e9d1b3 make the small inner assemblage clear against its surrounding white.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="07-scrap-of-evidence-agogo-igbeyawo" label="Scrap of Evidence (Agogo Igbeyawo), 2019" caption="A black triangular block, a printing plate, and a cutout woman form a descending counterweight across a red-and-green field." />

This is a frontal object, not a perspective space. The overlay traces a visual route from the upper triangular block through the dark plate to the cutout woman, while the horizontal line marks the actual color change. The triangle anchor explains why the upper register remains active even when the figure gathers recognition below. Mean luminance 0.862, p05 0.325, p95 1.000, and edge density 0.005 show how spare the display is; #fefefe, #757a63, and #dbc3a5 let color and placement carry the structure.

## portraits, blocks, and offsets

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="08-scrap-of-evidence-aka-arogundabi" label="Scrap of Evidence (Aka-Arogundabi), 2019" caption="A seated portrait sits above a lower emblem in a simple vertical stack, with red ground visible above and below the inner image." />

The two frames distinguish picture from emblem, and the short connecting path states their hierarchy without pretending that the work is a fragmented archive. The lower emblem anchor shows a second mass pulling against the sitter’s larger dark rectangle. Phi is the analyzer’s closer grid fit; mean luminance 0.839, p05 0.294, p95 1.000, and edge density 0.004 reflect the sparse display of #fefefe, #b74525, and #4a483a.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="09-scrap-of-evidence-apon-i" label="Scrap of Evidence (Apon I), 2019" caption="A solitary standing figure occupies a quiet brown field while a small green plaque interrupts the lower left." />

The standing axis measures the figure’s long vertical presence, not symmetry. The plaque receives a separate frame and anchor because its offset prevents the surrounding ground from becoming empty. The high-key display (mean 0.853; p05 0.322; p95 1.000; edge density 0.003) places #fefefe beside #7f6a5c and #a28c7b, making the plate’s saturation and placement decisive.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="10-scrap-of-evidence-apon" label="Scrap of Evidence (Apon), 2019" caption="A standing portrait is held inside a brown inner rectangle by four dark corner blocks, converting a single figure into a nested object." />

The rectangle and its four supports are literal parts of the construction; the overlay follows their perimeter before marking the portrait’s vertical axis. The upper-left anchor identifies one support rather than inventing a second subject. Mean luminance 0.870, p05 0.341, p95 1.000, edge density 0.003, and #fefefe, #a2845c, #73643e explain why the small dark blocks are forceful in a pale field.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="11-scrap-of-evidence-awalah" label="Scrap of Evidence (Awalah), 2019" caption="A small printing block, a horizontal typebar, and a seated portrait make a compact vertical stack in a cyan field." />

The typebar is the strongest lateral interruption. Above it, a small block sets the stack in motion; below it, the portrait becomes the broadest image area. The overlay preserves those different scales instead of calling them a generic emblem. The analyzer finds mean luminance 0.871, p05 0.325, p95 1.000, and edge density 0.009; #fefefe, #639e9c, and #676656 make the cyan ground and dark printing forms clear.

<Plate slug="kelani-abass" id="12-scrap-of-evidence-boys-scout" label="Scrap of Evidence (Boys Scout), 2019" caption="A small badge, paired figures, and a large lower circular face form a vertical chain of scale changes." />

The ellipse is reserved for the oversized lower circle, whose face cannot be reduced to a decorative stamp. The connecting path makes a sequence: badge, pair, circle. The lower anchor confirms where the composition settles, even as the pale figures rise behind it. Phi is again the closer grid fit; mean luminance 0.858, p05 0.278, p95 1.000, and edge density 0.007 make #fefefe, #566e4b, and #a3b17f a compact green-and-pale counterpoint.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make a case, not a collage">
Place six photographs into an uneven grid of compartments. Let one image cross a divider and leave two spaces empty. Notice how storage, scale, and interruption change the order in which the pictures are read.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Give an image a counterweight">
Make a portrait on one side of a small board, then place a compact object, word, or printed mark on the other. Move the smaller element until it changes the portrait’s apparent balance without becoming a caption.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Build a field of intervals">
Arrange nine image fragments in a three-by-three field. Keep the gutters visible and test whether changing one fragment’s tone, color, or scale makes the grid read as a sequence rather than a single picture.
</ExerciseCard>
