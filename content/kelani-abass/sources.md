# Kelani Abass — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This 12-work set follows Abass’s use of family photographs, letterpress material,
and hand-built frames: the modular archive of *Casing History*, the three-part
comparisons of *Chronological Remuneration*, and the layered portrait/object
arrangements of *Scrap of Evidence*. MoMA’s [New Photography 2023
checklist](https://www.moma.org/documents/moma_master-checklist_589517.pdf) and
collection records for [*Casing History, Spilling Memories
1*](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/434635) and [2](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/434636)
confirm that these archive-and-type-case structures are central to the artist’s
recent practice. The acquired works below come from SMO Contemporary Art’s public
[Kelani Abass works archive](https://smocontemporaryart.com/artists/53-kelani-abass/works/),
which presents each work as a gallery display and enquiry record.

All files opened successfully after acquisition. The gallery serves its public
display derivatives as WebP responses; they were decoded and saved as JPEGs for
the raw handoff, with no cropping, retouching, or upscaling. Every file
has a 1,600px long edge, above the 640px source floor.

All entries use **fair-use**, not open access. MoMA’s collection records state
`Copyright © 2026 Kelani Abass`; SMO’s public presentation supplies no open licence.
Each assessment below is made for its named work: the use is criticism,
scholarship, and transformative composition teaching; Abass’s works are creative;
the complete web-sized display is needed to analyze the frame, print, archive, and
image relationship; and the chapter will credit and link to the gallery record,
using a public 1,600px derivative that does not replace the unique work, a licensed
reproduction, or its sales market.
