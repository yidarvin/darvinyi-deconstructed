# Philip-Lorca diCorcia — source shortlist

This set moves from the deliberately lit domestic pictures of the late 1970s to the travel, street, portrait, and *Hustlers* work that makes diCorcia’s photographs feel at once observed and staged. Eight files are public display renditions served by SFMOMA’s collection pages; two are public IIIF display renditions from the Art Institute of Chicago. Every download was opened as a JPEG and is at least 843px on its long edge. No open-licence or public-domain reproduction was located for these copyrighted works.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. Each assessment below is individual: this book uses a credited, record-linked, web-sized copy for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; it needs the complete published frame to analyze diCorcia’s spatial and lighting construction; and the copy does not replace a museum record, original print, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

MoMA describes diCorcia’s work as combining real people and places with carefully arranged objects, lighting, and framing, and identifies the late-1970s friends-and-family pictures, *Hustlers*, and later street work as key bodies of work: https://www.moma.org/artists/7027-philip-lorca-dicorcia. SFMOMA’s collection pages supplied the artist credit, title, date, copyright notice, and public display files for the first eight selections. The Art Institute API supplied the title, date, public-domain status, and IIIF file references for the final two.
