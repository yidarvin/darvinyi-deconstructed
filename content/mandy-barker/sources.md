# Mandy Barker — source shortlist

Mandy Barker’s *SOUP* (2011) turns recovered marine plastic into dark-field, specimen-like constellations. The series makes scale visible by arranging each material class—bath toys, pellets, fishing line, cups, and fragments—as an all-over field whose visual seduction gives way to evidence of extraction, circulation, ingestion, and persistence. This 11-work group is the artist’s public canonical gallery and supplies a particularly coherent set for studying repetition, density, negative space, figure-ground reversal, and serial variation.

### 01 — *SOUP: Turtle* (2011)
- **Why it matters:** Repeated blue bath toys form an exuberant vertical swarm whose nearly even spacing and black gaps turn consumer objects into a false zoological specimen field.

The artist identifies toys from the 1992 *Evergreen Ever Laurel* loss, circulating in the North Pacific Gyre for 16 years.
- **Use basis:** **fair-use.** © Mandy Barker; artist gallery credit retained.
