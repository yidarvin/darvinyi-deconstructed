# Mandy Barker — research note

## What the supplied records establish

The supplied artist-gallery record identifies this eleven-work selection as *SOUP* (2011). It describes recovered marine plastic arranged in separate material groupings: bath toys from the 1992 *Ever Laurel* loss, translucent fragments, lettered debris, charred fragments, pellets collected from six beaches, fishing line compacted by tidal movement, cup fragments, red plastic, refused plastic, shoreline material from Spurn Point Nature Reserve, and more than 500 pieces recovered from an albatross chick’s digestive tract. The artist gallery is the primary title, material, and public-display source; the supplied SFO Museum record independently corroborates the artist credit and 2011 date for *Nurdles* and *Refused*.

The selection is a serial still-life practice: discrete found material is made into a dark-field picture in which collecting, sorting, repeated placement, interval, and negative space are structural rather than merely illustrative. The documents support those material histories and the series date. They do not document the exact production sequence, lighting arrangement, digital-compositing workflow, print edition, paper, ink, or dimensions for these individual works.

## Technical limits

No supplied source establishes camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, capture medium, lighting setup, post-production method, printing process, paper, edition, or other individual-work settings. The ingested gallery JPEGs contain no usable EXIF camera settings. Their pixel dimensions are public display-copy dimensions, not capture or print dimensions. The chapter therefore restricts technical discussion to the documented materials and dates, plus visible and measured composition.
