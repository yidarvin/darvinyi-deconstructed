# Farah Al Qasimi — source shortlist

This twelve-image set follows the 2016–19 photographic core presented by Al Qasimi's own portfolio in the exhibition page [*Arrival*](https://farahalqasimi.com/Arrival).  The page identifies the 2019 Third Line exhibition and describes its photographs as scenes drawn from a world of domestic indicators, youth culture, jinn folklore, display technology, and a deliberately uneasy, high-color atmosphere. It supplies public, full-frame display JPEGs for the works below. Every downloaded file opened successfully; its long edge is 1,599–4,200px, above the project's 640px acquisition floor, with no upscaling or alteration.

All selections are copyrighted and are used on an individual **fair-use** basis. Al Qasimi is identified as the artist on her [official site](https://farahalqasimi.com/Info), which provides no open licence or public-domain dedication. The artist's gallery, The Third Line, presents the exhibition and represents her work. Each retained file is a web-published display rendition used for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching—not for decorative replacement or resale. The complete frame is retained only where its particular spatial argument requires it; each item remains credited and linked to the artist's presentation and does not replace an original print, the artist/gallery record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

The [MoMA artist record](https://www.moma.org/artists/131770-farah-al-qasimi) independently lists *Ghaith at Home*, *Piper at Barbeque in Houston*, *S Folding Blanket*, *Gurdwara Nanak Darbar Sahib (Kansas)*, *Living Room Vape*, and *May's Blue Eye*, confirming the breadth of Al Qasimi's photographic practice beyond this focused selection. The Third Line's [artist profile](https://www.thethirdline.com/artists/40-farah-al-qasimi/) describes the artist's interest in photographic prints, screens, the internet, and hierarchies of information and emotion. The present list deliberately concentrates on the reliably titled and full-frame public portfolio images from *Arrival*, where domestic interiors, surfaces, reflected images, retail display, and containment form a cohesive compositional chapter. No low-resolution exception, generated image, alteration, or limited-source fallback applies.
