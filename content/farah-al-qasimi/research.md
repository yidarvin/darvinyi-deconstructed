# Farah Al Qasimi — research notes

## Record and scope

The source set is a focused group of twelve works dated 2016–19, presented in
Al Qasimi’s *Arrival* portfolio. The artist’s site and The Third Line describe
a practice concerned with photography, screens, the internet, domestic
indicators, youth culture, jinn folklore, and hierarchies of information and
emotion. Those are useful contexts for this chapter’s readings of interiors,
retail display, reflection, and patterned surfaces; they do not establish a
single universal meaning for any individual photograph. See the
[source notes](sources.md) for links and image-specific records.

## Medium and technique

The official display filenames for all twelve selected works identify the
medium as **inkjet print**. The source record supports calling them photographic
inkjet prints; it does not document a particular camera body, lens, focal
length, sensor or film stock, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting setup, or
post-production workflow. The ingested display JPEGs contain no capture EXIF.
Accordingly, this chapter discusses the visible image—framing, color,
reflection, depth, and illumination—without presenting those observations as
documented capture settings or production facts.

## Method for the chapter

Composition claims are grounded in the ingested files and their deterministic
analysis records. The overlays distinguish measurable structure (such as a
screen edge, ceiling seam, or subject location) from interpretive paths such as
a knife, drape, or orchid branch. Palette and tonal values describe only these
web display derivatives, not the color or density of an exhibition print.
