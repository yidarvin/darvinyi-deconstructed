# Julia Fullerton-Batten — research note

## What the supplied sources establish

The twelve plates are from Julia Fullerton-Batten's *Teenage Stories* (2005). The artist's project page identifies the works and explains that she photographed street-cast girls on location in model villages, directing them to remain static and unemotional. The artist's biography describes the broader practice as cinematic visual storytelling built from staged tableaux, unusual locations, and sophisticated lighting. [Source notes](sources.md)

The source record therefore supports discussion of deliberate scale mismatch, location work, direction, staging, and visible illumination. It does not identify a print process, camera body, lens, film or sensor, aperture, shutter speed, sensitivity, lighting plan, composite workflow, or retouching process for these individual photographs. The web-display JPEGs retain only scanner/vendor `Make` metadata on some files (`Itek Colour Graphics Ltd`); that is not evidence of capture equipment or settings.

## Method for this chapter

This chapter treats the twelve full-frame web displays as photographs whose visible structure can be described: miniature architecture, bodies, roads, water, roofs, and light. Overlay claims are limited to those pictured structures, and tonal and palette remarks come from analysis of the ingested files. The images are used under the image-specific fair-use assessments in the [source notes](sources.md); their full frames are necessary to teach the relation between life-sized bodies and model settings.
