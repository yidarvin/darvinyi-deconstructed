# Mishka Henner — source shortlist

This set concentrates on *Eighteen Pumpjacks* (2012), Henner's typology of oil-extraction sites assembled from satellite imagery. The National Gallery of Art (NGA) holds eighteen works from the portfolio and publicly serves these ten 900px-long-edge IIIF display renditions. Each file was retrieved from that public display service, opened successfully, and retains the complete vertical published frame. NGA's individual records identify the portfolio, artist, date, accession number, and the notice “© Mishka Henner”; the records also say that their media are not available for ordinary download. The deliberately web-sized IIIF renditions are therefore used here only under the documented fair-use basis below.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. These are copyrighted contemporary works (Mishka Henner; © Mishka Henner), not open-licensed reproductions. Each assessment is limited and transformative: this nonprofit book uses the complete, credited 900px display frame for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching about typology, central anchoring, field texture, and satellite-scale abstraction. The whole frame is needed to assess the relationship between pumpjack, access trace, and surrounding land; a crop would remove that visual argument. Each source links to the NGA collection record and uses only its public web display rendition, which does not replace the original inkjet print, collection record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

NGA identifies these works as part of *Eighteen Pumpjacks* and describes each as an inkjet print, dated 2012, with a © Mishka Henner notice. Its artist record lists nineteen Henner works and makes the portfolio's repeated titles, locations, and accession information available: https://www.nga.gov/artists/50037-mishka-henner. Henner's own overview situates this typology among his satellite, surveillance, and public-data projects, which investigate systems of vision and their relation to power: https://mishkahenner.com/Biography.
