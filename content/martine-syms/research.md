# Martine Syms — research note

## Scope and documented material

The Whitney Museum of American Art records the eight 2019 works in this selection as inkjet prints by Martine Syms, accessioned as 2022.20.1–2022.20.8; the associated *People Who Aren’t Friends or Lovers or Exes* record is a wall painting with variable dimensions, and *Intro to Threat Modeling* is a 2017 color-and-sound video of 4:32. Titles, accession numbers, media, credits, and public display links are retained image by image in [sources.md](sources.md). The source notes also link the Whitney’s [artist record](https://whitney.org/artists/17568) and the Studio Museum in Harlem’s [artist page](https://www.studiomuseum.org/artists/martine-syms), which describes her engagement with the representation of Blackness in the digital age and the effects of media and technology on the performance of race and gender.

The sequence belongs to the late-2010s, with the photographic prints dated 2019 and the available embedded capture timestamps for the eight photograph files dated October 2016. That difference is significant: the files establish capture metadata, while the museum establishes the exhibited works’ 2019 date and inkjet-print medium. It would be wrong to treat a web JPEG as documentation of an edition, paper, dimensions, or post-production process.

## File-embedded camera data and limits

The ingested public display JPEGs preserve EXIF that the ingestion manifest recorded before normalization. Files 01–02 identify a Hasselblad H6D-400c MS with a 50 mm focal length, 1/125 s, f/5.6, ISO 64; files 03–05 and 07–08 identify that body with an 80 mm focal length, 1/125 s, f/6.8, ISO 64; file 06 returns to 50 mm, 1/125 s, f/5.6, ISO 64. These are file-embedded values, not a separately published account of Syms’s working method, and they do not establish lighting, crop, digital processing, print paper, edition, or display intent.

The two gallery-view files (09–10) carry Phase One IQ4 150MP metadata—35 mm, 1/60 s, f/10, ISO 50—with a 2019 timestamp. Because the Whitney uses the same public installation-view rendition for both collection records, those values describe the supplied documentation photograph, not the camera or settings of Syms’s photographed prints or video installation. The chapter relies on the visible structure of those museum-provided renditions and on measured geometry, tone, and palette; it does not infer undocumented technique.

## Access and rights

All ten files are credited, public Whitney display renditions used under the per-image fair-use rationale in [sources.md](sources.md): limited, whole-frame copies are needed to teach the works’ framing and display relations and do not substitute for a print, video installation, or licensed reproduction.
