Martine Syms uses the camera as one part of a larger social apparatus: a body can be clipped by a frame, a billboard can speak louder than a face, and an exhibition wall can become another image surface. This selection of Whitney collection renditions moves through the 2019 *40x60*, *Insert*, *Half Sheet*, *One Sheet*, and *Window Card* works before widening to the installation context of *People Who Aren’t Friends or Lovers or Exes* and *Intro to Threat Modeling*. The overlay claims refer only to visible structure in those complete museum display files; titles, records, credits, and the image-specific fair-use rationale remain in the [source notes](../../content/martine-syms/sources.md).

## blockage is not a missing view

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="01-40x60-a" label="40x60, 2019 (2022.20.1)" caption="A bent figure occupies a lower field while an intentionally expansive pale wall makes concealment and off-frame pressure active parts of the image." />

The first *40x60* treats a small body as an event at the edge of a much larger near-empty field. The overlay does not dignify the detector’s false “horizon”; instead it anchors the shielded figure, outlines the lower action field, and follows the body fold. The strikingly low measured edge density, 0.008, supports the sense that almost nothing competes with the gesture. Pale gray-beige `#bebab4` and `#d3cdc3` dominate, while near-black `#17171a` concentrates the body’s weight. The measured p05–p95 span of 0.078–0.847 gives that pale wall enough tonal room to feel present rather than blank.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="02-40x60-b" label="40x60, 2019 (2022.20.2)" caption="A curled page slices through pegboard and lettering, making a diagonal act of occlusion more important than a complete statement." />

The second *40x60* reverses the first image’s austerity: pegboard dots establish a repeated field, then a torn sheet crosses it on the diagonal tracked by the overlay. Its paper edge is a real measured break at y0.318, while the page anchor marks the occlusion rather than treating the text as transparent information. With p05 0.667, p95 0.910, and a palette of cool off-whites through brown `#3c3228`, it stays high-key even as it turns reading into interruption.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="03-insert" label="Insert, 2019 (2022.20.4)" caption="A small walking group drifts across the shore beneath broad bands of water and sand." />

In *Insert*, distance is the subject. The phi grid and water-to-shore band hold a tiny group of walkers at the saliency point near x0.498, y0.633, while the shore-drift path refuses a single dramatic vanishing point. The image is formally quiet, with edge density 0.011 and p05–p95 0.569 to 0.827; the palette of gray-blue `#a6aaa7`, sand `#dacbbc`, and dark brown `#5a4a32` turns a public shoreline into an interval between intimacy and scale.

## the street arrives as a cropped collage

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="04-half-sheet" label="Half Sheet, 2019 (2022.20.3)" caption="Rock planes, a foreground bag, and clipped bodies press the figures into a shallow, unstable enclosure." />

*Half Sheet* is the set’s densest natural tangle. The two measured rock-plane lines meet a cropped figure edge, while the anchor retains the foreground bag as a local counterweight rather than pretending the viewer can cleanly extract a portrait. Its 0.101 edge density is nearly ten times that of the spare wall image; black-green `#1c1b16`, brown `#42382b`, and pale rock `#c6c1bd` span p05 0.067 to p95 0.847. Compression, not clarity, makes the scene work.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="05-one-sheet" label="One Sheet, 2019 (2022.20.5)" caption="Sign stacks, billboard surfaces, and a curb divide the frame around two small figures waiting on a corner." />

*One Sheet* lets public signage control the scale of the waiting figures. The overlay follows the vertical sign stack and its billboard block before arriving at the pair near x0.580, y0.695; the curb-and-street band at y0.770 makes their pause part of traffic and commerce rather than a detached street portrait. A moderate 0.040 edge density and the mixture of wall-gray `#c0b9b3`, dark brown `#2b2722`, and faded sign colors let the frame read as a built collage.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="06-three-sheet" label="Three Sheet, 2019 (2022.20.6)" caption="A van becomes a near-field screen; a raised arm and a narrow street opening make seeing conditional." />

In *Three Sheet*, the white vehicle is not background architecture; it is the obstacle through which the city must be read. The overlay frames that large screen, names the raised arm at x0.692, y0.430, and keeps a short vehicle edge as a directional cue. A broad p05–p95 range of 0.216 to 0.914 and low 0.033 edge density make the dark arm, pale van, and thin urban glimpse feel like unequal layers of access.

## repetition and display make their own frames

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="07-window-card-a" label="Window Card, 2019 (2022.20.7)" caption="A repeated rank of suited figures sits beneath fluorescent ceiling lines that funnel the space toward a central body." />

The first *Window Card* turns repetition into a controlled corridor. The vertical axis is supported by the room’s strong measured symmetry, while the central suited figure and fluorescent diagonal show how the ceiling narrows the group into a rank. Yellow `#eec74c` and `#c7a13c` puncture the gray-green `#36413a` field; with only 0.013 edge density, the broad ceiling planes carry as much authority as the bodies below.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="08-window-card-b" label="Window Card, 2019 (2022.20.8)" caption="Leaf diagonals and counter-stems turn a plant arrangement into a layered, shallow screen." />

The second *Window Card* refuses the corridor’s order. The shelf break at y0.322 stabilizes a field whose leaf diagonal, counter-stem, and pale cluster repeatedly interrupt depth. At 0.108, its edge density is the highest in this selection: dark green `#1e2b1e`, muted green `#425131`, and pale leaf `#cbc7c0` make the image teem without granting the eye a single unblocked route.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="09-people-who-arent-friends" label="People Who Aren’t Friends or Lovers or Exes, 2019 (installation view)" caption="Text, framed works, ceiling convergence, and a reflective bench turn the gallery itself into a second composition." />

The installation view expands Syms’s cropping logic to room scale. The gallery wall seam, ceiling convergence at x0.371, y0.133, and bench line coordinate the orange text and display fragments without reducing them to decoration. The rendition is light and spacious, with mean luminance 0.780 and p05–p95 0.576 to 0.922; orange `#dc863b` carries the principal chromatic interruption across white walls and pale wood.

<Plate slug="martine-syms" id="10-intro-to-threat-modeling" label="Intro to Threat Modeling, 2017 (installation view)" caption="The available Whitney installation view places a small video screen within a larger field of text, image, ceiling, and reflection." />

The Whitney supplies the same installation-view rendition for *Intro to Threat Modeling*, so this plate analyzes that documented display relation, not a frame of the four-minute, thirty-two-second video. The overlay anchors the small screen at x0.195, y0.500 and traces the bench’s long lateral pull; the result is a spatial account of a time-based work being made deliberately partial. Its close tonal range (p05–p95 0.573–0.922) and orange `#d7853f` reinforce the continuity with the preceding room view while the screen remains a small, insistent interruption.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make obstruction carry the image">
Photograph a person or action through a door, vehicle, sign, or another near object. Keep enough of the obstruction in frame that it changes the meaning of what can be seen. Do not treat it as an accidental crop to be corrected later.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Let a repeated field meet one interruption">
Use a pegboard, windows, shelves, leaves, tiles, or another repeated structure. Introduce or find one torn, bent, gestural, or chromatically distinct element. Compose so the regular field is still legible when the interruption appears.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Photograph the second frame">
Make an installation or room view in which a text line, bench, reflection, ceiling, or doorway recomposes the artworks inside it. Check that the architectural structure contributes an argument rather than merely documenting that the work was exhibited.
</ExerciseCard>
