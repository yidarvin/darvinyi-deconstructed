# Michael Wolf — source notes

All 12 files below were downloaded directly from the publicly accessible Michael
Wolf Photography archive, opened after acquisition, and retained at the archive's
native web rendition. Their long edges run from 900px down to 900px (the portrait
images are 675–720px wide and 900px tall), exceeding the 640px normal floor. The
archive labels its work “all images copyright Michael Wolf”; no open licence is
claimed. These are therefore documented fair-use reproductions, not open content.

The set follows the artist's movement from Hong Kong's façade-as-field, through the
human pressure of Tokyo's train windows and Chicago's distant surveillance, to a
screen-derived street photograph. It establishes both physical density and the
networked forms of looking named in the chapter brief.
