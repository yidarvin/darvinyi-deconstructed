# Michael Wolf — research

## Scope and sources

This chapter studies twelve web-sized photographs from Michael Wolf Photography's publicly accessible archive. The image-by-image titles, series dates, archive links, credits, and fair-use rationale are preserved in [sources.md](sources.md). They move from the Hong Kong façades of *Architecture of Density* (2003–2014), through *Tokyo Compression* (2010) and *The Transparent City* (2008), to the screen-derived *Paris Street View* works (2007 and 2009). This is a teaching selection, not a catalogue of Wolf's work.

## Technique and visual method

The archive record supports a description of Wolf's working method as selection, crop, and serial comparison. In the facade photographs, a telephoto-looking crop makes separate towers read as patterned planes; windows, blinds, air conditioners, and laundry keep an abstract field tied to habitation. *Tokyo Compression* places commuters behind train glass, treating condensation, reflections, and window edges as active planes rather than obstructions. *The Transparent City* turns lit office cells into a distant surveillance field, while *Paris Street View* explicitly works with a pre-existing online image and retains pixels, blur, and interface-era coarseness as part of the picture. These visual claims describe the supplied images; they do not claim that every relation was preplanned or that the archive files establish the artist's complete procedure.

## Camera metadata, settings, and printing

Most normalized files carry no capture EXIF, and the archive/source notes do not document a camera, lens, exposure, film or sensor format, or original print process for the twelve works. Two raw web files retain limited embedded metadata: `10-transparent-city-20` reports a Phase One P 45+ at 135mm, f/11, ISO 200, and 6 seconds (timestamp 2007-10-11); `09-transparent-city-01` reports 90mm, f/11, ISO 100, and 61 seconds (timestamp 2007-10-04) but no camera maker/model. `06-tokyo-compression-118` reports a Canon EOS 5D Mark II with an EF50mm f/2.5 Compact Macro at 50mm, f/3.5, ISO 200, and 1/13 second (timestamp 2008-09-08). Because these are embedded in archive web renditions rather than a primary technical record, they are reported as file metadata, not generalized to their titled series or treated as a verified original-print recipe. No original printing process is identified, and no setting is inferred for the remaining nine images.

## Rights and provenance

The archive states “all images copyright Michael Wolf”; no open licence is claimed. Each reproduction is therefore used as documented fair use for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. [sources.md](sources.md) records the public archive link, direct display rendition, credit, and four-factor rationale for every complete frame. The copies remain at their native web sizes and are linked and credited; they are not substitutes for an artist print, archive record, or licensed reproduction.
