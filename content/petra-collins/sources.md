# Petra Collins — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This set concentrates on Collins's own public portfolio rather than treating a
social-media repost as a source. Her **Selfie** page describes the work as an
ongoing series begun in 2013, concerned with teenage girlhood and young women's
ability to create, curate, and distribute their own images. **Neon Works** is
dated 2014–15 on the same portfolio. The selected twelve display files were
downloaded directly from those public artist pages; each opened successfully,
and their long edges are 800px or 820px. No file was enlarged or altered.

The source pages state: “All content copyright the artist. No commercial use
without express written permission” and “© 2026 Petra Collins.” They therefore
do not grant an open licence. Each image below is retained on an individual
**fair-use** basis for this nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative
composition-teaching book. The chapter needs the complete, web-sized frame to
analyse the stated arrangement, color, and picture-plane decisions; it credits
and links to Collins's public portfolio and does not replace an original,
licensed reproduction, or the artist's market. The works are creative
photographs (a factor weighing against use); the limited, contextual analytical
reproduction and non-substitutive purpose weigh in favor. Each item below also
states the image-specific pedagogical necessity.
