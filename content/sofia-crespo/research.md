# Sofia Crespo — research notes

## Record and method

Sofia Crespo's *Neural Zoo* is dated 2018–2022 on the artist's [project page](https://sofiacrespo.com/neural-zoo/). The same page identifies convolutional neural networks (CNNs) as its medium and describes a practice of recombining familiar natural textures into forms that do not resolve as known organisms. The ten works in this chapter are complete, artist-published web renditions from that project, documented individually in [sources.md](sources.md). The exhibition text for [*Neural Zoo*](https://www.everythingfine.org/en/berlin-09-2022/digital-hope-inner/neural-zoo/) independently identifies the series as machine-generated speculative animals and names *Free Will* and *Soft Sea of Awareness*.

This is contemporary, computational image-making, not camera photography. The source record provides no camera body, lens, film or sensor, exposure, lighting setup, original print process, edition, or post-production workflow. The normalized files contain no EXIF settings. This chapter therefore discusses only the published images' visible structure and the stated CNN medium; it does not invent photographic settings or printing facts.

## Visual concerns

The series makes taxonomic recognition unstable: bilateral axes, eyes, limbs, translucent tissues, and specimen-like isolation appear, then fail to settle into a single biologically coherent body. Black grounds and high local contrast often turn a synthetic aggregate into a portrait; crowded marine and insect fields reverse the strategy, making a body emerge from distributed detail. The composition analysis supports that range: the moth image has unusually low measured edge density (0.0510) against a very bright mean luminance (0.7345), while the parrot-like and anatomical works have dense edges (0.1891 and 0.2041) that sustain layered recognition.

## Teaching limits

The images can be analyzed as finished published frames, but their source record does not establish the training data, model architecture beyond the artist's CNN description, prompts, seeds, or the sequence of human and machine decisions that produced each work. Any discussion of such hidden production details would be speculation.
