# Sofia Crespo — source shortlist

This ten-image working set comes from Crespo’s own publicly accessible *Neural Zoo* project page. That page identifies the project as *Neural Zoo* (2018–2022), made with convolutional neural networks, and displays the complete works at web-accessible URLs. Each downloaded JPEG opens successfully and preserves the artist’s complete published frame; all exceed the project’s 640px long-edge floor.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. These are copyrighted contemporary works; the artist site gives the notice “© Sofia Crespo 2025,” so Sofia Crespo is credited as creator/rightsholder. Each is used for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching about synthetic biological form, framing, hierarchy, rhythm, and figure–ground. The whole published frame is necessary to make those arguments; crops would remove the relations being studied. This project will use a web-sized rendition, credit and link to the artist’s project page, and does not replace an original artwork, artist record, edition, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Crespo describes *Neural Zoo* as an exploration of recombination: images resemble nature but are made from familiar textures rearranged into forms outside known reality. The artist page dates the project 2018–2022 and lists CNNs as its medium: https://sofiacrespo.com/neural-zoo/. The exhibition text from Everything Fine independently identifies *Free Will* and *Soft Sea of Awareness* as works in the series and describes its machine-generated speculative animals: https://www.everythingfine.org/en/berlin-09-2022/digital-hope-inner/neural-zoo/.
