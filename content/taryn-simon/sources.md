# Taryn Simon — source shortlist

## Selection and rights basis

This four-image working set establishes Simon's characteristic combination of
forensic access, frontal description, controlled colour, and serial/indexical
thinking. Three files are public 800px display renditions supplied by Centre
Pompidou collection records. The fourth is the artist's own publicly served
600px display rendition. All four are copyrighted works, so this chapter uses
them under **fair-use**, not as open-licensed material. Centre Pompidou's
records identify Taryn Simon as creator and name its Musée national d'art
moderne as copyright holder; Simon's official project page is credited to the
artist. Each copy is used only as a credited, record-linked, web-sized complete
frame for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition
teaching. That use does not replace the original print, collection record, or
licensed reproduction.

The first three images clear the normal 640px long-edge floor. The final image
is a narrowly documented 600px exception: the official artist file is the only
public full-frame rendition found after checking the project page, and its
unique blue pool, capsule grid, and receding handrail remain legible at that
size. It is a compositionally essential example of Simon's restricted-site
access; it was neither enlarged nor altered.

All local JPEGs opened successfully after download.

## Broader canonical research shortlist

The following eight works were verified as strong builder leads from Simon's
official project pages and collection records. They are not part of this
source unit's acquired set and are not used here; if selected later, each needs
its own fresh source and four-factor fair-use assessment.

- *Charles Irvin Fain, Scene of the Crime, the Snake River, Melba, Idaho* (2002), *The Innocents* — a portrait placed at a legally charged but visually open site, for studying caption/frame contradiction. [Official project page](https://tarynsimon.com/works/innocents/).
- *Larry Mayes, Scene of Arrest, The Royal Inn, Gary, Indiana* (2002), *The Innocents* — a tight room and bed create a spatially claustrophobic counterpoint to a procedural caption. [Official project page](https://tarynsimon.com/works/innocents/).
- *Calvin Washington, C&E Motel, Room No. 24, Waco, Texas* (2002), *The Innocents* — a frontal motel interior lets ordinary architecture carry the image's evidentiary pressure. [MoMA artist record](https://www.moma.org/collection/artists/41212).
- *Ron Williamson, Baseball Field, Norman, Oklahoma* (2002), *The Innocents* — a sparse field makes biography and place read through a deliberately quiet, horizontal arrangement. [Official project page](https://tarynsimon.com/works/innocents/).
- *Bird Corpse, labeled as home décor, Indonesia to Miami, Florida (prohibited)* (2010), *Contraband* — a single isolated specimen shows how white-background cataloguing turns a seized object into an image typology. [Guardian public display and credit](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2011/jan/03/taryn-simon-contraband-america).
- *Anabolic Steroids (Illegal)* (2010), *Contraband* — modular boxes and repeated small frames turn regulated evidence into an archival grid. [Official project page](https://tarynsimon.com/works/contraband/).
- *Chapter I* (2011), *A Living Man Declared Dead and Other Chapters* — portrait rows, a text panel, and footnotes make layout itself an argument about lineage and proof. [MoMA collection record](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/155734).
- *Chapter XVIII* (2011), *A Living Man Declared Dead and Other Chapters* — a long three-part installation offers a later comparative case for panel sequence, absence, and archival narration. [Centre Pompidou collection record](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/ressources/oeuvre/c4ykbd4).
