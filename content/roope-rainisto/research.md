# Roope Rainisto — research notes

## Documented practice and chronology

Roope Rainisto’s artist materials describe *Life In West America* as a 2023
collection of 500 unique AI-generated, post-photographic works. The project
uses a deliberately unstable version of mid-century American visual language:
bright domestic surfaces, cars, motels, carefully groomed figures, and scenes
that feel photographically coherent until a body, prop, or spatial relation
will not resolve. Ragnar Digital’s artist note similarly describes a practice
that works through photography, cinema, memory, custom-trained generative
systems, and retained distortions.

The supplied source packet identifies the twelve plates in this chapter with
titles and connects the selection to the project. The collection record is a
useful account of the project’s date and method, but it is not a substitute for
individual work records. Accordingly, this chapter treats the titles and
image-specific provenance as the supplied source packet documents them; it
does not infer an individual creation date beyond that record.

## Media, making, and what is not documented

The sources support calling this a generative post-photographic practice, not
a conventional camera-made series. They describe custom-trained models,
DreamBooth, inpainting, AI upscaling, and hand editing as methods in Rainisto’s
practice. They do not establish which of those methods was used for any one
supplied plate, nor do they document prompts, model versions, training data,
seeds, aspect-ratio settings, colour workflow, or retouching decisions for the
selected images.

The artist-served display renditions in this chapter are 1599 × 1279 pixels.
The ingest manifest records no EXIF in any of them. There is therefore no
documented camera, lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, print paper,
or editioning process to report. The tonal, palette, and geometry observations
in the chapter describe these supplied web renditions only.

## Rights and identification

The artist site identifies its gallery material as copyrighted and all rights
reserved; no open licence is supplied. Image-specific attribution, source
links, and the chapter’s fair-use rationale are retained in
[sources.md](sources.md). The chapter uses the full frame only where its
relations of figure, architecture, prop, and spatial discontinuity are the
subject of criticism.
