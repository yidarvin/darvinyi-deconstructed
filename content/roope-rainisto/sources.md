# Roope Rainisto — source shortlist

This selection concentrates on *Life In West America* (2023), Rainisto’s 500-work AI “post-photography” collection. The twelve files below were downloaded from the artist’s own publicly accessible gallery as its served 1600px display renditions and opened successfully at 1599 × 1279px. The archive identifies the artist and labels its images “Copyright © 2025 Roope Rainisto” / “© All rights reserved”; it supplies no open licence. These copyrighted, contemporary works are therefore included on the documented **fair-use** basis below.

**Rights basis:** Each use is a limited, transformative use for nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching—not illustration or replacement. The chapter retains each complete published frame because the teaching depends on the relations among figure, architecture, prop, and artificial photographic continuity. Every local copy is a 1599 × 1279px public display rendition; it credits and links to the artist’s gallery and does not replace an original NFT, print, the gallery record, or a licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Rainisto’s [collection statement](https://www.rooperainisto.com/collections) identifies *Life In West America* as a 2023, 500-image AI-generated post-photography project whose distorted Americana is deliberately both nostalgic and subtly alien. The artist’s [gallery](https://www.rooperainisto.com/life-in-west-america) supplies the selected complete frames and identifies the works by title. [Ragnar Digital’s artist note](https://www.ragnardigital.art/artists/roope-rainisto) independently describes Rainisto’s use of photography’s visual language, custom-trained models, and generative distortions as part of the work rather than defects to remove.
