# Abraham Oghobase — source shortlist

This retained set follows Oghobase's study of the Jos Plateau: landscape photographs in which the built environment and a bodily intervention make extraction and colonial history legible through the frame. Four complete-frame JPEGs were retrieved from KADIST's public collection page, opened successfully, and retained at their supplied 2500 × 1406px dimensions. KADIST credits Abraham Oghobase but does not state an open licence.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every retained selection. Abraham Oghobase is the identified artist/rightsholder and KADIST credits him. Each use is individually limited and transformative: this nonprofit book uses a credited, publicly displayed, web-sized copy for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The complete frame is needed to analyze the particular relationship of body, geological material, horizon, scale, and layered photographic surface; a crop would defeat that visual argument. Each copy links to and credits its public collection record and does not replace the original, a museum record, print, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

- MoMA's [artist record](https://www.moma.org/artists/134873-abraham-oghobase) identifies eleven online works from *Constructed Realities* (2019–22) and locates Oghobase in *New Photography 2023*.
- KADIST's [collection page](https://kadist.org/people/abraham-oghobase/) identifies the four *Anatomy of Landscape – Jos* works and frames the practice around socio-economic and historical geographies.
- The [Lahore Biennale Foundation exhibition record](https://www.lahorebiennale.org/lb03-artists/abraham-onoriode-oghobase/) describes the Jos works' relation to residual British colonial tin mining and the artist's use of his body within those landscapes.
- The [Art Institute of Chicago record for *Rock Anatomy*](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/251294/rock-anatomy) independently describes the 2018 work's photographic layering of mineral specimens onto self-portraits and the mining history behind it.
