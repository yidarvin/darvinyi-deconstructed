# Alex Prager — research note

## Scope of the source record

This chapter considers ten photographs from Alex Prager's *Face In The Crowd* (2013). The artist's [selected-work record](https://www.alexprager.com/part-ii-view) identifies the titles, year, and dimensions of each work: the selection ranges from 59 × 92.85 inches for *Crowd #3 (Pelican Beach)* to 59.5 × 56 inches for *Crowd #11 (Cedar and Broad Street)* and *Crowd #12 (Speedy-click.com)*. The source packet preserves the artist-page links, credits, and image-by-image fair-use rationale in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Technique and printing

The record documents a 2013 series of large works and public web display files, but it does not identify the photographic capture process, negative or sensor format, lighting plan, retouching workflow, print medium, paper, edition, printer, or printing date for these ten photographs. The chapter can therefore describe visible staging, color, viewpoint, architecture, and crowd distribution; it does not convert those observations into undocumented production claims.

## Camera settings and digitization metadata

No supplied source identifies a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO/sensitivity, film stock, or exposure setting. The normalized manifest records no EXIF for the acquired JPEGs. Those absences are significant: the 1,600px normalized images are analytical copies of credited web renditions, not evidence about how Prager made the original photographs.
