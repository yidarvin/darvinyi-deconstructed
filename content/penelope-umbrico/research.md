# Penelope Umbrico — research note

## Scope of the supplied evidence

The source set documents twelve works and installation views dated 2009–2023.
It identifies a practice built from publicly circulating photographs, camera-app
filter names, serial arrangements, and display situations. The supplied gallery
records identify Penelope Umbrico and credit the display files © Penelope Umbrico;
David B. Smith Gallery. They do not document an open licence, so every reproduced
plate in this chapter is used under the chapter's documented fair-use rationale in
[sources.md](sources.md).

## Technique and era

- *Suns from Sunsets from Flickr* (2018 and 2023) aggregates/resequences sunset
  photographs into gridded fields. The supplied 2018 image is an installation view;
  the 2023 image is a square serial field.
- *Everyone's Photos Any License* (2019) is presented as a long sequence of moon
  images. *Suns from Sunsets from Flickr — Out-takes/Birds (Blue)* (2018) is a
  blue twelve-image field in which bird silhouettes interrupt the repetitions.
- *Range: Masters of Photography* (2017) is shown as a gallery installation of
  reframed landscape images. *(Not) Easy Canvases (Kinkos)* (2013–2015) likewise
  makes its displayed supports, spacing, and seams visible. *TVs from Craigslist*
  (2009–2011) is a panoramic sequence of obsolete-screen photographs.
- The 2014 works identify camera-app effects in their titles: Calotype SepiaPlate,
  Oyster2, GrungeScratch, SplitScreen, LightLeak, Duotone6 Rusty and Burn,
  NightBokeh MultiExposure Quad, and LomoRedscale100 35mm SepiaVintageWarm.
  These names support discussion of visibly imposed color, divisions, and repeated
  exposures; they do not establish a camera body, capture settings, or the source
  photograph's original technical history.

## Printing and camera record

The supplied sources do not state print medium, paper, edition, dimensions of a
physical edition, camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO,
film stock, sensor format, lighting, or Umbrico's capture workflow for any plate.
The ingested display JPEGs contain no usable EXIF settings. This chapter therefore
does not assign a print process or photographic settings. Its observations concern
the visible reproduction or installation view, not a claim about the unverified
physical object.

## Image-analysis evidence

The analyzer measured best-fit thirds grids for plates 01, 02, 06, 07, 10, and 12,
and phi grids for plates 03, 04, 05, 08, 09, and 11. It found very low edge density
in the broad color fields—0.005 in plate 04 and 0.002 in plate 10—and substantially
more articulation in the moon installation (0.150), the multi-exposure image
(0.096), and the Kinkos installation (0.059). Those values describe the supplied
renditions only; they are used as visual evidence, not as technical specifications
of the artworks.
