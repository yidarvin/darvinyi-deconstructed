# Karl Ohiri — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Karl Ohiri (born 1983) is a living British-Nigerian artist. This twelve-image
set joins *The Archive of Becoming*—his recontextualisation of deteriorated Lagos
studio negatives—with *Equation for Humanity*, in which he photographs inscribed
stones, and six source portraits from the Lagos Studio Archives. MoMA identifies
the first work as “Courtesy of the artist. © Karl Ohiri”; Autograph captions the
five *Equation* prints “© and courtesy the artist.” The Lagos Studio Archive
source identifies its images as courtesy of the archive, with no named original
photographers or reuse licence.

No source grants an open licence. Every entry is therefore **fair-use**. Per
image, factor 1 favors this book’s criticism, scholarship, and transformative
composition teaching; factor 2 weighs against use because the image is creative
photography or a creative archival recontextualisation; factor 3 favors use
because its complete frame is needed to analyze the stated visual relationship;
and factor 4 favors use because this is a credited, source-linked, web-sized copy
that does not replace the original object, exhibition record, or licensed
reproduction. All files opened successfully; their long edges range from 807px
to 1,500px. None was upscaled or altered.

## Research context and acquisition conclusion

MoMA describes *The Archive of Becoming* as work made from discarded Lagos studio
negatives, where chemical deterioration is part of the new image logic. Autograph
states that Ohiri made *Equation for Humanity* during the COVID-19 lockdown by
collecting, inscribing, and photographing stones. The Lagos Studio Archives feature
explains its preservation of discarded studio negatives documenting Lagos
portraiture from the 1970s onward. This full 10–12-image set exceeds the registry
minimum of four, so no limited-source fallback is used.
