# Phillip Toledano — research note

## Scope and record

This sourced set is drawn from *Another America* (2023–2024), Phillip
Toledano’s project of AI-generated alternate histories that deliberately evoke
the 1940s and 1950s. The public LensCulture project page supplies the display
images and project framing; the publisher records the 2024 book release. The
period appearance is therefore a constructed visual language, not evidence that
these are archival photographs or scenes made in those decades. See [source
notes](sources.md).

## Method and medium

- In interviews, Toledano describes making the project through text prompts and
  iterative refinement in Midjourney, sometimes working for hours or days to
  narrow a result. The chapter consequently treats these plates as generated
  images, not as camera-captured events.
- The sources document an 80-page hardcover edition issued by L’Artiere in
  2024. They do not establish an individual print process, paper, print size,
  edition size, or output method for the selected images.
- The mid-century cues—period clothing, cars, monochrome or muted colour, haze,
  and city architecture—are analysed here as visible construction. They are not
  used to assign a historical location, date, or documentary truth to a scene.

## Settings and analytical limits

Camera settings are not applicable to these generated images, and no source
documents a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film
stock, exposure, or darkroom process. The ingested web JPEGs contain no usable
capture EXIF. Tonal statistics, palettes, and geometry in the chapter are
deterministic measurements of these supplied display files, not claims about an
original negative, sensor capture, or a definitive print colour.
