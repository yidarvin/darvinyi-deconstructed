# Joachim Schmid — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This twelve-image set samples the operational range of Schmid's found-image
practice: street-found photographs are sequenced as an archive; *Security Check*
uses the artist's own fragmentary transit pictures; and later works recirculate
webcam, satellite, and commercial-image material. The public artist page of
[Hollybush Gardens](https://hollybushgardens.co.uk/artists/joachim-schmid/) supplies
the selected display files, identifies the works and dates, and describes Schmid's
practice as recycling vernacular photography. Its displayed images were retrieved
directly, without login or technical-access circumvention. All opened successfully
after acquisition and measure 1,212–1,920px on their long edge, exceeding the
640px floor without upscaling.

The registry classifies the corpus as copyrighted. Every item below is therefore
used under an individual **fair-use** assessment. In each case the author/rightsholder
is Joachim Schmid (the gallery identifies the artist; no open licence is offered).
Factor 1 favors this nonprofit, transformative use for criticism, scholarship, and
composition teaching; factor 2 weighs against it because the work is creative;
factor 3 favors retaining the complete displayed frame because its composition is
the subject of the analysis; and factor 4 favors this limited web-sized, credited,
linked copy because it does not replace an original, gallery record, artist book, or
licensed reproduction. The work-specific applications of those four factors follow.
