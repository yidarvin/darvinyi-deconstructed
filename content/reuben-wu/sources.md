# Sources — Reuben Wu

## Acquisition and rights note

All twelve files below were downloaded as the artist's public 1200–1600px JPEG
display renditions from the indicated project page on 2026-08-03.  They open as
valid JPEGs and meet the 640px long-edge floor.  Reuben Wu's portfolio identifies
the projects and his biography identifies Wu as the artist; no open licence or
public-domain statement is offered for these contemporary photographs.  The
source route for every item is therefore **fair-use**, not `open`.

For each item, the four-factor assessment is: (1) this non-commercial book uses
the complete frame transformatively for criticism, scholarship, and composition
teaching; (2) the work is a highly creative photograph, which weighs against use;
(3) the complete frame is nevertheless necessary to teach the photograph's
spatial and light structure, and only a web-sized copy is retained; and (4) the
chapter credits and links to Wu's portfolio and does not replace the original,
museum record, or a licensed/editioned reproduction.  The individual entries
below identify the particular pedagogical need, so the determination is not
being presumed merely because the project is educational.

Portfolio context: [Lux Noctis](https://reubenwu.com/selected-works/lux-noctis/),
[Aeroglyphs](https://reubenwu.com/selected-works/aeroglyphs/),
[Siren](https://reubenwu.com/selected-works/siren/),
[The Sea of Ice](https://reubenwu.com/selected-works/the-sea-of-ice/), and
[artist biography](https://reubenwu.com/biography/). Credit/copyright notice for
all entries: **© Reuben Wu (artist's official portfolio; no open licence or reuse
permission stated).**
