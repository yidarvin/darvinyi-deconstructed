# Nan Goldin — build research

## What the records establish

The six selected Art Institute of Chicago objects span 1977 to 1989, with a 1978/95 *Self-Portrait* and the 1979/2001 installation of *The Ballad of Sexual Dependency*. The museum identifies the installation as 720 color slides with a programmed soundtrack and a 43-minute running time. It identifies the other five selected works as silver dye-bleach prints; *Self-Portrait* is specifically sixteen silver dye-bleach prints, edition three of three. These are object-record facts, not deductions from the display JPEGs. [Source notes](sources.md)

The collection records give the following display media and dimensions:

- *Kim and Mark in the Red Car, Newton, MA* (1987): silver dye-bleach print; image 40.4 × 59.8 cm, paper 50.9 × 61 cm.
- *Self-Portrait* (1978/95): sixteen silver dye-bleach prints; 92 × 138 cm.
- *J. and Richard, Chicago* (1977): silver dye-bleach print; image 39.2 × 59.7 cm, paper 50.7 × 60.8 cm.
- *Suzanne Crying, NYC* (1985): silver dye-bleach print; image 39.5 × 60 cm, paper 50.9 × 61 cm.
- *Clarence in the Window with Gun* (1989): silver dye-bleach print; image 59.5 × 40 cm, paper 60.9 × 50.9 cm.

MoMA describes *The Ballad of Sexual Dependency* as an evolving slideshow of candid color photographs made from about 1979 to 2004. That supports discussing sequence, color, and a diaristic social world; it does not turn the six museum display files into technical documentation. [Source notes](sources.md)

## Technical limits

The supplied museum records establish the print media above, but do not document a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, film stock, ISO, negative format, lighting setup, or exposure settings for these individual photographs. The ingested web files have empty EXIF fields. The chapter can therefore discuss visible flash-like directness, color, crop, scale, and the measured tonal and palette data, but it must not infer capture settings or name a camera process from appearance alone.
