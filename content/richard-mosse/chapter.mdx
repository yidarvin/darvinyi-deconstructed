Richard Mosse's pictures make technical vision conspicuous. In this sourced selection from *Broken Spectre*, landscape is not a neutral field: an arm with a tool, a flooded chapel, a saw, a blaze, and aerial channels each show a system of looking becoming part of the image. The source record documents the 2018–22 project as a four-channel work using multispectral and infrared imaging at several scales. The overlays stay with visible structures in these supplied whole-frame display files; they do not claim to recover a hidden camera setting or turn false colour into a transparent fact. [Source notes](../../content/richard-mosse/sources.md)

## pressure across a panoramic strip

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="01-broken-spectre-still-01" label="Broken Spectre (still 01), 2018–2022" caption="An outstretched arm, the horizontal tool beneath it, and a left hand make a wide surveillance-derived strip read as one directed action." />

Still 01 makes its long format feel cramped rather than expansive. The arm crosses from the cropped body at left to the right-hand grip, while the tool gives that gesture a second, nearly horizontal register. The overlay names those tangible lines instead of accepting a detector's nominal horizon in the blurred background. Its grayscale rendition has mean luminance 0.490, p05 0.122, p95 0.827, and a very low 0.025 edge density; gray #5c5d5f, #a0a1a3, and near-black #28292b leave bodily force clearer than setting.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="03-broken-spectre-still-03" label="Broken Spectre (still 03), 2018–2022" caption="A chainsaw and the nearby cut line meet a trunk that fills the right side, making the frame a collision between labour and obstruction." />

The third still turns the act into a hard division. The worker and chainsaw are anchored just left of the immense trunk; the trunk is a literal internal frame, not a decorative border. Mean luminance is 0.394, with p05 0.125 and p95 0.882, while edge density rises to 0.105. The palette of dark #2b2c2e, mid-gray #505153, and pale #e1e2e3 keeps the saw's pale body legible against the bark's dense tonal mass.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="05-broken-spectre-still-05" label="Broken Spectre (still 05), 2018–2022" caption="A cable sweeps across a nearly white field, dividing faint figures at left from a dark opening above the frame's centre." />

The fifth strip withholds ordinary description most aggressively. A cable curves through a nearly blank field; a black aperture and half-visible figures become the only firm events. Its mean luminance reaches 0.840 and edge density falls to 0.013, with #edeeef and #d0d1d3 dominating against #09090a. The few dark forms feel less like details than interruptions in an image that has almost washed itself away.

## evidence that glows or burns

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="02-broken-spectre-still-02" label="Broken Spectre (still 02), 2018–2022" caption="A floodwater edge steadies a small chapel while its sloping roof rises into clouds, lens flare, and the cross at far right." />

Still 02 stages its chapel against weather that seems too active to be a background. The waterline is the picture's one reliable horizontal; from it, the roof rises toward the cross and a measured light flare. The image is darker than its flaring sky implies: mean 0.335, p05 0.102, p95 0.839, and 0.119 edge density. Tree, roof, and reflection compete as sharply articulated evidence rather than settle into a pastoral scene.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="04-broken-spectre-still-04" label="Broken Spectre (still 04), 2018–2022" caption="A fallen foreground branch crosses the fireline, keeping a blaze behind a dense, dark screen of limbs." />

The fourth still refuses a distant, spectacular view of fire. A low fireline is repeatedly cut by the branch and foreground tangle, so the blaze is seen through obstruction. This is the set's widest tonal swing: p05 0.039 and p95 0.941 around a mean of 0.348. Its edge density of 0.146 explains why the white fire does not flatten the picture: #151617 branches and #e6e7e8 flare remain in continuous contention.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="06-broken-spectre-still-06" label="Broken Spectre (still 06), 2018–2022" caption="A serrated rhythm of palms and a low ground silhouette hold small bright openings inside an otherwise dark panoramic field." />

The sixth still compresses a forest into a nearly theatrical silhouette. The ground line and irregular palm rhythm do not promise a clean horizon; they enclose small bright breaks in cloud and flame. Mean luminance is only 0.249, with p05 0.035 and p95 0.573. The narrow #121314-to-#a3a4a6 grayscale palette and 0.088 edge density make visibility itself feel conditional.

## fields, surfaces, and aerial systems

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="07-domesticated-palms-amazonas" label="Domesticated Palms, Amazonas, 2020" caption="A mist boundary, reflected water channel, and repeated palms make wet ground read as a measured field rather than an untouched grove." />

*Domesticated Palms, Amazonas* uses repetition without making the grove orderly. Tall trunks climb through mist, then continue as unstable reflections in the water channel. The measured mist boundary and vertical channel hold that doubled depth in place. Mean luminance is 0.317, p05 0.047, p95 0.761, and edge density 0.130; #151515, #666361, and #dbdada make dark trunks and pale atmosphere share one constrained monochrome scale.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="08-burnt-pantanal-i" label="Artist-site display file listed as Burnt Pantanal I, 2020" caption="The supplied aerial-looking file is deliberately given only a conservative thirds grid and a bright left-bank detail: its competing channels do not support one clean directional claim." />

The source notes list this display file as *Burnt Pantanal I*, but the supplied rendition visibly presents a high aerial field of channels and coloured banks. Rather than force a false route through it, the overlay retains the measured grid and one bright left-bank detail; it is also logged for automatic re-audit. Its analyzer reading is dense, with edge density 0.277, mean luminance 0.252, and a compressed p05–p95 range of 0.051–0.514. Dark blue #151c27, blue-gray #334048, violet #631a54, and #5d8ea8 make the palette an active layer of information, not naturalistic description.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="09-bobo-43392" label="Broken Spectre artist-site display file bobo43392, 2018–2022" caption="Two curving surface contours and a blue-violet patch turn a shallow, near-black image into an oblique field of colour." />

The uncaptioned file identified as `bobo43392` is a close, oblique surface, not a landscape to be mapped. Two visible ridges carry the eye over a blue-violet patch before the image drops into blackness. Its mean is 0.193, p05 is 0.000, and p95 only 0.400, but the 0.157 edge density keeps the surface alive. The analyzer's #060a09, #2e4860, #5e4840, and #556d99 palette records how much color survives inside that dark value range.

<Plate slug="richard-mosse" id="10-broken-spectre-gis-aerial-01" label="Broken Spectre multispectral GIS aerial display file, 2018–2022" caption="Turquoise river curves distribute attention through a red false-colour canopy, making the aerial view read as both terrain and an encoded map." />

The final aerial file makes a river system visible as an invented palette. Rather than reduce it to one vanishing point, the overlay follows two actual curves and marks their fork. The analyzer records 0.247 edge density, mean luminance 0.341, and p05–p95 of 0.082–0.773. Rust #843629, dark #3c1916, salmon #c86a51, pale #b7dae4, and blue #63929b separate channel from canopy, but the project context cautions against treating those colors as ordinary landscape truth.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Let a tool set the frame">
Photograph an action with a tool, cable, rail, or other long object. Keep the person partly cropped if the object's direction still connects hand, body, and the edge of the frame. Mark only lines that physically exist in the image.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Make visibility conditional">
Work at dusk, in smoke, mist, glare, or deep shadow. Hold one definite horizontal or silhouette band, then allow the rest of the scene to remain partially unreadable. Compare the result with a corrected exposure and decide which better preserves the pressure of looking.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Treat false colour as structure">
Use an existing colour translation such as an infrared mode, screen rendering, map layer, or unusual white balance, without adding an arbitrary filter. Identify which colour boundary changes the picture's directional hierarchy, and explain what information it reveals or conceals.
</ExerciseCard>
