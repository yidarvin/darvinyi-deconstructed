# Danielle Ezzo — research note

## Documented context

The supplied source record identifies these ten works as Danielle Ezzo and Marin
Sardy's ongoing *Psychic Telephone* (2024–). Sardy's interviews about psychic
experiences are distilled into texts. Ezzo uses deliberately low-resolution
text-to-image output as a prompt and makes the final works as camera-made
photographs. The project record is the source for that account, the work titles,
and the collaboration credit. [Source record](sources.md)

## Process and technical limits

The available record documents an AI-assisted ideation stage followed by
camera-made final photographs. It does not identify a camera body, lens, focal
length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, sensor, lighting setup,
printing process, paper, or edition. The ten normalized display JPEGs carry no
capture EXIF. This chapter therefore discusses documented process and visible
structure, not inferred settings or a specific print method.

## Visual evidence used in the chapter

The analyzer finds a deliberately broad range of picture-making approaches:
near-black leaf relief in *Droplets at Night* (mean luminance 0.067), a sparse
studio flare in *Prism in Studio* (edge density 0.038), and an extremely quiet
high-key *Duskpan* (edge density 0.0014). The measured palettes are descriptive
web-image findings, not claims about original print color. They support the
chapter's account of value, surface, object scale, and constructed space.
