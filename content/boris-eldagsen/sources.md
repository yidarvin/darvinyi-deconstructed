# Boris Eldagsen — source shortlist

## Acquisition and rights record

This twelve-image study set is drawn from Eldagsen’s artist-hosted
[*PSEUDOMNESIA: Fake Memories* page](https://www.eldagsen.com/pseudomnesia/),
which dates the project 2022–23, describes the images as co-produced with AI
generators, and identifies their workflow of prompting, inpainting, and
outpainting. The same page carries `Copyright Boris Eldagsen 2026. All Rights
Reserved` and offers limited-edition prints through Photo Edition Berlin. No
open licence is claimed. Each direct public display file below opened after
download and has a long edge of at least 640px; none was upscaled, altered, or
captured from a screen.

**Use basis: fair-use for every image below.** Creator and known rightsholder
notice: Boris Eldagsen; the artist page states `Copyright Boris Eldagsen 2026.
All Rights Reserved.` For each distinct work, factor 1 favors this nonprofit,
transformative use for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; factor
2 recognizes a highly creative, contemporary promptographic image; factor 3
uses the complete frame only because framing, staging, and spatial relation are
the lesson; factor 4 favors the limited web-sized, credited, linked copy, which
does not replace the artist’s page, an original, a Photo Edition Berlin print,
or a licensed reproduction. The image-specific entries state the particular
composition requiring the complete frame.

- **Why it matters:** The nested pairing of a young foreground figure and an older figure behind it creates an uneasy vertical relay, making resemblance and temporal ambiguity compositional rather than merely narrative.
- **Source:** [Artist project page](https://www.eldagsen.com/pseudomnesia/); [direct public display file](https://www.eldagsen.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/eldagsen_THEELECTRICIAN.jpg).
- **Rights / fair-use assessment:** **fair-use**. The full portrait frame is necessary to analyze the spacing, mirroring, and tonal separation of the two figures; the 703 × 1200px credited display copy is used only for that transformative analysis and is non-substitutive under the four factors described above.
- **File:** `raw/boris-eldagsen/01-the-electrician.jpg` (703 × 1200px)
