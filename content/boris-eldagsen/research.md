# Boris Eldagsen — build research

## Scope and documented method

The supplied artist archive identifies *PSEUDOMNESIA: Fake Memories* as a
2022–23 project and describes the works as co-produced with AI generators.
Its documented workflow is prompting, inpainting, and outpainting. That is the
relevant technical record for this chapter: the pictures deliberately borrow
the tonal staging, portrait lighting, period costume, soft focus, and physical
wear associated with older photographic objects while remaining synthetic
constructions.

The chapter treats the artist-hosted files as contemporary promptographic
images, not as historical photographs. Titles, dates, production method,
rights notice, acquisition route, and image-specific fair-use assessments are
recorded in `sources.md` from the artist's project page.

## Camera, exposure, and printing record

No camera, lens, film stock, sensor, exposure, lighting setup, print process,
edition, prompt text, model, seed, or post-production sequence is documented
in the supplied source record. The twelve normalized web files have empty EXIF
objects in `manifest.json`. Accordingly, the chapter makes no claim about
camera settings or printing. References to grain, scratches, monochrome, blur,
or period-looking light describe visible image effects only, not an asserted
analogue process.

## Measured visual character

The deterministic analyses show consistently restrained monochrome or
near-monochrome palettes, with deep black/grey values especially in the 2023
square images. The 2022 selection ranges from the low-edge-density portrait
field of *The Electrician* (0.0229) to the more crowded *The Leftovers*
(0.0568); the later squares remain structurally legible through broad tonal
splits and strong local highlights rather than through documentary detail.
These measurements support readings of theatrical enclosure and carefully
distributed attention, while not proving the historical sources the pictures
evoke.
