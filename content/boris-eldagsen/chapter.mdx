# Boris Eldagsen: memories that refuse their evidence

Boris Eldagsen's *PSEUDOMNESIA: Fake Memories* (2022–23) makes period-looking imagery without asking it to certify a past. The artist's project page identifies the images as co-produced with AI generators through prompting, inpainting, and outpainting. That history matters: the soft monochrome, old clothes, scratches, and theatrical light are visible effects inside constructed pictures, not evidence of an old camera or a recovered archive. The artist-hosted display files appear here for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching under the image-specific fair-use assessments in the [source notes](../../content/boris-eldagsen/sources.md).

## a photograph-shaped stage

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="01-the-electrician" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | The Electrician, 2022" caption="Two faces, three hands, and a descending dress fold turn resemblance into a relay." />

*The Electrician* gives its foreground figure a clean, frontal claim, then lets a second face and hands surface directly behind her. The overlay's shoulder relay at y0.649 and dress fold turn that vertical stack into a circulation of touch rather than a simple double portrait. Its edge density is only 0.0229, with olive-grey #73735e, dark #1f2118, and pale #d1c7ac doing most of the work: the surrounding blur leaves the paired faces unusually exposed.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="02-the-deal" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | The Deal, 2022" caption="The object held at centre makes an extended hand feel like a proposition across the room." />

In *The Deal*, a man, the vertical object, and a hand entering from the right become one broken path. The horizontal shoulder-to-object band at y0.369 keeps the wide frame from dissolving into empty wall; the overlay's polyline makes the exchange visible without pretending that the image records an actual scene. Mean luminance 0.5324 and edge density 0.0196 confirm the unusually open tonal field, built from tan #bdaa8a, dark olive #26230b, and pale #ead2ad.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="03-the-leftovers" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | The Leftovers, 2022" caption="Three children occupy one narrow stage, but their faces and hands never settle into a unified group." />

*The Leftovers* makes instability out of a nearly level lineup. The left-face anchor, middle-face anchor, and low hands-and-bodies band show how the eye bounces between three independent expressions before it can read a family. The analyzer finds the highest edge density of this opening group (0.0568) and a broad tonal span from p05 0.0824 to p95 0.9098; black-green #1c211b, pale #e2e0d9, and grey #6a6b61 sharpen the crowding.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="04-the-connaisseur" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | The Connaisseur, 2022" caption="An empty inner picture and an entering hand make the seated man look watched by the frame itself." />

The wide crop of *The Connaisseur* is a lesson in withholding. The overlay marks the pale picture-within-the-picture and traces the route from pipe to empty frame; the man sits left of the whiteness, while the entering hand activates the far edge. With p05 0.0863 and p95 0.9608, the picture has the sharpest tonal range in this group, but its edge density remains a moderate 0.0362. Black #222016, pale #f5ece4, and silver-grey #8c8375 make the blank interior a compositional object rather than an absence.

## squares that press inward

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="05-eternity" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | Eternity, 2022" caption="A pale bed opens across the frame while bodies and a dark, snake-like contour set its unequal balance." />

*Eternity* turns a broad bed into a low theatre. The dark-back-wall line at y0.269 separates the bed from its recess; the measured balance axis at x0.375 and the right-figure anchor make the left woman's open mouth and the right masked body pull against each other through the open centre. A mean luminance of 0.5404 and pale #e3d7c4 give the linen its generous field, while dark #292918 and olive #4f4b36 keep the contour from becoming decorative.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="06-the-memory" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | The Memory, 2023" caption="Two blades cross before a guarded face, turning portrait lighting into an emphatic X." />

In *The Memory*, the two scoreable blade edges meet at the face-and-blade crossing near x0.402, y0.367. The folded-arm base at y0.691 closes the lower half, so the eye cannot leave the figure through ordinary portrait space. Mean luminance is only 0.2383 and the palette is a compressed ladder from #121212 to #b6b6b6; the contrast makes the white sleeve and paired diagonals read as a graphic construction rather than as available depth.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="07-the-breath" label="PSEUDOMNESIA | The Breath, 2023" caption="A covered face, pale window, and low tonal break compress the figure inside the square." />

*The Breath* uses tonal geometry rather than a literal narrative: the overlay centers the covered face at x0.507, y0.304 and isolates the window light at the right edge. The lower tonal break at y0.854 seals the figure into the square rather than opening it into a room. The high edge density of 0.0688 is driven by hair, cloth, and window edges, while #0a0a0a, #515150, and #d8d8d8 stage a hard transition from black enclosure to white glare.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="08-love" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | Love, 2023" caption="A lowered face, wound cloth, and sunlit bib make intimacy feel deliberately obstructed." />

The square of *Love* is divided into competing surfaces rather than stable anatomy. The overlay's path names the masked figure, while the forehead highlight at x0.556, y0.169 and the seat edge at y0.717 give the white cloth and black garment separate weights. Its low edge density (0.0216) and long tonal reach from p05 0.0157 to p95 0.9020 make the soft greys (#121212, #8f8f8e, #e2e2e2) feel more like a staged lighting arrangement than a description of a place.

## containment, rupture, care

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="09-the-illusion" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | The Illusion, 2023" caption="A person reaches toward a luminous sphere whose scale makes the room look provisional." />

*The Illusion* makes an internal object so large that it becomes the real frame. The ellipse and x0.562 globe axis hold the white sphere, while the floor-and-curtain band at y0.570 leaves the small figure visibly outside its centre. The palette runs from #1c1c18 through #7b7a75 to #f3f1ec, with a modest edge density of 0.0227; that restraint lets the enormous smooth mass carry the visual argument.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="10-the-confession" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | The Confession, 2023" caption="A bowed face and torn cloth direct attention to a small dark opening amid bright, theatrical skin." />

*The Confession* uses the body as a system of barriers. The bowed-face anchor, cord diagonal, and cloth opening near x0.440, y0.539 make the viewer repeatedly return to the small black rupture instead of treating the portrait as a whole. At mean luminance 0.2466, it is mostly charcoal #151515 and mid-grey #434341, but the p95 value of 0.6667 keeps the cloth and arms vivid enough to press inward.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="11-the-mask" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | The Mask, 2023" caption="Hands stretch a blank mask across a face, making concealment more concrete than the concealed person." />

The overlay for *The Mask* places the top edge at y0.142, its centre at x0.574, y0.463, and the dark opening lower and farther right at x0.660, y0.560. Those marks clarify the composition's displacement: fingers and fabric take over the job of expression, while the face is relegated to a missing volume. Mean luminance 0.3059 and the close range from #151413 to #bab9b5 preserve the material tension of the grey fabric without requiring colour as a cue.

<Plate slug="boris-eldagsen" id="12-the-torso" label="PSEUDOMNESIA, Part II | The Torso, 2023" caption="An attendant leans toward a reclining figure, turning the bed into a compressed, diagonal stage." />

*The Torso* finishes the group by trading mask and prop for a charged relation between two bodies. The head-and-shoulder band crosses the picture at y0.431; the right arm and bed folds pull down toward the measured point at x0.748, y0.752. At mean luminance 0.4370 and edge density 0.0468, white #dfdfdf, mid-grey #a7a7a7, and black #141413 keep that diagonal exchange legible even as the picture withholds a stable narrative.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make the prop conduct attention">
Build a staged portrait around one object held between people. Do not centre the object automatically: test whether it can make the eye travel through hands, faces, and the frame edge instead.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Use an inner frame as pressure">
Photograph a figure beside a window, mirror, empty panel, or bright screen. Make the internal rectangle active enough to compete with the face, then decide which boundary tells the story.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Make a fiction acknowledge its construction">
Arrange a scene that suggests a familiar photographic past, then introduce one compositional contradiction: an impossible prop, an implausible scale shift, or a seam that keeps the image from becoming simple nostalgia.
</ExerciseCard>
