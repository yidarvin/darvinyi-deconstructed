# Carmen Winant — research notes

## Scope of the available record

The source record identifies twelve public documentation images from projects
dated 2018–2023: *My Birth* (MoMA, 2018), *Lesbian Land* (Columbus Museum of
Art, 2018), *Body Index* (Stene Projects, 2018), *Introduction to
Consciousness Raising* (Bemis Center, 2021), *A History of My Pleasure*
(2019–20; MoCP lecture record, 2021), and *My Mother and Eye* (FOMU, 2023).
They are installation and detail views made available through the artist's
official archive; this chapter therefore analyzes the pictured arrangements and
their documentation, not an unverified single-camera series.

## Technique and format

Across the selected views, Winant's documented method is montage at
architectural scale: found and personal photographs, printed matter, text, and
image fragments are repeated, layered, pinned, or arranged on panels. The
source record specifically describes *My Birth* as 3,059 found papers and
photographic prints. The views of *Lesbian Land*, *Body Index*, and the later
projects similarly show serial arrays, walls, boards, or freestanding clear
supports. That visible format makes spacing, panel edges, and the exhibition
room part of the compositional evidence.

## Printing and capture information

The source record verifies the projects, venues, dates, and public display
files, but does not identify a uniform photographic process, printing process,
paper, ink, edition, camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed,
film stock, sensitivity, or lighting setup for this twelve-image selection.
The normalized files' palette and tonal measurements describe those web
documentation images only; they are not evidence about the original prints or
the equipment that made the constituent photographs.
