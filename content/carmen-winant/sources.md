# Carmen Winant — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Carmen Winant is a living artist and the registry classifies this corpus as
copyrighted. This twelve-image working set concentrates on the artist's
installation practice: walls of found and authored photographs in which serial
accumulation, gaps, scale, and the viewer's body are part of the image's
meaning. The files were downloaded directly from the public image service used
by [Winant's official archive](https://www.carmenwinant.com/); that archive
identifies the project, venue, and year for each group. The three *My Birth*
views are additionally anchored by [MoMA's object record](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/222741), which identifies the 2018 work, its 3,059 found
papers and photographic prints, and the notice `Copyright © 2026 Carmen
Winant`.

The archive makes these files publicly viewable but does not grant an open
licence. Every entry is therefore assessed as **fair-use**, not as an open
download. The named creator/rightsholder is Carmen Winant, and the official
archive supplies no separate reuse licence (MoMA supplies the explicit
copyright notice for *My Birth*). For each image: **factor 1:** this book uses
the image for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching;
**factor 2:** it is a creative contemporary photographic installation, which
weighs against use; **factor 3:** the complete displayed view is necessary to
analyze its spatial, serial, and framing decisions; **factor 4:** only a
web-sized, credited copy is used, linked back to the artist's archive and
relevant museum record, and it does not replace an original work, exhibition
record, artist's book, or licensed reproduction.

All downloaded files opened successfully. Their long edges range from 1,440px
to 5,393px, so each exceeds the 640px source floor without upscaling or image
alteration.
