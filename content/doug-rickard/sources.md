# Doug Rickard — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This source pass centers on seven named works displayed on the Museum of
Modern Art's public *New Photography 2011* project page. MoMA describes the
series as images Rickard composed from Google Street View on a computer screen,
then rephotographed and cropped panoramically; it identifies the work's concerns
as poverty, racial equity, online imagery, and privacy. The separate MoMA
collection record for *#82.948842, Detroit, MI. 2009* identifies the medium as
an inkjet print, credits it as a gift of the artist, and carries the notice
“Copyright © 2026 Doug Rickard.”

Each public exhibition rendition below was downloaded directly from MoMA's
publicly accessible exhibition server without a login or technical bypass, and
opened successfully. They are 479–483px on the long edge. This is below the
normal 640px floor but within the policy's rare 480–639px exception. They are
compositionally essential, public,
uncropped MoMA renditions: horizon, street recession, blurred figures, building
masses, and the deliberately wide frame remain legible. No file has been
upscaled, retouched, or otherwise altered. A stronger public rendition was not
advertised on the exhibition page; the primary MoMA collection record also says
that its image is not available. The set therefore preserves the institution's
available reference scale rather than manufacturing detail.

All seven uses are individual **fair-use** assessments. Creator/rightsholder
notice where identified: **© Doug Rickard** (MoMA collection record). For each,
(1) this book uses the web-sized image for criticism, scholarship, and
transformative composition teaching; (2) it is a creative, copyrighted
photograph, which weighs against use; (3) the complete panoramic frame is needed
to analyze the named relationships rather than display a decorative crop; and
(4) the linked, credited public exhibition-sized copy neither replaces the MoMA
record, artist's original print, nor a licensed reproduction.

## Acquired shortlist

1. **#29.942566, New Orleans, LA. 2008, 2009** —
   `raw/doug-rickard/01-29942566-new-orleans-2008-2009.jpg` (482 × 300px)
   - **Why it matters:** The empty road, low sidewalk figures, utility poles,
     and dramatic sky create a lesson in a vanishing point held against a broad,
     vulnerable foreground.
   - **Source:** MoMA, [*New Photography 2011* exhibition image](http://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/newphotography/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/29.942566-New-Orleans-LA-482x300.jpg), indexed on the [Doug Rickard exhibition page](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/newphotography/doug-rickard/).
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use.** The whole frame is necessary to discuss the
     road's recession, small figures, and overhead-wire rhythm under the
     four-factor assessment stated above. Credit: Doug Rickard; Museum of Modern
     Art, New York; © Doug Rickard.

2. **#32.700542, Dallas, TX. 2009** —
   `raw/doug-rickard/02-32700542-dallas-2009.jpg` (482 × 300px)
   - **Why it matters:** Its horizontal bands of pavement, façades, and sky make
     an intentionally low-resolution street scene read as layered color fields.
   - **Source:** MoMA, [public exhibition rendition](http://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/newphotography/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/32.700542-Dallas-TX.-2009-482x300.jpg), [exhibition context](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/newphotography/doug-rickard/).
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use.** The full panorama is needed to teach the
     stacked bands and broad lateral balance under the stated four-factor
     assessment. Credit: Doug Rickard; Museum of Modern Art, New York; © Doug
     Rickard.

3. **#34.546147, Helena-West Helena, AR. 2008, 2010** —
   `raw/doug-rickard/03-34546147-helena-west-helena-2008-2010.jpg` (483 × 300px)
   - **Why it matters:** A small teal house is held between winter trees and a
     glowing horizon, turning a modest structure into an off-center color anchor.
   - **Source:** MoMA, [public exhibition rendition](http://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/newphotography/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/34.546147-Helena-West-Helena-AR-483x300.jpg); corroborating [Davidson College collection note](https://www.davidsoncollegeartgalleries.org/news/seen-in-storage-doug-rickard/) identifies the work as an archival pigment print from *A New American Picture*.
   - **Use basis:** **fair-use.** The complete frame is required to examine the
     house/horizon displacement and tree silhouette under the stated
     four-factor assessment. Credit: Doug Rickard; Museum of Modern Art, New
     York; © Doug Rickard.
