# Aziz + Cucher — research note

## What the supplied sources establish

The supplied artist archive identifies the selected sequence as *Dystopia*
(1994–95), *Chimeras* (1998), and *Interiors* (1999–2001). SFMOMA describes
*Dystopia* as digitally altered portraiture whose removals make personal
identity obsolete, while the Wexner Center describes the later rooms and found
objects as digitally manipulated and wrapped in human skin. Together, those
records support a reading of this chapter as an investigation of how digital
alteration makes bodies, objects, and rooms exchange visual roles. [Source
notes](sources.md)

The supplied archive identifies *Chimera #1*, *Chimera #6*, and *Chimera #8*
as Light Jet c-prints. It identifies *Interior #1* and *Interior #5* as Light
Jet C-prints. The supplied record does not provide a corresponding
work-specific print-medium statement for the five *Dystopia* portraits, so this
chapter does not assign one to them.

## Technical limits for this chapter

The supplied records do not document camera body, lens, focal length, aperture,
shutter speed, sensitivity, film or sensor format, lighting scheme, capture
workflow, or the retouching software and procedure for the selected works. The
ingested public display JPEGs contain no camera EXIF. This chapter therefore
discusses only the documented digital alteration and print facts above, together
with visible crop, contour, measured tone, palette, and symmetry. It does not
infer settings or manufacture a general print process from web derivatives.
