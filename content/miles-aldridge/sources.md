# Miles Aldridge — source record

## Selection and rights note

This 2003–2017 sequence follows Aldridge's signature use of precisely staged
interiors, acid colour, role-playing figures, and cinematic mise-en-scène. The
[official artist archive](https://milesaldridge.com/works/) identifies the titles,
dates, and print medium and publicly displays the complete-frame JPEG renditions
acquired below. Its site footer carries the notice **© 2025 Miles Aldridge**; no
open licence is offered. Every selection is therefore a separate, documented
**fair-use** assessment, not an implied licence. The downloaded files are the
archive's publicly reachable source renditions, opened after acquisition without
alteration; each is 4,000px on its long edge. The later build will make only a
web-sized analytical rendition.

For each item: factor 1 favours this nonprofit criticism, scholarship, and
transformative composition teaching; factor 2 weighs against use because it is a
creative contemporary photograph; factor 3 favours use only insofar as the complete
frame is necessary to analyze the stated relationships; and factor 4 favours use
because the credited, linked web-sized teaching rendition does not substitute for an
original, the artist's archive, a book, print, or licensed reproduction.
