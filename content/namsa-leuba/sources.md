# Namsa Leuba — source record

## Selection and rights note

This is a twelve-image, artist-archive-led selection from four defining projects:
*Khoisan*, *Weke*, *Zulu Kids*, and *The Kingdom of Mountains*. The public project
pages identify the named works, dates, and series context, and each linked direct
JPEG was retrieved from the official site's public media server without a login,
paywall, or other access-control bypass. Every file opened successfully after
download; the shortest long edge is 2,584px, so all exceed the 640px floor without
upscaling or alteration.

All selections are **fair-use**. Namsa Leuba is the identified artist, and the
official archive credits her; no open licence is offered. Each item
below records a separate four-factor assessment: (1) this book's web-sized use is
transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; (2) the work is a
creative contemporary photograph, which weighs against use; (3) the complete frame
is required for the stated visual analysis rather than a decorative excerpt; and
(4) the credited, linked public copy is a limited teaching use and does not replace
an original, exhibition, print, monograph, artist page, or licensed reproduction.

## Acquisition record

The official pages describe the relevant series: *Khoisan* is a staged project made
in South Africa's Northern Cape; *Weke* stages narratives informed by Beninese
animist and Vodou cosmology; *Zulu Kids* was made with Zulu communities in KwaZulu-
Natal and Soweto; and *The Kingdom of Mountains* concerns traditions and aesthetics
around Lesotho's Katse Dam. This source pass acquired twelve real, verified,
complete-frame photographs—well above the registry minimum of four. No
limited-source fallback is used.
