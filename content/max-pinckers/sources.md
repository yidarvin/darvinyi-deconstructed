# Max Pinckers — sources

Max Pinckers’ public artist archive is the primary source for this selection. Its project pages identify the bodies of work and expose publicly reachable, complete-frame JPEG display renditions. The selection concentrates on the constructed/documentary tension across *Will They Sing Like Raindrops or Leave Me Thirsty* (2014), *Two Kinds of Memory and Memory Itself* (2015), and *Red Ink* (2018). The artist’s page describes the India project as a study of love, caste violence, the Love Commandos, and cinematic imagination; the archive is therefore essential context as well as the source of the files.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The archive identifies Max Pinckers as the artist; the images are copyrighted contemporary works (credit: © Max Pinckers). Each entry below makes an individual four-factor assessment: this nonprofit book uses a complete, web-sized frame for transformative criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching; its full frame is needed to discuss staging, spatial direction, colour, and visual evidence; the copy is limited to the archive’s public display rendition; and the chapter credits and links to the artist’s project page and does not replace an original, a licensed print, a book, or the artist’s archive.
