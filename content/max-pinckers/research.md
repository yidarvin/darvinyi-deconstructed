# Max Pinckers — research note

## Scope and evidence

This chapter examines eleven supplied public display JPEGs from Max Pinckers’
artist archive. The source record identifies five plates from *Will They Sing
Like Raindrops or Leave Me Thirsty* (2014), four from *Two Kinds of Memory and
Memory Itself* (2015), and two from *Red Ink* (2018). The project pages and
the artist biography/bibliography are the basis for those titles and dates;
they do not assign a further title or date to every individual frame. The
source notes also point to contemporary reporting that describes the India
project through love, caste violence, the Love Commandos, and cinematic
imagination. This chapter consequently calls the pictured situations staged
or theatrical only where the source record supports that larger
documentary/constructed tension. See [sources.md](sources.md).

## Method and era

These are contemporary colour photographs made for project-based bodies of
work in 2014, 2015, and 2018. The documented method relevant to this chapter
is the relation between social subject matter and cinematic-looking staging;
the supplied frames make that relation visible through centred props, built
openings, controlled figures, strong shadows, and carefully held perspective.
That is a reading of these web reproductions, not a claim that every element
was directed or that any pictured event is fictional.

## Printing and camera settings

The available source record does not identify camera body, lens, focal length,
aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting equipment, film stock or sensor,
capture workflow, print process, paper, edition, or print dimensions for these
eleven photographs. The ingested manifest contains no retained capture EXIF.
It would be inaccurate to infer settings from the finished images or to treat a
web-display JPEG as evidence about an original print.

## Reading the reproductions

The images are credited, web-sized fair-use reproductions for criticism,
scholarship, and composition teaching. Analyzer measurements in this chapter
describe the supplied display files only: their palette, tonal percentiles,
edge density, and measured geometry help make the visual argument, but do not
establish the colour, scale, surface, or material quality of an original print.
