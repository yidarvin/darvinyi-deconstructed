# Bas Uterwijk (Ganbrood) — research note

## Record and method

Bas Uterwijk (born 1968, Amsterdam), working as Ganbrood, describes a background
in special effects, 3D animation, videogame development, and photography. His
artist statement says that since 2019 his focus has been algorithmic image
synthesis: images positioned between constructed imagery and photographic
perception that "have never been captured by a camera." This selection spans
2021–2024, except the 2020 Napoleon work. The artist site establishes the titles
and dates for works 01–11. [Ganbrood artist site](https://ganbrood.com/)

For *An artist’s impression of Napoleon I produced using a neural network*
(2020), Fondation Napoléon records the technique as an image created with
computer-learning software. Its account of Uterwijk’s method specifies
deep-learning/Artbreeder tools trained on faces and the use of historical
paintings and sculptures as inputs; the artist calls the result an artistic
impression rather than a claim of historical accuracy. That work-specific
procedure is not generalized here to the other eleven images.
[Fondation Napoléon record](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/images/an-artists-impression-of-napoleon-produced-by-artificial-intelligence-may-2020/)

## Production limits

The consulted records do not establish a print process, paper, edition size,
physical dimensions, camera body, lens, focal length, exposure, lighting setup,
or other camera settings for these individual works. The ingested web JPEGs have
no retained EXIF settings. This chapter therefore calls them synthetic images,
not camera photographs, and confines technical claims to the documented
algorithmic/computer-learning method and to measured visible image structure.
