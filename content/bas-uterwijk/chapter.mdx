Bas Uterwijk’s images, made under the name Ganbrood, make photographic belief feel like a compositional achievement rather than a camera guarantee. A face-like anchor, a balanced axis, a dense silhouette, a panel rhythm, or a quiet field asks for recognition before the image permits it to settle. The artist describes this work as algorithmic image synthesis rather than camera capture; *Napoleon I* makes the historical stakes unusually explicit. [Source notes](../../content/bas-uterwijk/sources.md)

## belief assembled from parts

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="01-dream-of-europa" label="The Dream of Europa, 2021" caption="Two pale vertical divisions split a continuous synthetic field into three adjacent figure zones, while a low shared base turns their repeated bodies into a lateral rhythm." />

*The Dream of Europa* does not make one stable creature the subject. The overlay follows the two pale divisions and the low run of hooves and feet, letting each zone remain partly autonomous. Its relatively high edge density (0.170) and olive, ochre, brown, and blue-green palette (#bebf99, #9e8657, #584c36, #5e867b) make the surface feel continuously negotiated: resemblance is passed from one zone to the next rather than confirmed once.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="02-chivalry-in-retrograde" label="Chivalry in Retrograde, 2022" caption="Opposing diagonals and a pale central mast compress a many-part figure field against a broad red surround." />

In *Chivalry in Retrograde*, the red surround is not passive background. It gives the cool blue central tangle enough room to read as a single pressure point, while the two scored diagonals return the eye to that knot. The measured palette makes the argument unusually plain: deep blue #233f4d and two reds (#d43928 and #903225); the low mean luminance of 0.334 keeps the central mass weighty rather than merely decorative.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="03-the-troupe" label="The Troupe, 2021" caption="A low shared base and two tree interruptions pace a procession of unstable quasi-figures across the frame." />

The visual event in *The Troupe* is distribution. Repeated upright forms are separated by trunks and foliage, yet their feet share a low, wavering line. The analyzer finds the selection’s third-highest edge density here (0.209), which suits a picture whose rhythm comes from many small contour changes. The dark green, charcoal, and muted ochre palette (#5a5f46, #3d4232, #22221c, #847954) stops those interruptions from becoming a conventional landscape.

## axial promises, unstable bodies

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="04-umbra-resilientis" label="Umbra Resilientis, from Daemonica Syntheticum, 2024" caption="A helmet-like mass, a small low visual weight, and a rising garment contour make an asymmetrical close vertical image." />

*Umbra Resilientis* refuses the false calm of a centered portrait. The ellipse identifies a visibly helmet-like mass rather than a named machine or role; the garment contour rises across the frame and pulls attention to the right. Its tonal range is broad (p05 0.063 to p95 0.906), with near-black #181a1a set against pale #dee9d3, so the image’s uncertain form is carried by value before detail.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="05-nepios" label="nēpios, 2024" caption="A near axis, head mass, and uneasy eye line make a face-like presence direct without making it fully symmetrical." />

The nearly frontal head in *nēpios* asks the viewer to read a portrait, then interrupts that habit through the eye shapes and slight turn. The overlay’s vertical axis is deliberately called “near,” not absolute. An exceptionally low edge density of 0.008 and the muted gray, pink, and rose palette (#5e615e, #e0b8b0, #c1928f, #976d6d) make broad tonal transitions, rather than descriptive detail, do the work of presence.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="06-polyphonic-prose-reprise" label="Polyphonic Prose Reprise, 2022" caption="A pale mast and rightward armature brace a crowded central form against a saturated red field." />

In *Polyphonic Prose Reprise*, the mast gives a crowded central mass an apparent spine, while the diagonal carries fragments into the red field. The image has a mean luminance of 0.386 and edge density of 0.104; high-chroma red #ea3033 and blue #13305c make the packed form read as controlled density, not simply visual noise.

## enclosure, seriality, and authority

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="07-eightfold-noble-path" label="The Eightfold Noble Path Towards Enlightenment, 2022" caption="An irregular inner enclosure and central strut keep attention circulating through a dense square field." />

The frame inside *The Eightfold Noble Path Towards Enlightenment* offers a promise of order but never delivers one protagonist. A bright cue near the center and the central strut keep attention moving through the enclosure. With an edge density of 0.221, the picture is among this group’s most crowded measured surfaces; red #8c1e1c, charcoal #2f322f, and ochre #9d6d45 keep its competing objects in active tension.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="08-deathless-aphrodite" label="Deathless Aphrodite, throned in flowers, 2021" caption="A frontal axis, head mass, and level eye line give a face-like image the rhetoric of a formal portrait." />

*Deathless Aphrodite, throned in flowers* deploys frontal balance as a rhetorical device. The axis and level eye line make recognition immediate, while the overlay’s right-edge weight records a small counterpressure. The extraordinarily low edge density of 0.003 and cream-to-brown palette (#ece3d3, #987867, #6e4f41, #3c231b) make this one of the selection’s smoothest images: authority is delivered through tonal continuity.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="09-aviarium-reticulum" label="Aviarium Reticulum Generationis Adversaria, 2021" caption="A nine-panel field and two gutters turn serial variation into the image’s main compositional subject." />

*Aviarium Reticulum Generationis Adversaria* is not one specimen arranged for inspection. Its outer frame and internal gutters establish a sequence in which similar pale, avian-like forms can differ from panel to panel. The analyzer records a near-full tonal span (p05 0.027 to p95 0.996) but a low edge density of 0.026; white #fdfcfc and near-black #0f1616 make the separated panels feel both clinical and unstable.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="10-observatorivm-v3" label="Observatorivm V3, 2024" caption="A tall pale silhouette rises toward a central face-like interruption and resolves at a low converging base." />

The pale monument-like mass in *Observatorivm V3* uses height as a form of authority. The overlay follows its left contour into the low base instead of forcing the pale environmental transition into a horizon claim. At mean luminance 0.771, the lightest image in this selection, pale green and cream (#dce6d7, #d2caac) let the isolated turquoise #489593 interruptions feel almost diagnostic.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="11-cenotaphs-62" label="Cenotaphs #62, 2023" caption="A relief-like border and central axis lend authority to a figure whose looping cable breaks the apparent stillness." />

*Cenotaphs #62* borrows the visual grammar of a bordered relief without becoming an archaeological object. The central figure is framed, frontal, and gold, but the looping cable prevents the surface from settling into ceremonial calm. Its edge density is 0.233, the set’s highest, while the brown-to-gold palette (#60431d, #8c652a, #b98c3d) makes that intricate contour work register as material density.

<Plate slug="bas-uterwijk" id="12-napoleon-i" label="An artist’s impression of Napoleon I produced using a neural network, 2020" caption="A face ellipse and uniform silhouette borrow the authority of an official portrait without establishing a historical likeness." />

The Napoleon image supplies the chapter’s sharpest caution. Fondation Napoléon records it as a computer-learning image built from historical visual sources, and Uterwijk calls it an artistic impression rather than a verified reconstruction. The face and uniform silhouette are exactly the cues that make it persuasive: dark brown #4a3721 and near-black #21201b surround pale skin and uniform values, while the restrained edge density of 0.033 leaves the face legible. Plausibility here is a visible construction, not evidence.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Build belief from an axis">
Make a portrait-like image without relying on a face. Establish a vertical axis with a seam, object, or pair of matching masses, then disrupt it slightly. Compare the version that is perfectly symmetrical with the one where a small mismatch keeps the eye working.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Turn repetition into a sequence">
Photograph or assemble nine related forms in a three-by-three field. Keep the white or dark gutters visible. Change one compositional variable per panel: scale, turn, distance, or background; then decide whether the grid makes difference easier or harder to notice.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Test an official-looking portrait">
Make a frontal portrait using a plain ground, a broad shoulder shape, and one carefully placed bright facial area. Then remove or alter one cue of authority: the axis, the collar-like edge, or the even background. Describe which cue had been doing the persuasive work.
</ExerciseCard>
