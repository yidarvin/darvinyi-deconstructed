# Bas Uterwijk (Ganbrood) — source shortlist

## Selection and rights record

Bas Uterwijk, working as Ganbrood, describes his practice as algorithmic image
synthesis at the intersection of constructed imagery and photographic perception.
The artist’s public [Ganbrood gallery](https://ganbrood.com/) identifies the first
eleven titles and dates below, displays the complete works without a login, and
carries the notice **© 2026 Ganbrood**. It does not grant an open licence. The
twelfth work is a historical-portrait landmark documented by Fondation Napoléon
with the credit **© Bas Uterwijk**. The twelve direct public JPEG renditions were
downloaded without alteration; each decodes as a JPEG and has a 1,200–3,840px long
edge.

Every use below is consequently assessed as **fair-use** for this criticism,
scholarship, and transformative composition-teaching book. The factors are applied
to each work individually: (1) the use analyzes how an authored synthetic image
constructs photographic belief, rather than using it decoratively; (2) these are
creative, published contemporary works, which weighs against use; (3) the complete
frame is necessary because the teaching concerns the placement, edge relations,
scale, and voids across the whole image, while the local copy remains web-sized;
and (4) the chapter credits and links to the artist’s gallery and cannot replace an
original, NFT, exhibition, print, or licensed reproduction. Credit throughout:
Bas Uterwijk (Ganbrood), © 2026 Ganbrood.

  rhythm can keep synthetic likeness shifting rather than settle it in one portrait.
