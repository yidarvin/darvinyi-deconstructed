# Logo Oluwamuyiwa — research note

## Record

SMO Contemporary Art identifies Oluwamuyiwa Logo as a Nigerian artist born in 1990 and lists the eleven supplied photographs as works from 2019. Its biography describes documentary and conceptual work about Lagos. MoMA's *New Photography 2023* material places *Costain Overpass* (2016) in *Monochrome Lagos*; the accompanying interview records the artist's interest in symmetry, balance, and urban life. See the [SMO biography](https://smocontemporaryart.com/artists/34-oluwamuyiwa-logo/biography/), [MoMA interview](https://www.moma.org/magazine/articles/899), and [MoMA checklist](https://www.moma.org/documents/moma_master-checklist_589517.pdf).

## Technique and process

The supplied 2019 gallery JPEGs are monochrome photographs whose framing ranges from high, compressed street views to close portraits and reflections. The source record supports discussion of black-and-white tonal structure, cropping, repetition, reflection, and the stated interest in balance and urban life. It does not identify a camera body, lens, film or sensor, exposure, lighting setup, digital workflow, or original print process. The ingested files have no retained EXIF settings. Accordingly, this chapter makes no claim about camera settings or original printing beyond the available records.
