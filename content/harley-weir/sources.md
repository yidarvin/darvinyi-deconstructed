# Harley Weir — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

Harley Weir's photographs are copyrighted. The exhibition and editorial pages
used here identify the works as **© Harley Weir**; the *Boundaries* material
also identifies the originating magazines. The twelve public display JPEGs
below opened successfully after download. Their verified long edges range from
826px to 2108px, above this project's 640px normal floor, and none was
upscaled, cropped, or otherwise altered.

The selections pair eight explicitly captioned works in Foam's 2016
*Boundaries* presentation with four plates from Weir's 2019 photobook *Father*.
They make a useful teaching sequence for Weir's close-range, tactile way of
making bodies, props, and shallow spaces collide at the picture plane.

Each use below is an individual **fair-use** assessment for criticism,
scholarship, and transformative composition teaching. The complete published
frame is necessary for the stated reading; each local copy is web-sized. This
chapter credits and links to the public publisher or exhibition record and
does not replace the original work, the record, or a licensed reproduction.
The purpose is analytical rather than decorative, only the necessary frame is
used, and the limited web copy does not substitute for the rights holder's
market. These four factors are assessed for every numbered image below.
