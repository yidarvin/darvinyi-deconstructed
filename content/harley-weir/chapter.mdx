Harley Weir makes proximity feel less like access than pressure. Across this
2013–2019 selection, a body is pushed into a car window, reduced to lips, blurred
into a magenta tangle, or allowed to register as shadow and surface. The overlays
do not explain those images away; they name the small number of visible structures
that make their closeness active. Titles, dates, credits, and the per-image
fair-use rationale are in [Source notes](../../content/harley-weir/sources.md).

## bodies at the picture plane

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="01-boys-dont-cry-2016" label="Boys Don't Cry, September 2016" caption="A car window and enormous pink bag turn a figure leaving the vehicle into a compressed field of arm, face, and color." />

The car window is a usable internal frame, but it does not stabilize the scene:
the bag crosses its top edge while the arm descends out through the door. The
analyzer finds a moderate 0.015 edge density and palette of pale mauves, near-black
`#241a1d`, and red-brown `#833a48`; the pink material therefore lands as a broad
weight, not as a decorative accent.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="02-baron-magazine-2013" label="Baron Magazine, 2013" caption="A raised red wrap and one long back contour make an upright body read as a narrow, vulnerable vertical event." />

Here the long body contour has more force than the room. The wrap blocks the
expected face, and the small anchor on the draped shorts keeps the eye from
treating the lower crop as empty. Its 0.005 edge density is extremely low, while
the pale peach palette makes the few red and blue cloth decisions unusually clear.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="03-double-magazine-ss-2016" label="Double Magazine, Spring/Summer 2016" caption="Two enlarged lips and their narrow seam turn minute surface variation into the photograph's whole territory." />

The two ellipses are not abstractions imposed on the picture: they identify the
upper and lower lips that occupy almost everything. This is the set's dense close
view, with 0.092 edge density and dark browns offset by muted rose and gray; the
crop makes texture and contact carry the composition in place of a conventional
portrait.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="04-i-d-magazine-fall-2015" label="i-D Magazine, Fall 2015" caption="A raised fist and submerged body surface within a large dark water field, making the waterline a decisive horizontal break." />

The water break lies below the brightest face-and-fist cluster, so the figure
seems to emerge from rather than stand before the field. Deep blue-black values
dominate: the analyzer gives the rendition a mean luminance of 0.201 and only
0.006 edge density. That restraint makes the small warm reflection and wet skin
feel physically immediate.

## objects that refuse to stay still

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="05-pop-magazine-fw-2014" label="Pop Magazine, Fall/Winter 2014" caption="A central garment spine and dense ruffled hem turn a hidden figure into a stack of red-and-white folds." />

The vertical axis is a quiet scaffold for a much less stable thing: the white
ribbon opens into folds that keep changing direction. Nearly all of the rendition
stays in dark red (`#390e09` to `#6f130b`), with the pale ruffle as the only strong
release. Its 0.009 edge density confirms that the image works through large tonal
masses rather than fine description.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="06-unpublished-june-2016" label="Unpublished, June 2016" caption="A bent black sleeve and translucent polka-dot veil compress the reclining figure into the grass." />

The bent sleeve contour folds back over the torso, so the body arrives as a set of
overlapping fragments rather than a single reclining outline. The veil ellipse is
the more assertive interruption: its red dots and translucent folds turn covering
into another picture plane. At 0.180,
the measured edge density is among this selection's highest, fitting the grass and
crumpled fabric that compete for attention with the body.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="07-homes-november-2016" label="Homes, November 2016" caption="A reaching hand, orange-ringed shadow, and textured green screen make the photograph an exchange among partial bodies." />

The frame-in-frame is the green screen itself, not a separate room behind it. A
hand reaches in from one side while a large shadow gives the painted orange ring a
second, darker body. The 0.125 edge density and green-brown palette explain why
the tarpaulin's granular texture holds its own against the figures.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="08-dazed-confused-fall-2015" label="Dazed & Confused Magazine, Fall 2015" caption="A cropped citrus and one molded fruit use their shadows and wide blank surround as a restrained tactile stage." />

The fruit surface is small in the frame, but its roughness becomes conspicuous
because the surrounding pale field is so empty. The analyzer records a near-zero
0.00005 edge density, mean luminance 0.700, and blue-gray palette; the spacing,
not visual busyness, produces the image's tension.

## private images, unstable surfaces

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="09-father-02" label="Father, plate 02, 2019" caption="A standing profile, a hanging garment, and a tinted room turn a private interior into a staged but unsettled scene." />

The baseboard separates the cool wall from the purple floor, while the body ellipse
holds a profile whose silhouette is interrupted by hair, belly, and the dropped
trousers. Soft aqua, gray, and yellow-pink marks sit around the photographic field;
the 0.017 edge density lets that material treatment remain a persistent part of the
picture rather than a neutral border.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="10-father-04" label="Father, plate 04, 2019" caption="Overlapping body mass and two limb chains make a magenta image difficult to resolve into separate bodies." />

The two polylines offer a deliberately limited reading: upper and lower limbs
remain legible, but the central ellipse stays ambiguous. With 0.007 edge density,
the blur is not a lack of structure; it is the condition that makes pale flesh
forms fuse into a single red-violet field.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="11-father-06" label="Father, plate 06, 2019" caption="A cropped torso and two arm sweeps press dark skin, a red tassel, and electric blue ground into one close field." />

The torso ellipse is offset by the two cropped arm paths, so no complete figure is
offered. The palette's near-black `#060913`, saturated blues `#253fb5` and
`#1d3197`, and isolated red tassel make color carry the separation that contour
withholds. Its 0.006 edge density keeps the force on those broad, lit volumes.

<Plate slug="harley-weir" id="12-father-07" label="Father, plate 07, 2019" caption="Pale blooms and fine drips turn a blue field into an unstable, bodily-looking surface." />

The two bloom ellipses make the image's unequal masses explicit, while the lower
drip trace prevents the surrounding blue from becoming blank support. Mean luminance is
0.540 and edge density 0.007; muted blue-gray, mauve, and pale pink explain the
image's quiet, materially unsettled finish.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make proximity structural">
Photograph a subject so closely that one contour, seam, or contact point has to
organize the entire frame. Remove any detail that does not change the pressure of
that contour.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Let an object interrupt a body">
Use a bag, cloth, reflection, or shadow to block part of a figure. Make the
interruption descriptive enough to be a second subject, rather than a prop placed
beside one.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Hold a large field against a small rough thing">
Place one weathered object on a broad, even surface. Test how much blank space is
needed before the object's texture becomes the photograph's central event.
</ExerciseCard>
