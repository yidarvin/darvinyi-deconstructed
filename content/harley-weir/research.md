# Harley Weir — research note

## Scope of the record

This chapter's selected works date from 2013 to 2019: eight public display
renditions associated with Foam's 2016 *Boundaries* presentation and four plates
from Weir's 2019 *Father* photobook. The source record identifies Weir as the
photographer and records the editorial affiliations or book context for each
plate. It does not establish a single shared commission, camera system, or
production workflow for the selection.

## Technique and presentation

The accessible records support only a reading of the finished photographs and
their editorial/exhibition or photobook presentation. They do not document film
or sensor format, camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO,
lighting setup, post-production, paper, ink, print process, edition, or print
dimensions. The ingested public JPEGs contain no retained EXIF camera metadata.
Accordingly, the chapter treats close framing, color, blur, surface, and cropping
as visible picture decisions; it does not infer technical settings or a printing
method from those appearances.
