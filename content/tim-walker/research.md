# Tim Walker — research note

## Scope and period

This selection spans 2002–2018: fashion-editorial commissions alongside the
2015 Bosch-related *Garden of Earthly Delights* project. Walker's official
biography describes a practice of imaginative fashion pictures and elaborate,
narrative shoots; it also identifies the Bosch commission and the V&A's
*Wonderful Things* commission. The bibliography and image-by-image credits are
in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Documented method

The available record supports discussion of constructed sets, props, costume,
collaborative production, posing, and controlled lighting. In a Getty account of
his collaborative process, Walker describes months of preparation with a set
designer, stylist, hair and makeup team, followed by repositioning and lighting
adjustments during the shoot: [Getty, “The Beauty of Collaboration”](https://www.getty.edu/news/the-beauty-of-collaboration/).
Those facts support reading the photographs as built tableaux; they do not prove
that any individual image was made without post-production.

## Printing and capture limits

There is no documented uniform printing process for these twelve editorial
images. The official biography describes the separate 2015 *Garden of Earthly
Delights* series as printed on gesso-coated canvases; that fact is not extended
to the rest of the set. The supplied image files carry no capture EXIF for eleven
works. The remaining file has `Microtek SM 9800XL 9800` metadata, which identifies
a scanning device rather than a camera. No consulted record establishes a camera
body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting diagram, or film
format for any plate. The chapter consequently treats visible arrangement as
evidence and leaves exposure and optical claims unstated.
