# Stephanie Dinkins — research note

## What the supplied sources establish

The source packet identifies *Conversations with Bina48* (2014–present) as a
long-term videotaped exchange between Stephanie Dinkins and the social robot
Bina48. It identifies *Conversations with Bina48: Fragments 7, 6, 5, 2* as a
four-channel video installation in the related Whitney record, while the
artist’s page supplies the selected International Center of Photography view
and a separate display image. The packet identifies the ICP view as *Fragments
7, 6, 5, 2* (2018); for *Fragments 17, 19 & 20*, it establishes only the title,
2020 date, and “image only” caption, not a venue. [Source notes](sources.md)

The same packet identifies *Not the Only One* (N’TOO, 2018–present) as a
conversational AI project built from the oral histories of three generations of
Dinkins’s family. The artist’s project page describes the displayed avatar as a
composite of the women whose stories inform N’TOO. The selected “Becoming” view
is identified as a 3D avatar sketch, and the selected process image is a
project diagram. These records support discussion of the works’ displayed
interfaces, portraits, and process language; they do not establish a uniform
technical workflow for the works.

## Technique, printing, and settings

The sources document moving-image installation, conversational AI, a 3D avatar
sketch, and a process diagram, but do not establish camera body, lens, focal
length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO/sensitivity, capture format, lighting
plan, renderer, training configuration, model architecture, print process,
paper, edition, or post-production workflow. The six normalized display JPEGs
retain no camera EXIF. This chapter therefore describes only documented project
contexts and visible, rendition-level evidence—framing, screen repetition,
silhouette, tonal range, palette, and text hierarchy—without inferring their
production settings from web copies.
