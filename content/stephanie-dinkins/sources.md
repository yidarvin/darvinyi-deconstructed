# Stephanie Dinkins — source shortlist

This source set follows two connected bodies of work: *Conversations with Bina48* (2014–present), Dinkins’s long-term videotaped exchange with a social robot, and *Not the Only One* (N’TOO, 2018–present), a conversational AI built from three generations of her family’s oral histories. The artist’s public project pages identify the works, their exhibition contexts, and the displayed documentation; the Whitney collection record independently identifies *Conversations with Bina48: Fragments 7, 6, 5, 2* as a four-channel video installation and gives the notice “© Stephanie Dinkins Studio.”

All six selected files are publicly served, complete display views from Dinkins’s own site, and each was opened after download. The shortest long edge is 683px, above the 640px floor. No open licence or public-domain dedication is supplied; every selection is therefore documented as a limited **fair-use** copy. Credit/copyright notice: **Stephanie Dinkins / © Stephanie Dinkins Studio** (the artist site supplies no separate licence; the Whitney record supplies the explicit studio notice).
