# Nadia Lee Cohen — research notes

## Scope and attribution

The twelve supplied images are presented by Dazed as *HELLO My Name Is* (2021), credited “Photography Nadia Lee Cohen.” Dazed’s accompanying project text describes a wider sequence of 33 self-portraits made from names collected on badges; each persona is paired here with a still life of associated possessions. The exact titles, gallery routes, credits, source files, and image-specific fair-use assessments are retained in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Method and era

This is a contemporary, deliberately staged portrait-and-still-life project. Its recurring devices in this selection are a full figure or a tightly organized set of possessions, a plain warm studio ground, costume, wigs, name badges, and commercial-looking props. The source record supports the project date, Cohen’s authorship, the badge-name premise, and the pairing of persona with still life. It does not establish the date, location, or production circumstances of each individual frame beyond the 2021 project context.

## Camera, lighting, and printing

Neither Dazed’s public gallery nor the ingested JPEGs supplies a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, film stock, lighting diagram, print process, edition, or paper. The ingested files contain no usable EXIF. Consequently this chapter describes only visible effects in the supplied web renditions—plain warm ground, directional-looking modeling, controlled palette, and staged props—not undocumented equipment or print facts. Analyzer measurements are properties of these web JPEGs, not claims about an original print.

## Reading discipline

The work’s fiction is visual and intentional, but it should not be mistaken for biographical evidence about real people. The captions identify named project personas and visible objects; the prose treats costume, pose, layout, and tonal organization as constructed signals. The full frames are reproduced at web size solely for documented criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching under the fair-use rationale in the source notes.
