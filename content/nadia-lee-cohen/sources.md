# Nadia Lee Cohen — source shortlist

This set follows Cohen’s *HELLO My Name Is* (2021): six staged self-portraits and the six accompanying still lifes from the same named-persona sequence. Dazed’s public twelve-image exhibition gallery identifies the titles/captions and credits every frame “Photography Nadia Lee Cohen.” Its public CDN served the complete published JPEG frames used here, each opened successfully at 1159–1500px wide and 1500–2000px tall. The alternation is intentional: the portraits make disguise, costume, frontal pose, and the plain ground available for analysis, while the still lifes show how Cohen lets an arrangement of possessions construct a second, off-screen biography.

**Rights basis:** **fair-use** for every selection. The works are copyrighted contemporary photographs by Nadia Lee Cohen; Dazed’s gallery credits “Photography Nadia Lee Cohen,” and no open licence or public-domain statement is supplied. Each assessment is limited and transformative: this nonprofit book uses a credited, public web-sized complete frame for criticism, scholarship, and composition teaching. The complete frame is necessary to assess the relation of body or object arrangement to the neutral studio ground; a crop would remove that visual argument. Each entry links to Dazed’s public gallery page and direct display rendition. The web-sized copy does not replace the artist’s work, the exhibition record, book, print, or licensed reproduction.

## Research context

Dazed presents the complete twelve-frame selection as *Nadia Lee Cohen, HELLO My Name Is* (2021) and credits the images to Cohen. The project’s 33 self-portraits were made from names found on collected badges; the still lifes are associated with each imagined persona. See Dazed’s interview and project context: https://www.dazeddigital.com/art-photography/article/55481/1/nadia-lee-cohen-hello-my-name-is-strangers-characters and the public gallery: https://www.dazeddigital.com/art-photography/gallery/30705/0/nadia-lee-cohen-hello-my-name-is-2021.
