# Wolfgang Tillmans — source shortlist

This 12-image working set follows Tillmans from the early, intimate portrait through astronomy, still life, darkroom abstraction, installation-derived paper forms, and the later information image. It deliberately samples the equal-status visual field described by [MoMA's 2022 survey](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5440), rather than treating portraiture as the sole center of the practice. All files opened successfully. The ten Walker files are public, web-sized collection-display responses; they are used here under individually documented fair use for criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition teaching. No access control, login, paywall, or image protection was bypassed.

## Shortlist

### 01 — *Lutz & Alex sitting in the trees* (1992)
- **Why it matters:** The paired, tree-bound bodies turn a seemingly casual portrait into a full-frame structure of diagonals, foliage, and reciprocal gaze; this is the essential early image of Tillmans's intimate social field.
- **Source:** [MoMA collection record](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/164451) (title/date, collection verification, and copyright notice); public display rendition acquired from [WikiArt](https://www.wikiart.org/en/wolfgang-tillmans/lutz-alex-sitting-in-the-trees-1).
- **Rights / fair-use assessment:** Wolfgang Tillmans; MoMA states “Copyright © 2026 Wolfgang Tillmans.” **Factor 1:** this is transformative criticism of the picture's dual portrait, tree lattice, and social framing. **Factor 2:** a highly creative contemporary photograph. **Factor 3:** the complete 1179 × 1600px frame is necessary to analyze both sitters and the enclosing branches. **Factor 4:** the web-sized copy is credited and links to MoMA and the display source; it does not replace a print, museum record, or licensed reproduction.
- **File:** `raw/wolfgang-tillmans/01-lutz-alex-sitting-in-the-trees.jpg` (1179 × 1600px)
