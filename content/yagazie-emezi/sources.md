# Yagazie Emezi — source record

## Selection and acquisition note

This twelve-image source set centers Yagazie Emezi's *#EndSARS Protests*
(2020), the body represented in MoMA's *New Photography 2023* and explicitly
listed as **Untitled, from “#EndSARS Protests,” 2020** in the exhibition
coverage. Emezi's [official archive project page](https://www.yagazieemezi.com/endsars-protests)
publicly displays the photographs, and the archive's public image service
provided the direct, unaltered JPG renditions below. MoMA identifies Emezi as a
living artist and directs reproduction requests to its image-licensing partners;
the artist archive supplies no open reuse licence. The set is therefore
documented as **fair-use**, not open access.

The creator and presumed rightsholder is **Yagazie Emezi**. The relevant
exhibition caption records `© Yagazie Emezi`; it identifies an *Untitled* work
from this 2020 series as “Courtesy of the artist and Richard Taittinger Gallery,
NY.” This chapter's assessment is made for each image below: **factor 1:** a
criticism, scholarship, and transformative composition-teaching use;
**factor 2:** a creative contemporary photograph, which weighs against use;
**factor 3:** the complete displayed frame is necessary to analyze the spatial
relations among people, signs, police, streets, and crowd density; **factor 4:**
the copy is a credited, linked, web-sized reference and does not replace the
original, exhibition record, or a licensed reproduction. The files were
downloaded directly from the public rendition used by the artist's archive,
opened successfully, and measure 1,599 × 1,066px; none was upscaled or altered.
