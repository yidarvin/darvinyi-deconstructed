Across these four frames from *An American Index of the Hidden and Unfamiliar*, Taryn Simon makes access a compositional condition. A bounded forest, a near-centered publication, a contained apparatus, and an irregular blue field each ask the eye to inspect rather than merely identify. The source records establish the titles, dates, and project membership; the overlays below make claims only about visible structure in the supplied display images. ## dense and frontal evidence

<Plate slug="taryn-simon" id="01-hoh-rain-forest" label="The Hoh Rain Forest, 2007" caption="Mossed trunks and a low mossed branch turn the frame into a layered field rather than a view through the trees." />

In *The Hoh Rain Forest*, the overlay holds on a mossed trunk cluster and traces a low mossed branch; together they counter the impulse to turn the thicket into a simple scenic recession. The supplied JPEG is dark (mean luminance 0.221) but edge-rich (0.289): near-black #121611, green-black #2c3721, and mossy #758d5a make local crossings and fern texture do the work of depth.

<Plate slug="taryn-simon" id="02-playboy-braille-edition" label="Playboy, Braille Edition, Playboy Enterprises, Inc., New York, 2007" caption="The cover rectangle and its central emblem hold against a marbled surround in which printed and tactile marks share one plane." />

The warm cover is near-centered in a broad black marbled surround. Its rectangle, emblem, and a competing marble seam keep the composition nearly frontal without pretending that the surrounding surface disappears. Mean luminance 0.172 and edge density 0.137 place #131213 around copper-like #945335, #bc7b4e, and #ddac7e; contrast isolates the publication while its dots and printed sign remain part of the same visible surface.

## enclosure, repetition, and absence

<Plate slug="taryn-simon" id="03-cryopreservation-unit" label="Cryopreservation Unit, Cryonics Institute, Clinton Township, Michigan, 2008" caption="A faceted vessel end, sparse stand, and one corrugated hose organize a pale enclosure." />

The cryopreservation image concentrates on one pale, faceted vessel end rather than an imagined array. The ellipse marks that round form; the hose and short stand rail give its fog-softened enclosure a small asymmetry. This is the lightest supplied frame (mean luminance 0.647) and has the lowest edge density (0.004). Its #c2c2c2, #a9a9aa, #858585, and #e2e3e3 palette lets value changes, not crisp outline, hold the apparatus in place.

<Plate slug="taryn-simon" id="04-nuclear-waste-encapsulation" label="Nuclear Waste Encapsulation and Storage Facility, Cherenkov Radiation, Hanford Site, U.S. Department of Energy, Southeastern Washington State, 2007" caption="Repeated dark circles puncture an irregular blue field around a central void." />

The Hanford frame turns repeated dark circular forms into a luminous cyan-on-black field around a central absence. Its overlay follows the visible upper edge and irregular blue boundary rather than naming indistinct infrastructure. Mean luminance is 0.068 and edge density 0.117; #000001, #0b637e, #053e58, and #257f95 keep the serial pattern vivid while the void interrupts its rhythm.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make a dense field legible">
Photograph a crowded natural or built surface. Mark one repeated structure and one crossing contour; revise until the frame reads as a set of relationships, not a single scenic passage.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Hold an object against its surround">
Place a small rectangular object on a patterned surface. Keep its edges nearly frontal, then let one seam, crack, or reflection contest the rectangle without hiding it.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Use repetition to describe absence">
Arrange repeated dark forms around a larger empty region. Test whether the empty region becomes active only when the surrounding pattern is consistent enough to be interrupted.
</ExerciseCard>
