Noémie Goudal photographs a landscape as a proposition that has not finished becoming a photograph. In *Phoenix*, palms appear across suspended strips, vertical slats, clips, and black intervals. In *Tropiques*, a garden and its watercourses offer recession, yet the edge, clamps, and discontinuities keep the pictured space close to the surface. The overlays do not treat these features as flaws to be ignored on the way to the scene. They show how the scene is made persuasive precisely by leaving its construction visible. ## Phoenix: a picture broken into parts

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="01-phoenix-ii" label="Phoenix II, 2021" caption="A central palm is held inside a hanging field whose repeated horizontal cuts make the image read as assembled." />

*Phoenix II* makes a single palm feel both monumental and precarious. The overlay contains the suspended picture field, then names the central palm mass and the interrupted strips that repeatedly cut through it. The result is not a landscape receding behind an invisible window; it is a tall object with its own internal seams. The analyzer's high edge density of 0.2563 helps explain why so many small interruptions remain active. Near-black #100b0a, gray #646061, and pale #c3c3c2 make the strips alternately disappear and flash forward.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="02-phoenix-iii" label="Phoenix III, 2021" caption="A stacked palm canopy is repeatedly crossed by cuts inside a bounded suspended screen." />

In *Phoenix III*, the broad crown appears to spread through a deep grove, but the horizontal bands keep returning it to a surface. The full screen boundary and canopy ellipse make that double reading explicit: the canopy supplies the recognizable whole, while the breaks refuse continuity. Its mean luminance is only 0.266 and its edge density 0.2641. The palette, from #0b0706 through #545346 to muted #a6aa9a, lets the lighted leaves hold their shape against a mostly black ground.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="03-phoenix-iv" label="Phoenix IV, 2021" caption="A central palm trunk and crown remain legible across a clipped field of horizontal panel joins." />

*Phoenix IV* places a central palm inside a clipped hanging field. The strip joins do not simply obscure it; their regular interruptions meter how the eye reconstructs its trunk and crown. This is the darkest *Phoenix* selection by mean luminance, 0.230, while its edge density reaches 0.2829. The range from #0a0807 to #a3a99c makes the foliage and panel seams equally responsible for the image's tense, granular surface.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="04-phoenix-v" label="Phoenix V, 2021" caption="A narrow central panel is pinned inside a larger hanging boundary and interrupted by crosswise strip breaks." />

Here a narrow central panel turns the frame into an arrangement of pieces rather than one view. The wider hanging boundary and crosswise breaks create a grid of decisions: the viewer must keep asking which fragment belongs to which tree. The mean of 0.271 and palette from brown-black #150f0a to pale blue-gray #b5bcba produce an image whose illumination feels selective rather than atmospheric. The 0.2529 edge density keeps every join insistently present.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="05-phoenix-vi" label="Phoenix VI, 2021" caption="A centralized palm crown is held inside a suspended plane cut into horizontal layers." />

*Phoenix VI* makes the central palm crown the chapter's clearest test of reconstruction. The crown can be seen as one figure only because the viewer bridges the segmented horizontal layers. The surrounding black intervals make that work conscious. With a mean of 0.328 and p95 of 0.827, pale #c5cabe and gray #939386 produce sharp flashes against #110e08; its 0.2358 edge density keeps the image lively without restoring seamless depth.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="06-phoenix-vii" label="Phoenix VII, 2021" caption="Three vertical panels partition one hanging palm, making continuity and physical separation visible at once." />

*Phoenix VII* changes the rhythm from strips to three large vertical panels. The overlay keeps each panel distinct and anchors the central hanging palm, so the picture can be read as both a single grove and a partitioned installation. The dark value structure is compressed, with mean 0.269 and p95 0.694, yet the edge density reaches 0.2658. Gray-green #5b5c4f and pale #afb2b4 emerge from #100c08 chiefly as isolated palm fragments, not as continuous foliage.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="07-phoenix-viii" label="Phoenix VIII, 2021" caption="A central dark interval and repeated strip interruptions deny the palm grove a seamless natural space." />

The final *Phoenix* plate makes absence structural. A central dark interval separates bright palm fragments, while strip interruptions cross the whole tableau. The screen is therefore not a neutral support for a scene; its gaps are one of the scene's subjects. Mean luminance is 0.270 and edge density 0.2418. The restrained sequence #0d0b06, #5d5e5a, and #b2b4ad keeps the image cool and nocturnal even where the leaves become bright.

## Tropiques: depth held inside a screen

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="08-tropiques-i" label="Tropiques I, 2020" caption="A foreground leaf screen and a watercourse sit inside a visibly clipped garden panel." />

In *Tropiques I*, oversized leaves press forward while a narrow watercourse offers a route toward the middle distance. The overlay distinguishes those two actions, then retains the clipped landscape boundary that makes them a staged encounter. With a mean of 0.315, p05 of 0.078, and edge density 0.2243, the image balances dark #1e1e1c against layered greens #3b4433 and #5e6e50, with #cbcfc2 appearing as a rare light on the leaves.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="09-tropiques-ii" label="Tropiques II, 2020" caption="A central rock opening and waterfall axis offer depth, while foliage and the hung-scene boundary keep it theatrical." />

*Tropiques II* gives the eye a centered rocky opening and waterfall axis, but it withholds a clean entrance. Leaves and the picture boundary sit in front of the apparent distance, turning the opening into an invitation that is also a flat cutout. Its edge density is the set's lowest at 0.1986, which leaves the larger dark-green masses readable. #1c1c1d, #636b5c, and #c7cfb7 maintain a cool tonal ladder from concealed edge to bright foliage.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="10-tropiques-iii" label="Tropiques III, 2020" caption="Fern masses frame a dark central clearing and watercourse inside a suspended garden field." />

In *Tropiques III*, fern masses close around a dark central clearing. The water path is a modest vertical promise, not a decisive perspective line; the screen boundary remains the firmer structure. Mean luminance is 0.324 and p95 0.773, with the stronger edge density of 0.2479 supplied by fern texture, trunks, and staged joins. Olive #687050 and #94a27d carry the foliage against #191718 rather than opening it into an unlimited forest.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="11-tropiques-iv" label="Tropiques IV, 2020" caption="An enclosed fern field and central water run are held inside the edges of a clipped garden screen." />

*Tropiques IV* is brighter and more open in value, with mean 0.385 and p95 0.839, but its basic claim remains containment. The fern enclosure circles the water run, and the frame keeps the lushness from reading as unbounded growth. The palette moves from #20211f through #676c61 and #929790 to #ced4cd; at edge density 0.2153, the repeated fronds make a textured field rather than a view that can be traversed without resistance.

<Plate slug="noemie-goudal" id="12-tropiques-v" label="Tropiques V, 2020" caption="A central stone and waterfall path promise recession while the suspended screen retains its staged surface." />

*Tropiques V* closes on a central stone mass and waterfall path. The water seems to continue behind the foreground leaves, but the hung-screen boundary keeps that continuity provisional. Mean luminance is 0.325 and edge density 0.1917; #211f19, #414733, and #8e9589 organize the deepest values and foliage before the pale #c6cccd highlights touch the water. The photograph succeeds by letting depth happen without allowing depth to win.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Let a seam stay visible">
Photograph a scene through, across, or beside a constructed surface: a fence, reflection, printed panel, window, screen, or a sheet of paper. Keep both the scene and one material edge visible. Make a second frame that hides the edge, then compare which image makes you more aware of how the view is made.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Make continuity an active task">
Photograph a single object through repeated gaps, slats, strips, or shadows. Place enough information in each gap that the viewer can reconstruct the whole, but do not make the interruptions decorative. Identify whether the eye follows the object, the rhythm of the breaks, or switches between them.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Contain a landscape">
Frame a small outdoor or planted scene so foreground foliage creates an enclosure around a path, stream, or opening. Include a visible boundary, such as a window, display case, sheet, wall, or hanging edge, that stops the setting from becoming generic scenery. Decide whether the boundary stabilizes or unsettles the promised depth.
</ExerciseCard>
