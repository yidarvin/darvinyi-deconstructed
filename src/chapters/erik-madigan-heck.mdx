Erik Madigan Heck makes fashion photography behave less like a record of a look
than a negotiation among figure, garment, and field. In this 2007–2018 selection,
a hat can replace a face, a dress can distribute a pattern across the frame, and a
colored backdrop can carry as much narrative weight as the sitter. The overlays
name only the visible structures behind those effects; titles, dates, and the
fair-use rationale are in [Source notes](../../content/erik-madigan-heck/sources.md).

## costume as a field

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="01-without-a-face-lanvin" label="Without A Face (Lanvin), Old Future, 2013" caption="A centered butterfly mask makes the head a graphic event, then scatters its colors over the white garment." />

The mask ellipse and central axis make the blue butterfly the portrait's first
event; the shoulder arc separates it from the smaller printed forms below. A high
mean luminance of 0.826, near-white `#fefefd`, blue `#0756d5`, and deep `#08181e`
make every colored interruption decisive.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="02-valentino" label="Valentino, Old Future, 2014" caption="A broad, swinging butterfly-patterned cape meets a dense horizontal band of roses." />

The cape ellipse holds the model against the rose-bed band, turning the garment
into a dark moving mass. At 0.219, this set's highest measured edge density comes
from the abundance of flowers; `#221d1b` and blue-gray `#36385a` anchor it.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="03-blue-hat" label="The Blue Hat, Old Future, 2007" caption="The low brim of a blue hat interrupts a seated figure and turns the portrait into a sequence of soft volumes." />

The hat ellipse and brim edge name the picture's first mass. From there, the
skirt-fold sweep carries attention down through the seated body, not toward a
face. Its low 0.023 edge density and `#242435`, `#dfd8d0`, and `#4e9ce1` palette
make it read through large tonal planes rather than fine detail.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="04-red-socks" label="The Red Socks, Old Future, 2014" caption="A bent white figure bridges a dark blue field and white floor, with the red socks anchoring the lower edge." />

The white floor seam is a real division, not an invented horizon. Above it, the
folded jacket is an almost continuous white volume; below it, the red socks
provide the chromatic anchor. A 0.043–0.961 luminance span and `#e7ecf2`,
`#012147`, and `#a30104` keep this low-edge-density (0.005) picture crisp.

## face withheld, silhouette enlarged

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="05-kirsten-owen" label="Kirsten Owen, MUSE, 2014" caption="The sitter's cropped head and a wrapped black-and-white body turn broad stripes into the photograph's main action." />

Two stripe sweeps make the wrapped body feel larger than the crop. The phi grid
is only a quiet reference; the enclosing ellipse is the stronger claim. Red
`#8b0009`, black `#0b0809`, pale `#f1f1ea`, and 0.018 edge density let broad bands
rather than texture govern the rhythm.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="06-florals" label="Florals, Harper's Bazaar UK, 2015" caption="A floral-clad figure rises from a matching carpet of petals, linking hat, dress, and floor as one patterned system." />

The floral figure extends the ground upward. A raised arm breaks the silhouette,
and the petal ellipse returns the same pattern to the floor. Mean luminance 0.876,
near-white `#fefcfd`, and reds from `#ec0a1d` to `#f25482` give figure and field
one bright, limited vocabulary.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="07-junya-watanabe" label="Junya Watanabe, Without A Face, 2018" caption="Two black hat lobes obscure the head while oversized circles make the garment into a graphic panel." />

The paired hat ellipses deny the expected facial anchor. Beneath them, the
shoulder wrap opens into the garment's circles, so the sitter is read first as a
designed surface. Edge density is only 0.008; pale `#f1f5f9` and near-black
`#14161c` keep the visual decision stark.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="08-giorgio-armani" label="Giorgio Armani, Without A Face, 2018" caption="A nearly centered navy hat swells into an abstract silhouette above a small pink skirt field." />

The centered balance axis and hat ellipse explain why the body recedes: the hat
occupies nearly the entire upper frame. The smaller pink-skirt ellipse is a quiet
counterweight. At 0.007 edge density, pale `#f5f9fc`, navy `#192746`, and
pink-lilac `#d1b3d3` are enough to hold the image together.

## decorative theater

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="09-without-a-face-dior" label="Without A Face (Dior), 2018" caption="A blue petal headdress outweighs an exposed profile, while the red spotted garment completes the color triad." />

The headdress ellipse gives the blue construction visual priority, and the short
profile line waits at its edge to release the face. A mean luminance of 0.090 and
black `#010103` make blue `#253a6f`, red `#8d100d`, and skin-toned `#dbd1c5`
arrive as accents, not atmosphere.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="10-queen-of-hearts" label="Queen of Hearts, Harper's Bazaar UK, 2015" caption="A slender central figure becomes the pin around which red and black decorative forms unfold." />

The figure ellipse and head anchor keep the body readable, while the red-flourish
ellipse shows how the setting spreads like a sign rather than a room. High-key
`#fbfbfb`, `#8f0b1b`, `#060304`, and a 0.012 edge density make the forms feel cut
from clean color fields.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="11-without-a-face-valentino" label="Without A Face (Valentino), Old Future, 2013" caption="A butterfly garment field and raised hand divide attention among body, patterned fabric, and a single perched butterfly." />

The garment ellipse refuses to reduce the body to one outline. The shoulder line
and right-shoulder butterfly distribute attention across pattern and lifted
gesture. Its 0.089 edge density is far higher than the studio-like portraits,
which suits the dense butterfly field against blue `#2b6d9a`.

<Plate slug="erik-madigan-heck" id="12-without-a-face-yellow" label="Without A Face (Yellow), Old Future, 2013" caption="A centered back figure becomes a black-dotted yellow field, with the pointed collar interrupting the long silhouette." />

The final plate is the most economical version of this method. A central axis
holds the back-facing figure; the hair ellipse and collar line make the only large
breaks in the repeating dress. Yellow `#e4be2d`, black `#0d0a07`, and a moderate
0.041 edge density let the dots accumulate as pattern before they resolve as cloth.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Let a garment take the first look">
Photograph a figure with one oversized hat, collar, or wrap. Make the face less
immediate than the garment, then identify the contour that still keeps the body
legible.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Repeat a pattern across roles">
Choose a pattern that can appear in clothing and in the ground. Arrange it so the
viewer has to decide where the figure ends and the field begins.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Build a three-color stage">
Use a pale field, one dark mass, and one saturated accent. Keep texture sparse so
the relationship among the large colors, rather than a detail, organizes the
picture.
</ExerciseCard>
