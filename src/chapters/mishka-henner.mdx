Mishka Henner's *Eighteen Pumpjacks* makes extraction legible at a scale where the machine is almost too small to be a subject. The supplied NGA records identify these 2012 works as inkjet prints; the source notes describe a satellite-image typology. Rows, tracks, scrub, and cleared earth first read as abstract surface, then a black pumpjack turns the surface into evidence of a system. The overlays mark visible routes, field boundaries, cleared pads, and machines rather than inventing a conventional aerial horizon. [Source notes](../../content/mishka-henner/sources.md)

## small machines, large fields

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="01-api-21930378-slaughter-tx" label="API 21930378 Slaughter, TX, 2012" caption="An access trace runs through stacked cultivated fields to a pumpjack at their compressed meeting point." />

The long vertical access trace gives *API 21930378 Slaughter, TX* a spine before it meets the pumpjack and pale pad. The tilted field boundary at y0.522 makes the land read as broad registers. Mean luminance is 0.431 and edge density 0.130; #5b6251, #7f7662, and #bb8f5f make the machine's route the decisive interruption.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="02-api-21931252-san-andres-tx" label="API 21931252 San Andres, TX, 2012" caption="A road register carries the eye into a small pumpjack amid a dispersed field of bright marks." />

Here a curving access road enters a pale cleared pad in a blue-gray field of scattered light points. Mean luminance 0.509 and edge density 0.081 keep #7f8081, #959492, and #434445 sparse enough for the cleared site to read as a local surface change, not a monument.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="03-api-21902614-levelland-tx" label="API 21902614 Levelland, TX, 2012" caption="A centered pumpjack breaks the repetition of two tightly ruled agricultural fields." />

The centered pumpjack breaks red-brown crop bands while a lower road edge closes the tall frame. Its edge density of 0.234 makes #7e4c38, #9e6248, and #402c26 a structured surface against which the compact dark machine becomes exact.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="04-api-21902673-levelland-tx" label="API 21902673 Levelland, TX, 2012" caption="A horizontal access track passes through an almost uniform orange field and meets a small central pumpjack." />

This plate needs little beyond a track at y0.524 and its slight continuation into the machine. Mean luminance 0.595, edge density 0.002, and the orange range from #b47240 to #e5a270 make the site a minute disturbance in a continuous plane.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="05-api-50133229-clear-fork-tx" label="API 50133229 Clear Fork, TX, 2012" caption="Vertical crop-row rhythm and a crossing track hold a central pumpjack within a repetitive field." />

Vertical crop rows pull attention into the central pad while a crossing track checks their rhythm laterally. With edge density only 0.010, #996949, #b47a52, and #dcc8b0 make the contrast chiefly material: pale cleared ground against fine marks.

## routes, blocks, and interruptions

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="06-api-07932684-levelland-tx" label="API 07932684 Levelland, TX, 2012" caption="A long access trace and a turning spur convert a small pumpjack into a junction in open scrub." />

The road arrives from the lower edge and bends at the pumpjack, making the site a junction rather than a dot. Mean luminance 0.587, edge density 0.037, and #b98d70, #c99c7d, #a57c60, and #503826 leave the road geometry to organize scrub.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="07-api-21901820-slaughter-tx" label="API 21901820 Slaughter, TX, 2012" caption="A stepped field division descends through crop blocks to a small pumpjack, with a low field edge closing the frame." />

An oblique, stepped division carries attention down through crop blocks more forcefully than the machine itself. At edge density 0.220 and p05–p95 of 0.169–0.906, #a87d5e, #7e6755, and #574c40 make field seams carry real depth.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="08-api-02914653-mountain-view-ca" label="API 02914653 Mountain View, CA, 2012" caption="An upper field boundary and access road turn an extraction site into a node within an orthogonal agricultural system." />

An upper field boundary, horizontal road, and perpendicular crop rows make this the set's most diagrammatic plate. It has the chapter's highest edge density, 0.251; #263724, #4c6846, #7d8059, and #c0b58e make the aerial field cultivated and coded at once.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="09-api-21931656-levelland-tx" label="API 21931656 Levelland, TX, 2012" caption="A narrow vertical access road terminates at a cleared pad and pumpjack inside a dark vegetation field." />

The vertical road enters from the top and widens at the cleared site around the pumpjack. This darkest plate has mean luminance 0.336 and edge density 0.246; #313d3f, #454e4f, #575d5d, and #b49677 make the pad a scored opening.

<Plate slug="mishka-henner" id="10-api-21932470-slaughter-tx" label="API 21932470 Slaughter, TX, 2012" caption="An access trace arrives at a cleared pad, making an oil site read as a small interruption in scrub." />

The final plate concentrates the double reading: an access trace reaches a cleared pad while the pumpjack sits beside its marked center. Mean luminance 0.400 and edge density 0.208 keep #555566, #686777, #3b3a47, and #caa38f separate without spectacle.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make a small subject govern a large field">
Photograph a patterned surface so one small object is the only local interruption. Add a route that reaches it; if the object remains readable because of that route, keep the frame.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Turn a boundary into structure">
Frame a clear change of surface: crop rows, paving, shadow, vegetation, or water. Compare the version held by that boundary with one where it has been cropped out.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Read infrastructure before narrative">
Choose a road, path, rail, or conduit. Describe only direction, connections, and surface changes before naming what it serves; let that delayed recognition carry the picture.
</ExerciseCard>
