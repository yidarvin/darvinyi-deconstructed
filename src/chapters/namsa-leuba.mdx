Namsa Leuba's pictures insist that portraiture is made, not merely found. A body is staged with fabric, fur, beadwork, masks, staffs, documents, and a particular piece of ground; the setting is then reduced to a field of colour, a wall, a dark shelter, or distant mountains. Across *Khoisan*, *Weke*, *Zulu Kids*, and *The Kingdom of Mountains*, that construction makes each costume and prop carry compositional weight. The overlays name visible contours, enclosures, and directional paths rather than translating every detector signal into a claim. [Source notes](../../content/namsa-leuba/sources.md)

## costume as a picture-making instrument

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="01-chunara" label="Chunara, from Khoisan, 2014" caption="A central masked figure divides the blue field while fur presses in as a warm, tactile counterweight." />

In *Chunara*, the face is withheld by a white crocheted mask, but the figure is made emphatically present through its upright axis and expansive sleeves. The overlay encloses the costumed body, follows its mask-to-body descent, and gives the fur at lower left its own counterweight instead of treating it as incidental texture. The analyzer finds blue #067db3 and pale blue-gray #7695a5 alongside brown #765f49 and near-black #1c100e; mean luminance is 0.404, with p05 0.031, p95 0.784, and edge density 0.0482. The quiet blue field lets the material differences do the work.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="02-maria" label="Maria, from Khoisan, 2014" caption="A frontal figure is separated from a low, colourful textile cluster by a generous field of sand and sky." />

*Maria* is frontal without becoming static. The central body reads as a narrow vertical held between sky and ground, while the cluster of fabric and objects at left creates a competing, lower rhythm. The overlay therefore keeps one ellipse on the posed figure, one on the textile cluster, and a simple axis through the pose. Its palette moves from blue #6a9ecb and #adbfd7 to sand #b39d82 and dark #1f1c1c; mean luminance 0.556, p05 0.110, p95 0.733, and edge density 0.0600 describe a scene whose detail remains concentrated in costume and props.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="03-kokos" label="Kokos, from Khoisan, 2014" caption="A profile, beaded shoulder, and hip turn an upright portrait into a chain of alternating contours." />

The profile in *Kokos* offers a different kind of centrality. Head, shoulder, hanging beadwork, and hip do not settle on one vertical; they make a series of sideward turns against the striped wall. The overlay follows the profile-to-hip contour and the beaded shoulder's diagonal rhythm, while the phi grid aligns with the analyzer's preferred fit. This is the most tonally expansive *Khoisan* plate here: p05 is 0.035, p95 0.980, and the pale #e4eaf0 field sets off near-black #1b1619, blue-gray #99adbf, and rust #944f36 at a restrained 0.0393 edge density.

## compression, enclosure, and colour

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="04-black-jc" label="Black JC, from Weke, 2017" caption="Two contrasting figures meet across a cropped diagonal, making the empty pale gap an active interval." />

*Black JC* compresses two bodies into a close exchange. The dark profile at left bends downward and the pale, inverted-looking counter-figure rises at right; the narrow pale space between them is not background but the hinge of the picture. The paired ellipses and the crossing path make that relation explicit. Mean luminance is 0.602, p05 0.122, p95 0.933, and edge density 0.0569, while #dadde4 and #babbc5 meet violet-black #391e33. The delivered web file retains Nikon D800 metadata of 62 mm, f/3.2, 1/125 s, ISO 5000; it supplies no evidence about the later print process.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="05-azaca" label="Azaca, from Weke, 2017" caption="A curtain-like enclosure repeats around a still central body and converts vertical decoration into a frame." />

In *Azaca*, repeated hanging strands behave as both decoration and architecture. The rectangular enclosure holds the figure almost as a shrine would, while the upper garland makes a shallow counter-arc above the head. That frame is a visual fact, not a detector invention: it concentrates the body inside an image whose mean luminance is only 0.188 and p95 only 0.592. Dense local detail produces a high edge density, 0.1303; black #101416, gray #595a5c, and red #ac4f56 keep the figure luminous in a dark field. The retained file metadata records Nikon D800, 70 mm, f/10, 1/125 s, ISO 200, without documenting print or lighting.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="06-transe-a" label="Transe A, from Weke, 2017" caption="Falling fringe cuts a vertical veil through a tight, high-chroma field of painted forms and costume." />

*Transe A* abandons the calm enclosure of *Azaca* for a near-field collision. The ellipse contains the masked head; one path follows the falling fringe, while the other catches the broad curved colour band that presses into it. The result is an image that is busy by measurement as well as by sight: edge density reaches 0.1976, with p05 0.051 and p95 0.820. Deep #251523 and #812028 interlock with cyan #56c0cb and pale #cca895. The delivered metadata records Nikon D800, 105 mm, f/11, 1/125 s, ISO 200; those individual values should not be generalized to the series.

## bodies on a public stage

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="07-power" label="Power, from Zulu Kids, 2014" caption="A raised arm punctures the broad sky above the low line of settlement and an isolated stump." />

The authority of *Power* comes from scale. The body is small enough to be surrounded by sky, but the raised arm and the stump make that smallness deliberate rather than vulnerable. The overlay keeps the measured settlement line at y 0.451, encloses the elevated figure, and follows the lifted arm; it does not make a speculative convergence claim from the buildings. Blue #6085ba and pale blue #abb8d6 dominate, while brown #7e6444 and dark #2b2217 locate the body in the field. Mean luminance is 0.519 and edge density 0.1344, much of it concentrated in grass, clothing, and the distant structures.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="08-passports" label="Passports, from Zulu Kids, 2014" caption="A body balanced on a stump, held papers, and an open window set up a three-part address across the yard." />

In *Passports*, the figure is not isolated; the building answers it. The blue-framed open window, dark doorway, held papers, and bright green face at the stump's base make the yard a stage full of smaller visual events. The overlay marks the actual window and follows the left arm to its held paper, while its large ellipse keeps the precarious vertical whole. The palette moves from sand #bca082 and blue #7d9ac6 to black #131214 and light #e1cbbb. A low 0.0323 edge density and p05-to-p95 range of 0.024 to 0.843 give the few sharp accents unusual force.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="09-16-june" label="16 June, from Zulu Kids, 2014" caption="A stilt-borne figure, upright staff, and long cast shadow make the entire open lot part of the action." />

*16 June* makes the figure's height legible by giving it an equally consequential shadow. The cast-shadow path extends diagonally down and right across the pavement, while the upright staff and vertical body rise against the wall and sky. The ellipse intentionally contains both the perched figure and its wooden support: the construction, not just the face, is the subject of the composition. Mean luminance is 0.571, p05 0.263, p95 0.812, and edge density 0.0536; blue #7298d4 and concrete #c4c1c4 make the fur and brown ground read as assertive interruptions.

## portrait as a field of materials

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="10-umfana" label="Umfana, from The Kingdom of Mountains, 2014" caption="A dark face, blue wrap, and pale mountain air build a concentrated vertical portrait from opposing tonal fields." />

*Umfana* turns the wrap into a second frame around the body. The blue edge bends down the left side, the dark face holds near the top, and the pale mountains leave enough air for the costume's seams and tags to stay legible. The analyzer prefers the phi grid and measures a very wide tonal spread, p05 0.016 to p95 0.894, yet only 0.0235 edge density. That combination explains the image's force: deep #0b0a1b and blue #1b578c do not need a busy surround to feel dense.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="11-qkhwini" label="Qkhwini, from The Kingdom of Mountains, 2014" caption="A red robe becomes a clear vertical signal against a near-black field of rock." />

In *Qkhwini*, red is not merely descriptive colour; it declares the figure's place against the dark rock. The headdress-to-robe axis turns the pose into an upright interruption of the landscape, while the ellipse keeps the robe's total shape intact. The image is low-key, with mean luminance 0.199 and p95 0.384, but red #c21624 and #7a0d0e arrive with exceptional authority against #131b16 and #30373b. Its 0.0742 edge density belongs largely to the headdress, hanging strands, robe edges, and rough rock surface.

<Plate slug="namsa-leuba" id="12-sangoma" label="Sangoma, from The Kingdom of Mountains, 2014" caption="A close costumed figure pushes forward while the mountain horizon stays as a shallow, receding counterfield." />

*Sangoma* ends the selection by bringing landscape and costume into the same shallow plane. The face-to-textile axis drops through fur, beads, reflective material, and patterned cloth, while the broad figure ellipse makes the body press outward against a lightly described mountain backdrop. The analyzer's phi fit is useful as a field structure, but the overlay's claims remain visible in the figure's materials and the short exposed section of receding ridge at left. Mean luminance is 0.608, p05 0.082, p95 0.878, and edge density 0.0459; pale #cad3e7 and #adb0ba allow brown #6f3e35 and #2a1513 to keep the foreground warm and insistent.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Build a costume as a silhouette">
Make a portrait in which one garment, mask, or material changes the body's outer contour. Before adding more props, check whether the silhouette still reads clearly against the field.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Give the setting an answering role">
Place a figure beside a wall, window, shadow, ridge, or horizon that redirects attention back to the body. Keep only the frames in which that setting changes the pose's meaning or direction.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Let one accent govern a dark field">
Photograph a strongly coloured garment or object against a quieter setting. Expose and frame so the colour establishes a clear hierarchy without flattening the material detail around it.
</ExerciseCard>
