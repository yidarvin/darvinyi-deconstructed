Amalia Ulman's *Excellences & Perfections* asks a social-media feed to perform as a photographic sequence. The supplied Rhizome replay records twelve posts from a work made between 19 April and 14 September 2014; the New Museum describes it as a five-month scripted performance. That context matters, but the images make the script believable through ordinary, legible picture languages: a mirror selfie, a close crop of clothing and accessories, a locker-room snapshot, a medical-looking bathroom, a sentimental emblem. Each square is small enough to appear disposable and exact enough to redirect the reading of the one before it. [Source notes](../../content/amalia-ulman/sources.md)

## a feed begins by declaring its frame

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="01-part-i-title" label="Excellences & Perfections: Part I, 19 April 2014" caption="A lone type baseline stretches across a nearly empty square, making the serial opening itself the image." />

The *Part I* card has almost no pictorial information: mean luminance is 0.967, p05 is 0.992, and edge density is only 0.0072. Its palette is nearly all #fefefe punctuated by #040405 type. The overlay therefore names the title field and its baseline rather than promoting the detector's tonal split to a horizon. The opening works because the unoccupied white field makes a feed's serial order visible before it supplies a lifestyle scene.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="02-good-morning-selfie" label="Good Morning!! 🌸🌸, 23 April 2014" caption="A tall mirror opening encloses the body while crossed legs and a foreground robe keep the apparently casual picture staged." />

“Good Morning!! 🌸🌸” turns the mirror into a contained stage. The inner rectangle holds the standing body, while the traced crossed-leg pose refuses the neutral straight-on balance a mirror might imply. A robe pushed into the lower left becomes a second, nearer layer. Mean luminance is 0.372, p05–p95 spans 0.098–0.804, and the palette moves from #221819 and #523e35 to #e2dfd6. That broad dark-to-light range lets the white outfit and lit legs look spontaneous against a deep domestic recess, even as the mirror gives the pose an exact boundary.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="03-ready-to-go-out" label="Ready to go ouuttt.!!, 4 May 2014" caption="The necklace and handbag chain make a cropped dress, hand, and bag read as a vertical run of consumer signs." />

The next crop withholds the face. Its necklace falls from the upper left, the bag chain drops at right, and the anchor sits on the handbag rather than on a person. The white dress fills much of a bright field, with mean luminance 0.732 and p95 0.969, plus small dark #342022 and #937060 accents from bag and accessories. At a low 0.0109 edge density, the image does not need much description: the crop makes the clothes, jewelry, phone, and handbag function as compact social signals.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="04-secret-spa" label="New fav spa…, 4 May 2014" caption="A floor break and repeating lockers make the centered pink robe a deliberate interruption in an institutional grid." />

In “New fav spa…,” repeating locker doors carry the scene farther than the picture's casual caption does. The overlay's vertical axis follows the locker rhythm, the measured floor break sits at y0.843, and the towel-wrapped head is the local anchor. Edge density rises to 0.0404, the set's first strongly articulated background; #3d241e, #664332, and #886b5a give the room a brown grid against which #e4cbd0 reads as an artificial pink interruption. The room makes the performance appear publicly situated while its repeated units make the pose look carefully inserted.

## medical proof as a photographic style

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="05-doctor-consultation" label="Doctor was soooo kind!!, 21 May 2014" caption="Face and phone appear beside a framed wall record and a medical armature, producing a clipped clinical-looking selfie." />

The clinical turn begins through crop and equipment, not explanatory text alone. Face and phone sit in the left third; a framed wall record and articulated armature create an environment that looks procedural. The derivative is unusually pale, with mean luminance 0.763, p05 0.467, p95 0.965, and edge density only 0.0057. Whites and beiges (#f2eee8, #dad3ca, #c8beb1) suppress visual drama. The small darker #453c3a details and the apparatus therefore acquire the force of evidence.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="06-part-ii-title" label="Part II / Single, Taken, Busy Getting Money, 22 June 2014" caption="The checkbox column, oversized checkmark, and word MONEY make a narrative turn through internet-style typography." />

*Part II* is another transition card, but it replaces the opening's quiet typography with a checklist and an enormous diagonal checkmark. The overlay follows the actual left column and checkmark and anchors the word “MONEY,” rather than inventing photographic depth. Its tonal span is broad (p05 0.039 to p95 0.957), its edge density 0.0373, and #190e0f black type presses against a cream #f5eed0 field. The frame makes a ready-made graphic idiom function as a serial plot device.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="07-procedure-announcement" label="nervous and excited!!, 10 July 2014" caption="A bathroom mirror field and a measured tile course lend the gown-and-phone image the look of firsthand documentation." />

The bathroom image narrows the available space. The mirror frame clips the body, a tile course crosses at y0.344 on a slight -4.4° angle, and the broad sleeve is the near anchor. The restrained palette of #64504f, #8e7a6f, #a89d8e, #bebbad, and #dde2d3 keeps the room's different materials close in value. With a 0.612 mean and 0.0149 edge density, it has the quiet, flattened legibility of a casual phone record. That is precisely how the ordinary tiles, dispenser, and robe become plausible visual proof.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="08-post-op-bandages" label="Im safe n happy!!, 11 July 2014" caption="A tilted bed edge and the cropped torso keep the bandaged chest from resolving into an ordinary portrait." />

“Im safe n happy!!” pushes the evidence style into a tighter crop. The overlay uses the detected line at y0.595 and -7° as the bed edge, then follows the torso contour toward the bandaged chest. The diagonal makes the bed feel unstable rather than like a calm horizontal base. Its palette shifts from #8b4a35 and #ba7a55 through #edd783 to #f1fddf; p05–p95 runs 0.314–0.973. The color and 0.0062 edge density leave large soft regions, so the bandage's white mass and the abrupt bodily crop carry the narrative pressure.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="09-morning-boob" label="Total #morningboob, 13 July 2014" caption="A garment axis and the bra's lower edge turn a frontal crop into an almost bilateral arrangement of fastening and texture." />

The next post is even more diagrammatic. The garment axis at x0.525 runs through the central fastening; the lower edge at y0.511 makes the bra's two sides visibly answer one another. The analyzer finds strong vertical correlation at 0.88, a useful measure of the garment's bilateral design rather than a claim that the cropped body is perfectly mirrored. Its warm palette (#633b37 to #eedfc1) and very low 0.0043 edge density make knitted texture, straps, and clasp carry the entire frame. The intimate crop is not simply close; it is organized to read quickly as a specific kind of update.

## apology, surrogate, ending

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="10-apology" label="Dear everyone… (apology post), 14 August 2014" caption="A granular heart is centered on a post border, reducing an apology to a single symmetrical emblem." />

The apology post withdraws from body and room into a heart-shaped field. The overlay names its vertical axis, broad heart ellipse, and visible post border. This is the brightest non-title image by mean luminance (0.794), but its narrow 0.608–0.957 p05–p95 range and muted #b5b1a1, #c9c8b6, and #f1f2f0 palette soften the contrast. At 0.0096 edge density, the granular surface cannot compete with the large emblem. The feed's emotional cue becomes an easily recognized, almost generic picture.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="11-part-iii-selfie" label="Part III / So pretty :), 15 August 2014" caption="A floral-crowned cat substitutes for a selfie: its near-central portrait axis and muzzle break organize the cute surrogate." />

The final phase opens on a cat rather than the performer. A portrait axis at x0.512 and the muzzle break at y0.556 organize the face, while a left flower provides an off-center color anchor. The image is pale overall, with mean luminance 0.722 and p95 0.961, yet its 0.0201 edge density is high enough for flower heads, eyes, whiskers, and ears to remain active. #653440 and #936b5f flowers interrupt cream #edece1 fur and background. The substitute portrait makes cuteness another role the feed can stage rather than a transparent change of subject.

<Plate slug="amalia-ulman" id="12-the-end-rose" label="THE END: EXCELLENCES & PERFECTIONS, 14 September 2014" caption="A central bloom, an upper petal, and surrounding dark masses make the rose a dense tonal full stop." />

The closing rose returns the square to an emblem but reverses the apology image's pale vacancy. The central bloom is held on a vertical axis, with an upper petal and surrounding darker flowers refusing a clean isolated silhouette. Mean luminance falls to 0.439, p05–p95 expands from 0.141 to 0.855, and edge density peaks at 0.0490. The grayscale palette, from #2f2e32 through #888387 to #d9cfce, makes petal folds and shadowed gaps into an all-over field. It gives a deliberately familiar image the weight of an ending without needing a final portrait.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make the feed a sequence">
Photograph three squares that share a small set of colors or props but change their picture language: a title card, a mirror frame, and a close crop. Put them in an order where the first makes the second more legible. The test is whether each image works alone while also making a role or mood accumulate across the set.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Use an ordinary setting as evidence">
Make a self-portrait in a bathroom, changing room, or other specific interior. Include one architectural repetition, such as tiles, lockers, or a mirror edge, and one object that changes how the scene reads. Keep the crop restrained enough that the location carries the plausibility rather than a dramatic pose.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="End with an emblem">
After photographing a short sequence of bodies or rooms, make one final square without a person. Use a flower, written phrase, object, or simple symbol, and decide whether it should be centered, cropped, bright, or dark. Compare it with the preceding images: does the emblem close the series or merely interrupt it?
</ExerciseCard>
