Thomas Struth makes attention spatial. A street, a family room, a museum gallery,
a forest, or a laboratory is never merely a setting: it is a system that determines
where the eye may travel and what scale can mean. The overlays name only the
visible routes and boundaries that carry that pressure; the measured tonal values
describe the display renditions, while the source record limits claims about
process and apparatus. [Source notes](../../content/thomas-struth/sources.md)

## a frontal world with a distance inside it

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="01-crosby-street-new-york-soho" label="Crosby Street, New York, Soho, 1978" caption="A street axis and paired curb routes turn the nearly empty intersection into a tightly regulated corridor." />

The centered street axis in *Crosby Street* makes the distance unusually active:
the curb lines tighten into the far street while the stopped car remains a blunt,
near interruption. Its high edge density (0.219) and p05–p95 range of 0.027–0.976
keep brick, fire escape, signage, and broken pavement equally available to the
eye. The palette's black #111111 through mid-gray #696969 permits the pale sky to
read as an opening rather than as expressive weather. MoMA records the related
work as a gelatin-silver print, printed in 1989; the supplied evidence does not
establish the camera settings of the 1978 exposure.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="02-the-hirose-family-hiroshima" label="The Hirose Family, Hiroshima, 1987" caption="A wall portrait, doorway, and foreground sitter make the domestic group read as both a face-to-face gathering and a layered room." />

*The Hirose Family* does not isolate one decisive sitter. The small anchor on the
front-row child is countered by the picture behind the group and the open doorway,
so the family is distributed through objects, generations, and recessions. Its
black-and-white rendition ranges from p05 0.012 to p95 0.957, with a 0.138 edge
density that lets furniture stay descriptive without overtaking the faces. No
supplied source establishes a camera or lighting setup for this domestic scene.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="03-louvre-4-paris" label="Louvre 4, Paris, 1989" caption="The framed painting and a low band of viewers make two scenes, and two kinds of looking, occupy the same wall." />

The internal frame of *Louvre 4* is the essential compositional fact: the history
painting gathers one drama while the visitors' backs form a second, quieter one.
The central viewer joins the two without owning either. The analyzer finds a low
mean luminance of 0.265 and a restrained p95 of 0.608; brown-black #1d1816,
#46312b, and muted ochre #a39879 keep the gallery's illumination concentrated on
the painting and parquet. The source record supports the title and the visible
spectator arrangement, not a claim about the visitors' intentions.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="04-pantheon-rome" label="Pantheon, Rom, 1990" caption="A compact visitor group establishes human scale within the Pantheon's broad, concentric enclosure." />

At the Pantheon, the small visitor group makes human scale legible against the
rotunda's broad, concentric enclosure. Dark #13110d and #363128 move into
stone-toned #5d574a, while p05 0.031 and p95 0.749 preserve the gradual rise from
shadowed dome to lit floor. The source documentation identifies the monument and
date but supplies no exposure or printing specification for this particular
rendition.

## fields that refuse a single subject

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="05-paradise-02-daintree" label="Paradise 02, Daintree, Australien, 1998" caption="Crossing limbs provide local routes through a foliage field that withholds a privileged focal point." />

In *Paradise 02*, the two marked crossing limbs are invitations to wander rather
than arrows to a destination. The lower foliage anchor is only a temporary pause
inside an unusually busy field: edge density reaches 0.268, and the near-black
#141817, green-gray #393e2f, and leaf #616c55 register continually shifting local
contrast. The official archive describes the 1998 Daintree pictures as the first
set of the *New Pictures from Paradise* and as a dense, less hierarchically
selected mode of looking; it does not verify a work-specific print process here.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="06-semi-submersible-rig" label="Semi Submersible Rig, DSME Shipyard, Geoje Island, 2007" caption="A near-bilateral rig is crossed by one visible mooring line that connects the foreground dock to the hull." />

The rig's centered bulk could have made a static industrial portrait. Instead, one
visible mooring line ties the foreground worksite to the hull, and the slightly
angled waterline prevents perfect bilateral calm. The
palette sets burgundy-black #1a1418 against blue-gray #9ea9b4 and beige #8d8678;
p05 0.020 to p95 0.816 separates the shadowed hull from the bright air. The source
record provides no technical settings for the photograph.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="07-tokamak-asdex-upgrade-interior-2" label="Tokamak Asdex Upgrade Interior 2, Max Planck IPP, Garching, 2009" caption="A toroidal chamber, one curved seam, and a compact hardware anchor turn the machine into an enclosing field." />

The large ellipse in the tokamak image makes its curved enclosure legible against
the central hardware. One chamber seam then pulls the eye across panels, cables,
and reflections. Mean luminance is 0.260, with p05 0.012 and p95 0.749: black
#0a0a0a, olive-metal #2e2c24, and bronze #655d48 let small highlights puncture a
largely dark mechanical space. The source supports the location and title, not an
exposure recipe.

## apparatus as an environment

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="08-brillenbaer-leibniz-izw" label="Brillenbär (Tremarctos ornatus), Leibniz IZW, Berlin, 2018" caption="The bear’s broad body arc, face, and forward paw make the clinical ground feel like an observation field rather than a natural habitat." />

The bear's silhouette crosses nearly the whole frame, but face and forward paw give
that dark mass a direction across the pale ground. With edge density only 0.043,
gray #aeb6b6 and near-black #19191c separate body from backdrop more by value than
by surrounding detail. The display file's embedded camera metadata is
derivative-file data and is not cited as historical capture evidence.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="09-schlichter-weg" label="Schlichter Weg, Feldberger Seenlandschaft, 2021" caption="Two fallen limbs set a lateral, low-contrast route across the winter thicket." />

The winter scene works by resisting the long view promised by its wide frame. Two
fallen limbs pull attention sideways through dense branches instead of along a
central vista. The high mean luminance (0.539) and p95 0.976 give snow-white
#efeff0 room to carry the air, while brown #504b3b and #2a2416 keep the thicket
dense. The public source identifies the work, but does not support a claim about
camera or print settings.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="10-x-ray-telescope-cast" label="X-ray Telescope, CAST, CERN, Ferney-Voltaire, 2021" caption="A circular vacuum vessel, one cable route, and an instrument arm create a vertically stacked technical field." />

The vacuum vessel makes a stable round counterweight at left; cable and instrument
arm distribute attention through a dense, tall assembly rather than toward one
heroic device. Its mean luminance is 0.271, edge density 0.178, and palette moves
from #151516 through #383635 to #61605c, making wires and polished metal register
as repeated tonal intervals. Neither the artist-site record nor the web rendition
substantiates a capture format or camera setting.

<Plate slug="thomas-struth" id="11-container-7-cern" label="Container 7, CERN, Meyrin, 2023" caption="The hard container boundary holds a centered tangle of cable and scrap without resolving its many competing lines." />

*Container 7* returns Struth's interest in architectural bounds to a tight,
vertical enclosure. Its frame-in-frame is literal, while one cable route crosses
a tangled core and a bright yellow housing creates a local stop. Edge density is
0.230; black #131110, gray #676561, and brown-gray #3e3933 make the colored scrap
flash locally against the container walls. The analysis describes only the shown
display JPEG, and the source record offers no work-specific technical settings.
