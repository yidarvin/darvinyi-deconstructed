Petra Collins’s *Selfie* and *Neon Works* make picture-making feel intimate without pretending it is unmediated. *Selfie* repeatedly places a phone between people, or sets it inside a mirror that already turns a room into an image. The neon works concentrate that condition into text: language is a sign, a colored light source, and a reason for a dark room to be framed. The overlays name screens, hands, mirrors, and visible sign panels in the supplied complete web frames; dates, credits, and the image-by-image fair-use rationale are preserved in the [source notes](../../content/petra-collins/sources.md).

## an image is a place where looking happens

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="01-selfie-pdf-2" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="A hand carries a small lit phone through a large brown field, turning the screen into a picture nested within the room." />

In 01 the phone sits high at the right and becomes the decisive small light inside a predominantly brown image. The arm gives the screen a physical route from the bottom edge. Mean luminance is only 0.252 and edge density 0.009; dark `#2e242a` and rust `#733e2f` allow the pale screen to feel almost like a second exposure.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="02-selfie-pdf-8" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="Faces, hands, and a small phone close into an inward-facing social knot rather than a conventional group portrait." />

02 makes collecting and showing images a collective gesture. The close-group oval makes the small phone a shared point of attention without forcing a false route through the hands. The frame remains dark (mean luminance 0.264) but its p05–p95 span of 0.059–0.576 and 0.037 edge density let several bodies remain active without separating them into individual portraits.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="03-selfie-10" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="A black phone rectangle interrupts a yellow-green wall, making the screen a compact second image." />

03 reverses 01’s strategy: the high-key green room makes the device into the dark interruption. The phone frame and hand entering from below keep attention low and central. Mean luminance is 0.646, p05–p95 is 0.243–0.898, and edge density only 0.008. With `#e7e39a`, `#b5a013`, and `#717a13` spread across broad fields, the picture reads through color and containment before detail.

## mirrors make the viewer part of the apparatus

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="04-selfie-9" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="A face, hair contour, raised phone, and cropped sleeve share a pale, shallow bathroom space." />

04 denies an all-over descriptive portrait. The face presses to the left; the hair contour and raised phone leave much of the pale tiled room nearly empty. Its p05–p95 range is 0.098–0.894, yet edge density is 0.007. Pale `#d8e2ca`, blue-black `#102437`, and muted green make brightness and crop do the work that sharp detail normally would.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="05-selfie-pdf-3" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="The mirror centers doubled arms and phone, making the self-portrait a layered exchange of looking." />

The mirror in 05 is not merely backdrop. Its visible edges hold both hands and the small phone-image in a repeated arrangement; the tilted tile-counter break checks the otherwise upright framing. `#8f3413`, `#e6d0af`, `#d5a96f`, and `#c26221` span p05–p95 values of 0.161–0.863, while 0.028 edge density preserves the room’s geometry without making it hard.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="06-selfie-41" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="Warm tiled surfaces and a mirror put hands, reflected phone, and room into a shallow nesting of rectangles." />

06 lets amber tile and reflected surfaces flatten the room into a set. The mirror contains a second device while the hands make a short route toward it. The p05–p95 range of 0.149–0.890 gives the image real light range; `#974004`, `#cf7f08`, and pale `#f0deac` make that range chromatic rather than neutral.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="07-selfie-pdf-9" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="A raised phone-hand arc leads into a softly cropped reclining figure, withholding a complete narrative." />

In 07 the low angle treats body and bed as a large, soft enclosure. The phone-hand arc enters before the face, so holding an image becomes the first readable event. High mean luminance (0.614) and low edge density (0.005) keep `#caa282`, `#dcbe9e`, and `#a56950` almost continuous: softness here is an organization of attention, not a lack of form.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="08-selfie-pdf-13" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="Two devices and an arm create a visible relay across a dim room, turning intimacy into a mediated exchange." />

08 distributes the scene between a seated figure, a central phone, and an arm from the right. The overlay traces the arm’s route to the screen without treating the gathered hands as architectural convergence. Dark olive `#987e45`, `#604a23`, and `#363824`, with mean luminance 0.334, make the lit rectangles carry the image’s social voltage.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="09-selfie-pdf-14" label="Untitled, *Selfie* series, ongoing since 2013" caption="A central mirror turns phone, room, and reflected body into a three-way exchange across a dark green interior." />

09 is the clearest statement of the series’ recursion. A vertical mirror center holds the phone-screen while room and body appear around it and within it. Mean luminance is 0.236, p05–p95 0.071–0.498, and edge density 0.029; near-black `#1c1717` and green `#4c5840` make the illuminated screen a hinge rather than a simple focal point.

## neon turns language into a frame

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="10-neon-works-p10102271" label="Untitled, *Neon Works*, 2014–15" caption="A rounded neon speech bubble concentrates writing in a deep black surround, with its tail pulling to the lower right." />

In 10 the sign is its own internal frame. Its measured center and glow-to-shadow break make the lettering feel held in a dark chamber; the little tail at right prevents the rounded shape from becoming a neutral logo. Mean luminance is 0.227 and p05–p95 stretches from 0.016 to 0.702, so near-black `#1c0507` and rose-red `#762432` turn white text into a localized event.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="11-neon-works-img-0817" label="Untitled, *Neon Works*, 2014–15" caption="Centered magenta lettering is held inside a rectangular sign panel." />

11 uses a near-symmetrical sign panel. The sign center and its visible border make the lettering read as a held field without implying that the room is neutral. It is the darkest plate here (mean luminance 0.140), with purple `#300e38`, near-black `#190519`, and pink `#ae1c69`; lettering illuminates the surrounding wall by chromatic pressure rather than descriptive light.

<Plate slug="petra-collins" id="12-neon-works-img-0818" label="Untitled, *Neon Works*, 2014–15" caption="A long lyric block makes the dark wall a shallow stage, its right end kept active by the taper of glowing text." />

12 expands that event into a long text field. The sign panel and center axis enclose the lyric block, while its right end keeps the frame asymmetrical from within. Edge density rises to 0.059, the highest of the neon group, because the lettering adds internal contour. The spare `#1a0a0f`, `#481f23`, `#962a3b`, and `#ca4a65` palette lets saturated writing carry the room’s temperature.

## Exercises

<ExerciseCard n="01" title="Make a device organize the room">
Photograph a phone, tablet, mirror, or other display without treating it as a product shot. Give it a visible route through a hand, reflection, cable, or gaze, then compare a version where that route is cropped away.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="02" title="Use a reflection as a second frame">
Work in a mirror or window. Place a figure so the reflected image changes the balance of the whole frame, not just the information inside the glass. Check whether both the physical boundary and the reflected subject are necessary.
</ExerciseCard>

<ExerciseCard n="03" title="Let text provide the light">
Photograph existing lit text, signage, or a screen in a dim space. Expose so the surrounding darkness remains an active field. Ask whether the lettering creates a structural boundary, a directional pull, or a separate picture inside the picture.
</ExerciseCard>
